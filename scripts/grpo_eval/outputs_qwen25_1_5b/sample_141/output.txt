To determine the number of worker bees in the hive, we are given the following relationships:

- The total number of bees is **700**.
- There are **twice as many worker bees as baby bees**.
- There are **twice as many baby bees as queens**.

---

### Step 1: Define Variables

Let:
- $ Q $ = number of **queens**
- $ B $ = number of **baby bees**
- $ W $ = number of **worker bees**

From the problem:
- $ B = 2Q $ (twice as many babies as queens)
- $ W = 2B = 2(2Q) = 4Q $ (twice as many workers as babies)

---

### Step 2: Total Bees Equation

The total number of bees is the sum of all three groups:

$$
W + B + Q = 700
$$

Substitute the expressions in terms of $ Q $:

$$
4Q + 2Q + Q = 700
$$

$$
7Q = 700
$$

$$
Q = \frac{700}{7} = 100
$$

---

### Step 3: Calculate Worker Bees

Now that we know $ Q = 100 $, we can find the number of worker bees:

$$
W = 4Q = 4 \times 100 = 400
$$
