Johnny picks up three sets of Legos:  
- The first set has **500** pieces.  
- The second set has **3 times more** than the first, which is $ 3 \times 500 = 1500 $ pieces.  
- The third set has **$ \frac{1}{4} $** the number of the first set, which is $ \frac{1}{4} \times 500 = 125 $ pieces.  

Total pieces:  
$$
500 + 1500 + 125 = 2125
$$
