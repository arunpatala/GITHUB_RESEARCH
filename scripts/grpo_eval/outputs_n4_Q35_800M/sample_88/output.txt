Let $H$ be the number of copies Harald sold.
Let $M$ be the number of copies Marilyn sold.

We are given the following information:
1.  They sold 88,000 copies combined:
    $$H + M = 88,000$$
2.  Marilyn's record sold as many times as Harald's:
    $$M = 10H$$

Substitute equation (2) into equation (1):
$$H + 10H = 88,000$$
$$11H = 88,000$$

Solve for $H$:
$$H = \frac{88,000}{11}$$
$$H = 8,000$$

Harald sold 8,000 copies.
