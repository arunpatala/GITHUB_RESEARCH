There are 27 total unicorns, and $9$ are in the Scottish Highlands.
$$ \text{Number of unicorns in other regions} = 27 - 9 = 18 $$

**Step 3: Calculate the number of females in the Scottish Highlands.**
Two-thirds of the unicorns in the Scottish Highlands are female.
$$ \text{Number of female unicorns in Scottish Highlands} = \frac{2}{3} \times 9 = 6 $$

**Step 4: Calculate the total number of female unicorns.**
We add the number of female unicorns found in the Scottish Highlands to the number of all unicorns in other regions (since every unicorn has a gender).
$$ \text{Total female unicorns} = 6 + 18 = 24 $$
