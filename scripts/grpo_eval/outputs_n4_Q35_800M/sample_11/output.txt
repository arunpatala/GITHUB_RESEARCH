**1. Calculate the cost of donuts:**
*   Type: Donuts
*   Quantity: 3 dozen
*   Price per dozen: $68
*   Calculation: $3 \times 3 \times 68 = 9 \times 68$
    *   $9 \times 60 = 540$
    *   $9 \times 8 = 72$
    *   Total: $540 + 72 = \$612$

**2. Calculate the cost of mini cupcakes:**
*   Type: Mini cupcakes
*   Quantity: 2 dozen
*   Price per dozen: $80
*   Calculation: $2 \times 2 \times 80 = 4 \times 80$
    *   $4 \times 80 = \$320$

**3. Calculate the cost of mini cheesecakes:**
*   Type: Mini cheesecakes
*   Quantity: 6 dozen
*   Price per dozen: $55
*   Calculation: $6 \times 6 \times 55 = 36 \times 55$
    *   First, $36 \times 50 = 1800$
    *   Then, $36 \times 5 = 180$
    *   $1800 + 180 = \$1980$

**4. Calculate the total cost:**
*   Total = Cost of donuts + Cost of mini cupcakes + Cost of mini cheesecakes
*   Total = $612 + 320 + 1980$
*   Summing the numbers (rightmost to left):
    *   $612 + 320 = 932$
    *   $932 + 1980 = 2912$

The total cost is $2912.
