We need to buy 18 flowers.

**Step 4: Calculate the cost for 18 flowers using the more expensive option.**

$$ \text{Savings} = \text{Total expensive cost} - \text{Total cheaper cost} $$
$$ \text{Savings} = \$15.00 - \$9.00 = \$6.00 $$
