The general formula for the $n$-th term $a_n$ of an arithmetic sequence is:
$$a_n = a_n' + (n-1)r$$
Where:
- $a_n$ is the value we are looking for (in this case, the total sum of the sequence).
- $a_n'$ is the first term ($a = 5$).
- $n$ is the number of terms (Kylar wants to buy **16** glasses).
- $r$ is the common ratio ($r = 1/2$).

Since we need to find the sum of the prices of all 16 glasses, we set $n = 16$.

**4. Perform the calculation:**

$$Total Cost = 5 + (16-1) \times \frac{1}{2}$$
$$Total Cost = 5 + 15 \times \frac{1}{2}$$
$$Total Cost = 5 + \frac{15}{2}$$
$$Total Cost = 5 + 7.5$$
$$Total Cost = 12.5$$
