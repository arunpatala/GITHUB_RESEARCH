Step 1: Identify Jenna's share based on the piecemeal assumption.
If her mom picked 20 apples, "her mom got 20 apples" means $20 = \text{Mom's share}$.
The problem states: "Jenna picked half as many apples as her mom."
Therefore, Jenna's share is $\frac{1}{2} \times \text{Mom's share}$.
$20 / 2 = 10$. So, Jenna picked 10 apples.

Step 2: Calculate the total number of apples.
Jenna picked 10 apples.
Her mom picked 20 apples.
Total picks = Jenna's share + Mom's share
Total picks = $10 + 20 = 30$.

The total number of apples picked by both was 30.
