To find how much each friend paid, we need to calculate the breakdown of the total cost and distribute it equally between the three friends.

**1. Calculate the total cost of tickets:**
The total amount spent on the tickets is $20.25.

**2. Calculate the cost of food:**
Food cost was $4.50 less than the tickets.
$$ \text{Food Cost} = \text{Ticket Cost} - 4.50 $$
$$ 20.25 - 4.50 = 15.75 $$

**3. Calculate the total cost for the rides:**
There were 2 rides, and each cost $33.
$$ \text{Ride Cost} = 33 \times 2 = 66 $$

**4. Calculate the total cost for all activities:**
$$ \text{Total Cost} = \text{Ticket Cost} + \text{Food Cost} + \text{Ride Cost} $$
$$ \text{Total Cost} = 20.25 + 15.75 + 66 $$

Let's add the tickets and food first:
$$ 20.25 + 15.75 = 36.00 $$

Now add the ride cost:
$$ 36.00 + 66 = 102.00 $$

**5. Calculate the cost per friend:**
Since the friends agreed to split the costs evenly, we divide the total cost by 3.
$$ \text{Cost per friend} = \frac{102.00}{3} $$
$$ 102 \div 3 = 34 $$

So, each friend paid $34.
