To find out how many pounds of insects a flock of ten ducks needs per day, we need to follow these steps:

1.  **Calculate the total weekly needs:**
    The flock has 10 ducks and needs 3.5 pounds per duck.
    $$10 \text{ ducks} \times 3.5 \text{ pounds/duck} = 35 \text{ pounds/week}$$

2.  **Calculate the daily per duck needs:**
    Subtract the weekly overall need from the weekly per duck need.
    $$35 \div 7 = 5 \text{ pounds/duck per day}$$

3.  **Calculate the total per day for the entire flock:**
    Multiply the daily per duck need by the number of ducks (10).
    $$10 \text{ ducks} \times 5 \text{ pounds/duck} = 50 \text{ pounds/week (total)}$$

4.  **Convert the weekly total to daily:**
    Since there are 7 days in a week, divide the weekly total by 7.
    $$50 \div 7 = \frac{50}{7} \approx 7.14$$

Since it is a rational number, it is often represented as a fraction or the decimal approximation rounded to two decimal places.

$$50 \div 7 = 7 \frac{1}{7}$$

In decimal form, this is approximately 7.14.

*Note: Often in such "math problem" context unless specified otherwise, 7.14 is accepted.*
