**Step 1: Calculate the total number of potted plants on each ledge.**
She has $2$ plants on each of the $40$ ledges.
$$2 \text{ plants/ledge} \times 40 \text{ ledges} = 80 \text{ plants total on ledges}$$

**Step 2: Calculate the number of plants she will give away.**
She will give $1$ plant from each of the $40$ ledges.
$$1 \text{ plant/ledge} \times 40 \text{ ledges} = 40 \text{ plants total to give away}$$

**Step 3: Calculate the remaining plants.**
Subtract the number of plants she will give away from the total number on the ledges.
$$80 \text{ original plants} - 40 \text{ plants to give away} = 40 \text{ plants remaining}$$
