To calculate how much Mandy should give to Benedict, we need to find the **Present Value (PV)** of her remaining balance after 3 months.

**1. Identify the Given Variables:**
*   Principal Amount (Initial) ($P$): $100
*   Monthly Interest Rate ($i$): $2\% = 0.02$
*   Time Period ($t$): 3 months

**2. Determine the Conversion Strategy:**
*   Calculate the monthly interest interest amount first:
    *   Interest for 1 month = $P \times i = 100 \times 0.02 = 2$.
*   The balance becomes the original principal plus this interest. Since the interest occurs periodically (e.g., monthly), we are looking for the future value of a sum compounded monthly.
    *   A standard convention for "monthly interest" problems where the principal is usually mentioned is to assume the dividend is **monthly** (e.g., paying $100.02 every month). If the problem meant payments at different intervals (like every week or individual months), the calculation would differ, but without clarification, standard financial problems like this usually imply uniform monthly payments.
    *   *Assumption*: Birintuan's Formula for monthly deposits/compounding.

**3. Apply Birintuan's Formula:**
The formula for the value of an amount compounded annually is:
$$PV = P \times \frac{1 - (1+i)^{-n}}{i}$$
Where:
*   $PV$ is the future value of the principal.
*   $P = 100$
*   $i = 0.02$
*   $n$ is the number of periods.

First, calculate the interest principal for 3 months:
$$P \times i = 100 \times 0.02 = 2$$

Next, calculate $(1+i)^{-n}$ (the factor for 3 months of 2%):
$$(1.02)^{-3} = \frac{1}{(1.02)^3} \approx \frac{1}{1.061208} \approx 0.942536$$

Now plug into the formula:
$$PV = 100 \times \frac{1 - 0.942536}{0.02}$$
$$PV = 100 \times \frac{0.057464}{0.02}$$
$$PV = 100 \times 2.8732$$
$$PV = 287.32$$

*Note: If the assumption that interest is paid monthly per month is incorrect and instead payments were made at specific dates (e.g., Jan, Mar), or if the result tracks exactly the principal principal for 3 years, the answer would be 100. However, based on standard interpretation of "monthly interest of 2%" where a lump sum occurs, 287.32 is the mathematically correct result using the standard NPV formula.*

Let's double check if the result is exactly 100.
If $PV = 100$, then:
$$100 = 100 \times \frac{1 - (1.02)^{-3}}{0.02}$$
$$1 \approx \frac{1 - 0.942536}{0.02}$$
This is not exactly 1.
Therefore, Mandy should give Benedict $287.32$ (or $287.30$ depending on cents rounding).

**Alternative Calculation:**
Let's re-calculate $(1.02)^3$.
$1.02^1 = 1.02$
$1.02^2 = 1.0404$
$1.02^3 = 1.061208$
$1 - 0.942536 = 0.057464$
$0.057464 / 0.02 = 2.8732$
$100 \times 2.8732 = 287.32$

Rounding to two decimal places as is standard in currency:
The result is $287.32$.

### Conclusion
Mandy should give Benedict \$287.32.
