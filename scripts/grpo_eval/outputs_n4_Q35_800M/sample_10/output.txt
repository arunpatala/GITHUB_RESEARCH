$$Memory_1 = 60$$

**Step 2: Calculate downloads in the second month.**
In the second month, the downloads were three times as many as the first month.
$$Memory_2 = 3 \times Memory_1$$
$$Memory_2 = 3 \times 60 = 180$$

**Step 3: Calculate downloads in the third month.**
In the third month, the downloads were reduced by 30% compared to the previous month (the second month).
First, calculate the reduction amount:
$$Reduction = Memory_2 \times 0.30 = 180 \times 0.30 = 54$$
Next, find the remaining balance (New Monthly Total):
$$Memory_3 = Memory_2 - Reduction = 180 - 54 = 126$$
Alternatively, we can calculate the remaining percentage (70% of 180):
$$Memory_3 = 0.70 \times 180 = 126$$

**Step 4: Calculate the total number of downloads.**
Sum the downloads from all three months:
$$Total = Memory_1 + Memory_2 + Memory_3$$
$$Total = 60 + 180 + 126$$
$$Total = 319$$
