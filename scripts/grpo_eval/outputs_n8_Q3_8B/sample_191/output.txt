To determine how many pounds of insects a flock of ten ducks needs per day, we start by calculating the total weekly consumption and then convert that to a daily amount.

---

### Step 1: Weekly Consumption for One Duck

Each duck eats **3.5 pounds of insects per week**.

---

### Step 2: Total Weekly Consumption for Ten Ducks

$$
\text{Total weekly consumption} = 3.5 \, \text{pounds/duck/week} \times 10 \, \text{ducks} = 35 \, \text{pounds/week}
$$

---

### Step 3: Convert Weekly to Daily Consumption

There are **7 days in a week**, so we divide the weekly total by 7 to get the daily requirement:

$$
\text{Daily consumption} = \frac{35 \, \text{pounds/week}}{7 \, \text{days/week}} = 5 \, \text{pounds/day}
