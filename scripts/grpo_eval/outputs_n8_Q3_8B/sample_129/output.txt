### Step 1: Calculate Total Time Spent Per Day

Each patient is seen for **24 minutes**, and the hospital sees **500 patients per day**.

$$
\text{Total time per day} = 500 \times 24 = 12,000 \text{ minutes}
$$

Convert minutes to hours:

$$
\text{Total time in hours} = \frac{12,000}{60} = 200 \text{ hours}
$$

---

### Step 2: Calculate Revenue

The hospital charges **$200 per hour** to patients.

$$
\text{Revenue} = 200 \text{ hours} \times 200 \text{ dollars/hour} = 40,000 \text{ dollars}
$$

---

### Step 3: Calculate Cost

The doctors charge **$150 per hour** to the hospital.

$$
\text{Cost} = 200 \text{ hours} \times 150 \text{ dollars/hour} = 30,000 \text{ dollars}
$$

---

### Step 4: Calculate Profit

$$
\text{Profit} = \text{Revenue} - \text{Cost} = 40,000 - 30,000 = 10,000 \text{ dollars}
$$
