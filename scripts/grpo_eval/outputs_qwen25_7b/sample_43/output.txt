To determine how many grams of chips you can eat given your daily calorie target, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Calculate the total number of calories available for the day:**
   \[
   \text{Total daily calorie target} = 2000 \text{ calories}
   \]
   \[
   \text{Calories already consumed} = 1800 \text{ calories}
   \]
   \[
   \text{Remaining calories} = 2000 - 1800 = 200 \text{ calories}
   \]

2. **Determine the number of servings that correspond to the remaining calories:**
   \[
   \text{Calories per serving} = 250 \text{ calories}
   \]
   \[
   \text{Number of servings allowed} = \frac{200 \text{ calories}}{250 \text{ calories/serving}} = 0.8 \text{ servings}
   \]

3. **Convert the number of servings into grams:**
   \[
   \text{Grams per serving} = \frac{300 \text{ grams}}{5 \text{ servings}} = 60 \text{ grams/serving}
   \]
   \[
   \text{Grams allowed} = 0.8 \text{ servings} \times 60 \text{ grams/serving} = 48 \text{ grams}
   \]

Thus, the number of grams you can eat is:
#### 48
