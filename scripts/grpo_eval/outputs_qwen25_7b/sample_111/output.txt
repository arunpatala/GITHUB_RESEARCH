To determine how much water the boat had taken on by the time Julia reached the shore, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Calculate the total distance Julia needs to row:**
   - The shore is 64 seconds away.
   - It takes her 16 seconds to row 20 feet.
   - Therefore, the rate at which she rows is:
     \[
     \frac{20 \text{ feet}}{16 \text{ seconds}} = \frac{5 \text{ feet}}{4 \text{ seconds}}
     \]
   - To find the total distance she rows in 64 seconds:
     \[
     \text{Total distance} = \left(\frac{5 \text{ feet}}{4 \text{ seconds}}\right) \times 64 \text{ seconds} = 80 \text{ feet}
     \]

2. **Determine the rate at which water enters the boat:**
   - The boat takes on 2 liters of water for every 10 feet rowed.
   - Therefore, the rate of water intake is:
     \[
     \frac{2 \text{ liters}}{10 \text{ feet}} = \frac{1 \text{ liter}}{5 \text{ feet}}
     \]

3. **Calculate the total amount of water taken on during the journey:**
   - Since the boat rows 80 feet to reach the shore:
     \[
     \text{Total water taken on} = \left(\frac{1 \text{ liter}}{5 \text{ feet}}\right) \times 80 \text{ feet} = 16 \text{ liters}
     \]

Thus, the total amount of water the boat had taken on by the time Julia reached the shore is ####16.
