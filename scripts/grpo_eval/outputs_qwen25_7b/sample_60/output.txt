To determine how many oranges are good, we need to account for all the different categories of oranges in the basket and subtract them from the total.

1. **Total Oranges**: The basket contains 25 oranges.
2. **Bad Oranges**: There is 1 bad orange.
3. **Unripe Oranges**: 20% of the oranges are unripe. To find out how many that is:
   \[
   20\% \text{ of } 25 = \frac{20}{100} \times 25 = 5 \text{ unripe oranges}
   \]
4. **Sour Oranges**: There are 2 sour oranges.

Now, let's sum up the oranges that are not good:
- Bad oranges: 1
- Unripe oranges: 5
- Sour oranges: 2

Adding these together:
\[
1 + 5 + 2 = 8
\]

The total number of good oranges is the total number of oranges minus the number of bad, unripe, and sour oranges:
\[
25 - 8 = 17
\]

Thus, the number of good oranges is:
\[
\boxed{17}
\]
