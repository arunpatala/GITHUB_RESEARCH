Each girl got \( \frac{1}{6} \) of 24 liters, which is:

\[ \frac{1}{6} \times 24 = 4 \text{ liters} \]

So, both girls together got:

\[ 4 + 4 = 8 \text{ liters} \]

The boy got 6 liters. Therefore, the total amount of water given out is:

\[ 8 + 6 = 14 \text{ liters} \]

Starting with 24 liters, the amount of water left is:

\[ 24 - 14 = 10 \text{ liters} \]
