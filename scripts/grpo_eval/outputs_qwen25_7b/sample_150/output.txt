To determine how long the winner will wait for the loser, we need to calculate the time it takes for each of them to travel the distance to their respective homes.

First, let's convert the distances from miles to feet:
- Steve's distance: \( 3 \) miles \( = 3 \times 5280 \) feet \( = 15840 \) feet
- Tim's distance: \( 2 \) miles \( = 2 \times 5280 \) feet \( = 10560 \) feet

Next, we calculate the time it takes for each of them to reach their homes:
- Time for Steve: 
  \[
  \text{Time} = \frac{\text{Distance}}{\text{Speed}} = \frac{15840 \text{ feet}}{440 \text{ feet per minute}} = 36 \text{ minutes}
  \]
- Time for Tim: 
  \[
  \text{Time} = \frac{\text{Distance}}{\text{Speed}} = \frac{10560 \text{ feet}}{264 \text{ feet per minute}} = 40 \text{ minutes}
  \]

Now, we find the difference in their times to determine how long the winner (Steve) will wait for the loser (Tim):
\[
40 \text{ minutes} - 36 \text{ minutes} = 4 \text{ minutes}
\]

Thus, the winner will wait for the loser for \boxed{4} minutes.
