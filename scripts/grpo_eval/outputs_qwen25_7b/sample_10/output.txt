To solve this problem, we need to calculate the number of downloads for each month and then sum them up.

1. **First Month:**
   - The program had 60 downloads.

2. **Second Month:**
   - The number of downloads is three times the first month.
   - Therefore, the number of downloads in the second month = \( 3 \times 60 = 180 \).

3. **Third Month:**
   - The number of downloads reduces by 30% from the second month.
   - First, find 30% of the second month's downloads:
     \[
     0.30 \times 180 = 54
     \]
   - Subtract this from the second month's downloads to get the third month's downloads:
     \[
     180 - 54 = 126
     \]

4. **Total Downloads Over Three Months:**
   - Sum the downloads from all three months:
     \[
     60 + 180 + 126 = 366
     \]
