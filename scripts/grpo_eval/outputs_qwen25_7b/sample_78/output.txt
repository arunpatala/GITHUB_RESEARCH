To determine how much money Vincent saves, we need to compare the cost of buying 18 flowers in the two different package options and then find the difference.

First, let's calculate the cost if Vincent buys the flowers in packages of 3 for $2.50 each:
- Each package contains 3 flowers.
- To get 18 flowers, he needs \( \frac{18}{3} = 6 \) packages.
- The total cost for 6 packages is \( 6 \times 2.50 = 15 \) dollars.

Next, let's calculate the cost if Vincent buys the flowers in packages of 2 for $1 each:
- Each package contains 2 flowers.
- To get 18 flowers, he needs \( \frac{18}{2} = 9 \) packages.
- The total cost for 9 packages is \( 9 \times 1 = 9 \) dollars.

Now, we find the difference in cost between the two options:
- The cost with packages of 3 is $15.
- The cost with packages of 2 is $9.
- The savings is \( 15 - 9 = 6 \) dollars.

Thus, Vincent saves \(\boxed{6}\) dollars by buying 18 flowers at the better price.
