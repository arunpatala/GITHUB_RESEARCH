To determine how many hours Jim spends on TV and reading in 4 weeks, we need to break down the problem step by step.

1. **Calculate the time spent on TV and reading each day:**
   - Jim spends 2 hours watching TV.
   - He reads for half as long as he watches TV, which is \( \frac{2}{2} = 1 \) hour.
   - Therefore, each day he spends \( 2 + 1 = 3 \) hours on TV and reading.

2. **Calculate the total time spent on TV and reading each week:**
   - Since he does this 3 times a week, the total time per week is \( 3 \times 3 = 9 \) hours.

3. **Calculate the total time spent on TV and reading in 4 weeks:**
   - Over 4 weeks, the total time is \( 9 \times 4 = 36 \) hours.

Thus, the total number of hours Jim spends on TV and reading in 4 weeks is ####36.
