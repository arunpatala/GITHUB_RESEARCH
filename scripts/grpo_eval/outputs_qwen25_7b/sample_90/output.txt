To determine how much potato salad Ted needs to bring, we first calculate the amount each type of dinosaur will eat.

1. **Adult Dinosaur Consumption:**
   - Each adult dinosaur eats 10 lbs of potato salad.
   - There are 20 adult dinosaurs.
   - Total consumption by adults = \( 20 \times 10 \) lbs

\[
20 \times 10 = 200 \text{ lbs}
\]

2. **Child Dinosaur Consumption:**
   - Each child dinosaur eats half as much as an adult.
   - Therefore, each child eats \( \frac{10}{2} = 5 \) lbs of potato salad.
   - There are 5 child dinosaurs.
   - Total consumption by children = \( 5 \times 5 \) lbs

\[
5 \times 5 = 25 \text{ lbs}
\]

3. **Total Potato Salad Needed:**
   - Sum the total consumption by adults and children.
   - Total potato salad needed = \( 200 + 25 \) lbs

\[
200 + 25 = 225 \text{ lbs}
\]

Thus, Ted needs to bring \(\boxed{225}\) pounds of potato salad to the picnic.
