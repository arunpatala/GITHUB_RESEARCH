After 20 years of working, Marcy is entitled to the standard annual pension of $50,000 per year. For each additional year she works beyond 20 years, she earns an extra 5% of the pension value on top of the standard amount.

For the 21st through 30th years, she earns an additional 5% of $50,000 per year. Let's calculate this additional amount:
\[ 5\% \text{ of } \$50,000 = 0.05 \times 50,000 = \$2,500 \]

So, for each of these 10 years, her total annual pension will be:
\[ \$50,000 + \$2,500 = \$52,500 \]

Therefore, if Marcy quits after 30 years, her annual pension will be:
