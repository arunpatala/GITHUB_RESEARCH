Let's denote the total number of vacuum cleaners Melanie started with as \( x \).

1. At the green house, she sold a third of her vacuum cleaners:
   \[
   \text{Vacuum cleaners sold at the green house} = \frac{x}{3}
   \]
   Remaining vacuum cleaners after the green house:
   \[
   x - \frac{x}{3} = \frac{2x}{3}
   \]

2. She then sold 2 more vacuum cleaners to the red house:
   \[
   \text{Remaining vacuum cleaners after the red house} = \frac{2x}{3} - 2
   \]

3. She sold half of what was left at the orange house:
   \[
   \text{Vacuum cleaners sold at the orange house} = \frac{1}{2} \left( \frac{2x}{3} - 2 \right)
   \]
   Remaining vacuum cleaners after the orange house:
   \[
   \left( \frac{2x}{3} - 2 \right) - \frac{1}{2} \left( \frac{2x}{3} - 2 \right) = \frac{1}{2} \left( \frac{2x}{3} - 2 \right)
   \]

4. According to the problem, she has 5 vacuum cleaners left:
   \[
   \frac{1}{2} \left( \frac{2x}{3} - 2 \right) = 5
   \]

5. Solve for \( x \):
   \[
   \frac{1}{2} \left( \frac{2x}{3} - 2 \right) = 5
   \]
   Multiply both sides by 2:
   \[
   \frac{2x}{3} - 2 = 10
   \]
   Add 2 to both sides:
   \[
   \frac{2x}{3} = 12
   \]
   Multiply both sides by 3:
   \[
   2x = 36
   \]
   Divide both sides by 2:
   \[
   x = 18
   \]

Therefore, Melanie started with ####18#### vacuum cleaners.
