To determine how many pieces of apple pie were taken by the guests, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Calculate the total number of pie pieces initially available:**
   - Grandma Jones baked 5 apple pies.
   - Each pie was cut into 8 pieces.
   \[
   5 \text{ pies} \times 8 \text{ pieces per pie} = 40 \text{ pieces}
   \]

2. **Determine the number of pieces remaining at the end of the evening:**
   - There were 14 pieces of pie left.

3. **Calculate the number of pieces taken by the guests:**
   - Subtract the number of remaining pieces from the initial total number of pieces.
   \[
   40 \text{ pieces} - 14 \text{ pieces} = 26 \text{ pieces}
   \]

Thus, the number of pieces taken by the guests is \(\boxed{26}\).
