To determine Sasha's profit from selling her leftover lumber, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Calculate the total cost of the 2x4x10 boards:**
   - Each 2x4x10 board originally cost $10.
   - Sasha has 10 such boards.
   \[
   \text{Total cost for 2x4x10 boards} = 10 \times 10 = 100 \text{ dollars}
   \]

2. **Calculate the total cost of the 4x4x10 boards:**
   - Each 4x4x10 board originally cost $16.
   - Sasha has 5 such boards.
   \[
   \text{Total cost for 4x4x10 boards} = 5 \times 16 = 80 \text{ dollars}
   \]

3. **Calculate the combined total cost of all the boards:**
   \[
   \text{Combined total cost} = 100 + 80 = 180 \text{ dollars}
   \]

4. **Determine the new price per board after the 50% increase:**
   - The original price of a 2x4x10 board was $10.
   - A 50% increase means the new price is:
   \[
   10 + (0.5 \times 10) = 10 + 5 = 15 \text{ dollars}
   \]
   - The original price of a 4x4x10 board was $16.
   - A 50% increase means the new price is:
   \[
   16 + (0.5 \times 16) = 16 + 8 = 24 \text{ dollars}
   \]

5. **Calculate the total revenue from selling all the boards at the new prices:**
   - Revenue from selling 10 2x4x10 boards at $15 each:
   \[
   10 \times 15 = 150 \text{ dollars}
   \]
   - Revenue from selling 5 4x4x10 boards at $24 each:
   \[
   5 \times 24 = 120 \text{ dollars}
   \]
   - Combined total revenue:
   \[
   150 + 120 = 270 \text{ dollars}
   \]

6. **Calculate the profit:**
   \[
   \text{Profit} = \text{Total revenue} - \text{Combined total cost} = 270 - 180 = 90 \text{ dollars}
   \]

Thus, Sasha makes a profit of \(\boxed{90}\) dollars.
