To determine how many pounds of insects a flock of ten ducks needs per day, we can follow these steps:

1. **Calculate the total weekly requirement for the flock:**
   Each duck needs 3.5 pounds of insects per week.
   \[
   \text{Total weekly requirement} = 10 \text{ ducks} \times 3.5 \text{ pounds/duck} = 35 \text{ pounds}
   \]

2. **Convert the weekly requirement into a daily requirement:**
   There are 7 days in a week.
   \[
   \text{Daily requirement} = \frac{35 \text{ pounds}}{7 \text{ days}} = 5 \text{ pounds/day}
   \]

Thus, the flock of ten ducks needs \boxed{5} pounds of insects per day.
