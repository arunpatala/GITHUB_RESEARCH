To determine how much money Jordan would earn in one week if he spent his video game time working instead, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Calculate the total hours Jordan spends playing video games in one week:**
   - Jordan plays for 2 hours each day.
   - There are 7 days in a week.
   \[
   2 \text{ hours/day} \times 7 \text{ days/week} = 14 \text{ hours/week}
   \]

2. **Determine Jordan's hourly wage from his part-time job:**
   - Jordan earns $10 per hour.

3. **Calculate the total earnings if Jordan worked those 14 hours:**
   \[
   14 \text{ hours/week} \times 10 \text{ dollars/hour} = 140 \text{ dollars/week}
   \]

Thus, if Jordan spent his video game time working instead, he would earn \(\boxed{140}\) dollars in one week.
