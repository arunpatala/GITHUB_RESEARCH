To determine how many boxes of pizza Marie ordered, we need to first calculate the total cost of the other items she purchased.

1. **Cost of the chicken meal:**
   \[
   \text{Cost of chicken meal} = \$12
   \]

2. **Cost of the milk:**
   \[
   \text{Cost of 5 packs of milk} = 5 \times \$3 = \$15
   \]

3. **Cost of the apples:**
   \[
   \text{Cost of 4 apples} = 4 \times \$1.50 = \$6
   \]

Next, we sum these amounts to find the total cost of the non-pizza items:
\[
\text{Total cost of non-pizza items} = \$12 + \$15 + \$6 = \$33
\]

Marie paid a total of \$50. Therefore, the amount spent on pizza is:
\[
\text{Amount spent on pizza} = \$50 - \$33 = \$17
\]

Each box of pizza costs \$8.50. To find the number of boxes of pizza Marie ordered, we divide the total amount spent on pizza by the cost per box:
\[
\text{Number of boxes of pizza} = \frac{\$17}{\$8.50} = 2
\]

Thus, Marie ordered \(\boxed{2}\) boxes of pizza. #### 2
