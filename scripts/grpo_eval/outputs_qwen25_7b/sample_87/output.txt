To determine Sylvie's annual salary after three more years of service, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Initial Salary**: Sylvie's initial monthly salary is $600.

2. **Annual Salary Calculation**: Since there are 12 months in a year, her initial annual salary is:
   \[
   600 \times 12 = 7200 \text{ dollars}
   \]

3. **Salary Increase Policy**: The company increases the salary by 10% every year for employees who have been with the company for five years. This increase applies starting from the sixth year.

4. **First Year After Five Years (Year 6)**: 
   - Calculate the 10% increase on the initial annual salary:
     \[
     7200 \times 0.10 = 720 \text{ dollars}
     \]
   - Add this increase to the initial annual salary:
     \[
     7200 + 720 = 7920 \text{ dollars}
     \]

5. **Second Year After Five Years (Year 7)**:
   - Apply another 10% increase to the new annual salary:
     \[
     7920 \times 0.10 = 792 \text{ dollars}
     \]
   - Add this increase to the previous annual salary:
     \[
     7920 + 792 = 8712 \text{ dollars}
     \]

6. **Third Year After Five Years (Year 8)**:
   - Apply another 10% increase to the new annual salary:
     \[
     8712 \times 0.10 = 871.20 \text{ dollars}
     \]
   - Add this increase to the previous annual salary:
     \[
     8712 + 871.20 = 9583.20 \text{ dollars}
     \]

Thus, Sylvie's annual salary after three more years of service is:
#### 9583.20
