To find out how many apples Jenna and her mother picked in total, we need to follow these steps:

1. Determine how many apples Jenna picked.
2. Add the number of apples Jenna picked to the number of apples her mother picked.

First, we know that Jenna's mother picked 20 apples. According to the problem, Jenna picked half as many apples as her mother. Therefore, we calculate the number of apples Jenna picked as follows:
\[ \text{Number of apples Jenna picked} = \frac{20}{2} = 10 \]

Next, we add the number of apples Jenna picked to the number of apples her mother picked to find the total number of apples picked by both:
\[ \text{Total number of apples} = 20 + 10 = 30 \]

Thus, the total number of apples picked by Jenna and her mother is:
#### 30
