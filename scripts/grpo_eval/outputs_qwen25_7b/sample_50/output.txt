To determine how much Lloyd makes from selling eggs per week, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Calculate daily earnings:**
   - Lloyd's chickens produce 252 eggs per day.
   - He sells the eggs at $2 per dozen.

2. **Convert daily egg production into dozens:**
   - There are 12 eggs in a dozen.
   - Therefore, the number of dozens produced per day is:
     \[
     \frac{252 \text{ eggs}}{12 \text{ eggs/dozen}} = 21 \text{ dozen}
     \]

3. **Calculate daily earnings:**
   - Lloyd sells each dozen for $2.
   - Therefore, his daily earnings are:
     \[
     21 \text{ dozen} \times 2 \text{ dollars/dozen} = 42 \text{ dollars}
     \]

4. **Calculate weekly earnings:**
   - There are 7 days in a week.
   - Therefore, his weekly earnings are:
     \[
     42 \text{ dollars/day} \times 7 \text{ days/week} = 294 \text{ dollars}
     \]

Thus, Lloyd makes \(\boxed{294}\) dollars per week from selling eggs.
