Then, twice the initial amount of bees leave the hive in the following 6 hours, which is 30 * 2 = 60 bees. This means that the total amount of bees that have left the hive so far is 30 + 60 = 90 bees. Since all of the bees that left in the second 6-hour period had not yet returned, this means that 90 bees that had left the hive now return in the last 6 hours.
