First, we calculate the total weight of the comic books that Uriah removes. Each comic book weighs \( \frac{1}{4} \) pound, and he removes 30 comic books.

\[
\text{Weight of comic books removed} = 30 \times \frac{1}{4} = 30 \times 0.25 = 7.5 \text{ pounds}
\]

Next, we determine how much more weight needs to be removed to reach the total of 15 pounds. 

\[
\text{Remaining weight to remove} = 15 - 7.5 = 7.5 \text{ pounds}
\]

Each toy weighs \( \frac{1}{2} \) pound. To find out how many toys need to be removed, we divide the remaining weight by the weight of one toy.

\[
\text{Number of toys to remove} = \frac{7.5}{\frac{1}{2}} = 7.5 \times 2 = 15
\]

Thus, Uriah needs to remove \(\boxed{15}\) toys.
