To solve this problem, we need to calculate the number of articles Meredith wrote each day and then multiply by the time it takes to write one article.

1. **Monday:**
   - Meredith wrote 5 articles.
   
2. **Tuesday:**
   - She wrote \( \frac{2}{5} \) times more articles than on Monday.
   - Number of articles on Tuesday = \( 5 + \left( \frac{2}{5} \times 5 \right) = 5 + 2 = 7 \).

3. **Wednesday:**
   - She wrote twice the number of articles she wrote on Tuesday.
   - Number of articles on Wednesday = \( 2 \times 7 = 14 \).

Next, we sum up the total number of articles written over these three days:
\[ 5 \text{ (Monday)} + 7 \text{ (Tuesday)} + 14 \text{ (Wednesday)} = 26 \text{ articles} \]

Each article takes 4 hours to write, so the total time spent writing articles is:
\[ 26 \text{ articles} \times 4 \text{ hours/article} = 104 \text{ hours} \]
