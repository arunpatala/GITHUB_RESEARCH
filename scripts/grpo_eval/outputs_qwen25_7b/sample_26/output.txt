To find out how much Mishka spent on all the clothing items, we need to calculate the total cost for each type of item and then sum these amounts.

First, let's calculate the total cost for the shorts:
- Mishka bought 3 pairs of shorts.
- Each pair of shorts costs $16.50.

\[
\text{Total cost for shorts} = 3 \times 16.50 = 49.50
\]

Next, let's calculate the total cost for the pants:
- Mishka bought 3 pairs of pants.
- Each pair of pants costs $22.50.

\[
\text{Total cost for pants} = 3 \times 22.50 = 67.50
\]

Then, let's calculate the total cost for the shoes:
- Mishka bought 3 pairs of shoes.
- Each pair of shoes costs $42.

\[
\text{Total cost for shoes} = 3 \times 42 = 126
\]

Now, we sum up the total costs for all the items:

\[
\text{Total cost} = 49.50 + 67.50 + 126 = 243
\]

Thus, Mishka spent \(\boxed{243}\) dollars on all the clothing items.
