\[ 20 - 5 = 15 \]

Now, we know that Brian has 15 video games at the time of the comparison. The problem states that Bobby has 5 fewer than 3 times as many video games as Brian. We can express this relationship with the following calculation:

\[ 3 \times 15 - 5 \]

First, we multiply 3 by 15:

\[ 3 \times 15 = 45 \]

Then, we subtract 5 from 45:

\[ 45 - 5 = 40 \]
