To solve this problem, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Determine the number of boys:**
   - We know that \( \frac{2}{5} \) of the 200 students are boys.
   - Calculate the number of boys:
     \[
     \text{Number of boys} = \frac{2}{5} \times 200 = \frac{400}{5} = 80
     \]

2. **Determine the number of girls:**
   - The total number of students is 200.
   - Subtract the number of boys from the total number of students to find the number of girls:
     \[
     \text{Number of girls} = 200 - 80 = 120
     \]

3. **Determine how many girls are in the girl scout:**
   - We know that \( \frac{2}{3} \) of the girls are in the girl scout.
   - Calculate the number of girls in the girl scout:
     \[
     \text{Number of girls in the girl scout} = \frac{2}{3} \times 120 = \frac{240}{3} = 80
     \]

4. **Determine how many girls are not in the girl scout:**
   - Subtract the number of girls in the girl scout from the total number of girls:
     \[
     \text{Number of girls not in the girl scout} = 120 - 80 = 40
     \]
