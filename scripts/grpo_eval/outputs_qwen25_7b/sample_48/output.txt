First, we need to convert the length of the wire from feet to inches because the pieces are measured in inches. There are 12 inches in a foot, so a 4-foot-long wire is:

4 feet * 12 inches/foot = 48 inches

Now, we can find out how many 6-inch pieces Tracy obtained by dividing the total length of the wire in inches by the length of each piece:

48 inches / 6 inches/piece = 8 pieces
