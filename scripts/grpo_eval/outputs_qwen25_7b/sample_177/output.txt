First, we calculate the amount Zaid spends on rent and car fuel.

Rent: \( \frac{1}{4} \) of $6000 = \( \frac{1}{4} \times 6000 = 1500 \) dollars

Car fuel: \( \frac{1}{3} \) of $6000 = \( \frac{1}{3} \times 6000 = 2000 \) dollars

Next, we find out how much is left after these expenses:

Remaining amount after rent and car fuel = \( 6000 - 1500 - 2000 = 2500 \) dollars

Zaid donates half of the remaining amount to his favorite charity:

Donation to charity = \( \frac{1}{2} \times 2500 = 1250 \) dollars

Now, we calculate the remaining amount after the donation:

Remaining amount after donation = \( 2500 - 1250 = 1250 \) dollars

Zaid gives his daughter 200 dollars and his wife 700 dollars:

Total given to daughter and wife = \( 200 + 700 = 900 \) dollars

Finally, we subtract this from the remaining amount to find out how much Zaid still has:

Money left with Zaid = \( 1250 - 900 = 350 \) dollars
