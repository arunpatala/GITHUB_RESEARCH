To find out how much Raphael spent in total, we need to calculate the cost of each item he bought and then add them all together.

   - Total cost for pens = \( 4 \times 1.5 = 6 \) dollars.

2. **Cost of Notebooks:**
   - Raphael bought 2 notebooks.
   - Each notebook costs $4.
   - Total cost for notebooks = \( 2 \times 4 = 8 \) dollars.

3. **Cost of Bond Paper:**
   - The rim of bond paper costs $20.
   - Total cost for bond paper = \( 20 \) dollars.

\[
6 \text{ (pens)} + 8 \text{ (notebooks)} + 20 \text{ (bond paper)} = 34 \text{ dollars}
