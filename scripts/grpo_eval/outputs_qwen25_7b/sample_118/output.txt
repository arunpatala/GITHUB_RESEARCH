To solve this problem, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Determine the initial number of vehicles:**
   - On the first day, there are 2 containers.
   - Each container has 5 vehicles.
   - Therefore, the total number of vehicles on the first day is:
     \[
     2 \text{ containers} \times 5 \text{ vehicles per container} = 10 \text{ vehicles}
     \]

2. **Calculate the additional number of vehicles brought in on the second day:**
   - The total number of vehicles at the port after the second day is 30.
   - Subtract the initial number of vehicles from the total number of vehicles:
     \[
     30 \text{ vehicles} - 10 \text{ vehicles} = 20 \text{ vehicles}
     \]

3. **Determine the number of containers brought in on the second day:**
   - Each container contains 5 vehicles.
   - To find the number of containers, divide the additional number of vehicles by the number of vehicles per container:
     \[
     \frac{20 \text{ vehicles}}{5 \text{ vehicles per container}} = 4 \text{ containers}
     \]

Thus, the number of containers that were imported on the second day is \(\boxed{4}\).
