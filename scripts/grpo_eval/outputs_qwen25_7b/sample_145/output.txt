To determine how many pineapples John can harvest in a year, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Calculate the total number of pineapples per hectare:**
   - John has 100 pineapples per hectare.

2. **Calculate the total number of pineapples for all hectares:**
   - John has 10 hectares.
   - Total pineapples = Number of pineapples per hectare × Number of hectares
   \[
   \text{Total pineapples} = 100 \times 10 = 1000
   \]

3. **Determine the harvesting frequency:**
   - John can harvest his pineapples every 3 months.

4. **Calculate the number of harvests in a year:**
   - There are 12 months in a year.
   - Number of harvests per year = Total months in a year ÷ Harvesting interval
   \[
   \text{Number of harvests per year} = \frac{12}{3} = 4
   \]

5. **Calculate the total number of pineapples harvested in a year:**
   - Total pineapples per harvest = 1000
   - Total number of harvests per year = 4
   - Total pineapples harvested in a year = Total pineapples per harvest × Number of harvests per year
   \[
   \text{Total pineapples harvested in a year} = 1000 \times 4 = 4000
   \]
