To find the total number of raspberries on the bush, we need to calculate the number of raspberries in the clusters and add the number of individual raspberries.

First, let's determine the number of raspberries in the clusters:
- There are 6 clusters.
- Each cluster contains 20 raspberries.

We can calculate the total number of raspberries in the clusters by multiplying the number of clusters by the number of raspberries per cluster:
\[
6 \text{ clusters} \times 20 \text{ raspberries per cluster} = 120 \text{ raspberries}
\]

Next, we add the number of individual raspberries:
- There are 67 individual raspberries.

Now, we sum the raspberries from the clusters and the individual raspberries:
\[
120 \text{ raspberries} + 67 \text{ raspberries} = 187 \text{ raspberries}
\]

Thus, the total number of raspberries on the bush is:
\[
\boxed{187}
\]
