To determine how many bags Jean can fill, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Calculate the number of lollipops left after Jean eats some:**
   - Jean starts with 30 lollipops.
   - Jean eats 2 lollipops.
   \[
   30 - 2 = 28
   \]
   So, Jean has 28 lollipops left.

2. **Determine how many bags can be filled with the remaining lollipops:**
   - Each bag contains 2 lollipops.
   - We need to divide the remaining lollipops by the number of lollipops per bag.
   \[
   \frac{28}{2} = 14
   \]

Therefore, Jean can fill \(\boxed{14}\) bags.
