To find out how much money Bailey started with, we need to subtract the total amount of her allowance from her final total.

First, calculate the total amount of allowance she received:
\[ \text{Allowance per week} = \$5 \]
\[ \text{Number of weeks} = 8 \]
\[ \text{Total allowance} = 5 \times 8 = \$40 \]

Next, we know that at the end of 8 weeks, Bailey has a total of $100. To find out how much she started with, we subtract the total allowance from her final total:
\[ \text{Initial amount} = \text{Final total} - \text{Total allowance} \]
\[ \text{Initial amount} = 100 - 40 = \$60 \]

Thus, Bailey started with ####60.
