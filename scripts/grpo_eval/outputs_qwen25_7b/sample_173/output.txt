First, we need to calculate the total minimum monthly payment for all debts:

- Student loans: $300
- Credit card: $200
- Mortgage: $500

Total minimum monthly payment = $300 + $200 + $500 = $1000

Next, we calculate 50% more than the minimum monthly payment:

50% of $1000 = 0.5 * $1000 = $500

So, Jessica pays $1000 (minimum) + $500 (50% more) = $1500 per month.

To find out how much she pays in a year, we multiply the monthly payment by 12:

$1500 * 12 = $18000
