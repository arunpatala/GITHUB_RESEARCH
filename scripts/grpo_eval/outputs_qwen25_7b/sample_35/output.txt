To determine the total points Mike scored in his ping pong game, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Points scored in the first 20 minutes:**
   Mike scores 4 points in the first 20 minutes.

2. **Points scored in the second 20 minutes:**
   Mike scores 25% more points in the second 20 minutes than he did in the first 20 minutes. To find out how many points this is, we calculate 25% of 4 points and add it to the original 4 points.
   
   \[
   25\% \text{ of } 4 = 0.25 \times 4 = 1
   \]
   
   Therefore, the points scored in the second 20 minutes are:
   
   \[
   4 + 1 = 5
   \]

3. **Total points scored:**
   To find the total points scored, we add the points from both halves of the game:
   
   \[
   4 + 5 = 9
   \]

Thus, the total points Mike scored is \(\boxed{9}\).
