To determine Eliza's earnings for the week, we need to calculate her regular pay and her overtime pay separately, then sum them up.

First, let's calculate her regular pay:
- Eliza's regular hourly rate is $10.
- She worked 40 hours at this rate.

\[
\text{Regular pay} = 40 \text{ hours} \times \$10/\text{hour} = \$400
\]

Next, let's calculate her overtime pay:
- Eliza worked 45 hours in total, so the number of overtime hours is \(45 - 40 = 5\) hours.
- Her overtime pay rate is 1.2 times her regular hourly rate.

\[
\text{Overtime pay rate} = 1.2 \times \$10 = \$12/\text{hour}
\]

\[
\text{Overtime pay} = 5 \text{ hours} \times \$12/\text{hour} = \$60
\]

Now, we add the regular pay and the overtime pay to find her total earnings for the week:

\[
\text{Total earnings} = \text{Regular pay} + \text{Overtime pay} = \$400 + \$60 = \$460
\]

Thus, Eliza's earnings for this week are \(\boxed{460}\).
