\[ 10 + 12 = 22 \text{ dollars} \]

Brittany's mom received $8 in change. This means that the amount of money she gave the cashier minus the total cost of the tickets equals $8. We can set up the equation:
\[ \text{Amount given} - 22 = 8 \]

\[ \text{Amount given} = 8 + 22 = 30 \]
