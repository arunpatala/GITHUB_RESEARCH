To determine the total number of quilt blocks Gene has, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Calculate the number of years Gene has been vacationing:**
   - Gene started vacationing at age 23.
   - He is currently 34 years old.
   - Therefore, the number of years he has been vacationing is:
     \[
     34 - 23 = 11 \text{ years}
     \]

2. **Determine the number of vacation blocks per year:**
   - Gene goes on 4 vacations each year.

3. **Calculate the total number of vacation blocks:**
   - Since he has been vacationing for 11 years and each year he goes on 4 vacations, the total number of vacation blocks (quilt blocks) is:
     \[
     11 \text{ years} \times 4 \text{ blocks/year} = 44 \text{ blocks}
     \]

Thus, the total number of quilt blocks Gene has is \(\boxed{44}\).
