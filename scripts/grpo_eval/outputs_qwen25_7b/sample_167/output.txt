Let's denote Brooke's current age as \( B \) and Seth's current age as \( S \).

From the problem, we know two things:
1. Seth is twice as old as Brooke: \( S = 2B \).
2. In 2 years, the sum of their ages will be 28: \( (S + 2) + (B + 2) = 28 \).

First, simplify the second equation:
\[ S + 2 + B + 2 = 28 \]
\[ S + B + 4 = 28 \]
Subtract 4 from both sides:
\[ S + B = 24 \]

Now substitute \( S = 2B \) into the equation \( S + B = 24 \):
\[ 2B + B = 24 \]
\[ 3B = 24 \]
Divide both sides by 3:
\[ B = 8 \]

Now that we know Brooke's age, we can find Seth's age using \( S = 2B \):
\[ S = 2 \times 8 = 16 \]

So, Seth is 16 years old. #### 16
