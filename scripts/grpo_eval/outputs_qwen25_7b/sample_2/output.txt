To determine Josh's profit from flipping the house, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Calculate the total cost of the house including repairs:**
   - Purchase price of the house: \( \$80,000 \)
   - Cost of repairs: \( \$50,000 \)
   - Total cost: 
     \[
     80,000 + 50,000 = 130,000
     \]

2. **Determine the new value of the house after the increase:**
   - The value of the house increased by 150%.
   - To find 150% of the total cost:
     \[
     1.5 \times 130,000 = 195,000
     \]
   - Therefore, the new value of the house is:
     \[
     130,000 + 195,000 = 325,000
     \]

3. **Calculate the profit:**
   - Profit is the difference between the new value of the house and the total cost:
     \[
     325,000 - 130,000 = 195,000
     \]

Thus, Josh made a profit of \(\boxed{195000}\).
