To solve this problem, we need to break it down into parts and use the information provided.

First, let's determine the total amount of straw given to the hamsters. We know there are 10 cages of hamsters, and each hamster gets 5 pieces of straw. Therefore, the total amount of straw given to the hamsters is:
\[ 10 \text{ cages} \times 5 \text{ pieces per hamster} = 50 \text{ pieces of straw} \]

Next, we know that the total amount of straw distributed among all the rodents is 160 pieces. Out of this, 50 pieces were given to the hamsters. So, the remaining straw was given to the rats and the rabbits. The amount of straw given to the rabbits is 20 pieces. Therefore, the amount of straw given to the rats is:
\[ 160 \text{ pieces} - 50 \text{ pieces} - 20 \text{ pieces} = 90 \text{ pieces} \]

We know that each rat gets 6 pieces of straw and there are 3 cages of rats. Let \( r \) be the number of rats in each cage. Then the total number of rats is \( 3r \), and the total amount of straw given to the rats is:
\[ 3r \times 6 = 18r \]
We already calculated that the total amount of straw given to the rats is 90 pieces, so we can set up the equation:
\[ 18r = 90 \]
Solving for \( r \):
\[ r = \frac{90}{18} = 5 \]

Therefore, the number of rats in each cage is \(\boxed{5}\).
