To determine how many 30-minute episodes Frankie watched on Wednesday, we need to first calculate the total amount of time he spent watching TV from Monday to Friday.

We know:
- On Monday and Tuesday, he watched 1 hour each day.
- On Thursday, he watched 1 hour and a 30-minute show.
- On Friday, he watched 2 hours.
- The total time spent watching TV is 7 hours.

First, let's add up the time spent watching TV on Monday, Tuesday, Thursday, and Friday:

\[
1 \text{ hour (Monday)} + 1 \text{ hour (Tuesday)} + 1 \text{ hour (Thursday)} + 2 \text{ hours (Friday)} = 5 \text{ hours}
\]

Since the total time spent watching TV is 7 hours, the remaining time must be the time spent watching TV on Wednesday:

\[
7 \text{ hours} - 5 \text{ hours} = 2 \text{ hours}
\]

On Wednesday, Frankie watched a few episodes of a 30-minute show. Let \( x \) be the number of 30-minute episodes he watched. Therefore, the total time spent watching TV on Wednesday can be expressed as:

\[
0.5x \text{ hours}
\]

We set this equal to the remaining time:

\[
0.5x = 2
\]

To find \( x \), we solve for \( x \):

\[
x = \frac{2}{0.5} = 4
\]

Thus, Frankie watched \(\boxed{4}\) 30-minute episodes on Wednesday.
