Let's denote the number of bags sold by the trader as \( x \).

The cost price for each bag is $20, and the transportation cost for each bag is $2. Therefore, the total cost price per bag is:
\[ 20 + 2 = 22 \text{ dollars} \]

The selling price per bag is $30. The profit per bag is:
\[ 30 - 22 = 8 \text{ dollars} \]

The total profit from selling \( x \) bags is given as $400. So, we can set up the following equation:
\[ 8x = 400 \]

To find \( x \), we solve the equation by dividing both sides by 8:
\[ x = \frac{400}{8} = 50 \]

Therefore, the number of bags sold is:
\[ \boxed{50} \]
