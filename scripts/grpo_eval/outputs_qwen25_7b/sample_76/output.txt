To determine how many bags of dog food Cecilia will use in the first year, we need to calculate the total amount of dog food required for the first 365 days and then find out how many 110-cup bags are needed.

First, let's break down the feeding schedule:
- For the first 180 days, the puppy is fed 1 cup per day.
- For the remaining days (365 - 180 = 185 days), the puppy is fed 2 cups per day.

Now, we calculate the total amount of dog food needed for each period:

1. **First 180 days:**
   \[
   180 \text{ days} \times 1 \text{ cup/day} = 180 \text{ cups}
   \]

2. **Remaining 185 days:**
   \[
   185 \text{ days} \times 2 \text{ cups/day} = 370 \text{ cups}
   \]

Next, we add the amounts from both periods to get the total amount of dog food needed for the first year:
\[
180 \text{ cups} + 370 \text{ cups} = 550 \text{ cups}
\]

Finally, we determine how many 110-cup bags are needed to provide 550 cups of dog food:
\[
\frac{550 \text{ cups}}{110 \text{ cups/bag}} = 5 \text{ bags}
\]

Thus, the number of bags of dog food Cecilia will use in the first year is:
#### 5
