To determine the total number of doorbell rings, we need to calculate the number of times each friend pressed the doorbell and then sum these values.

First, let's find out how many times the second friend pressed the doorbell. According to the problem, the second friend pressed the doorbell \( \frac{1}{4} \) times more than the first friend. Since the first friend pressed the doorbell 20 times, we can calculate the second friend's presses as follows:
\[
20 + \frac{1}{4} \times 20 = 20 + 5 = 25
\]
So, the second friend pressed the doorbell 25 times.

Next, we know that the fourth friend pressed the doorbell 60 times. The third friend pressed the doorbell 10 times more than the fourth friend. Therefore, the third friend's presses are:
\[
60 + 10 = 70
\]
So, the third friend pressed the doorbell 70 times.

Now, we sum the number of doorbell rings from all four friends:
\[
20 + 25 + 70 + 60 = 175
\]
