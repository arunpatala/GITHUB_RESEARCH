To determine how many stickers Charlie has left, we need to follow these steps:

1. Start with the initial number of stickers Charlie had.
2. Add the stickers he bought from the store.
3. Add the stickers he received for his birthday.
4. Subtract the stickers he gave to his sister.
5. Subtract the stickers he used to decorate a greeting card.

Let's go through each step in detail:

1. Charlie initially had 10 stickers.
2. He bought 21 more stickers, so now he has:
   \[
   10 + 21 = 31
   \]
3. He received 23 more stickers for his birthday, so now he has:
   \[
   31 + 23 = 54
   \]
4. He gave 9 stickers to his sister, so now he has:
   \[
   54 - 9 = 45
   \]
5. He used 28 stickers to decorate a greeting card, so now he has:
   \[
   45 - 28 = 17
   \]

Therefore, the number of stickers Charlie has left is \boxed{17}.
