To determine the total number of hours Sadie slept throughout the week, we need to break down her sleeping pattern day by day.

1. **Monday:**
   Sadie slept 8 hours.

2. **Tuesday and Wednesday:**
   She slept 2 hours less than Monday each day.
   \[
   8 - 2 = 6 \text{ hours per day}
   \]
   Therefore, for both Tuesday and Wednesday:
   \[
   6 + 6 = 12 \text{ hours}
   \]

3. **Thursday, Friday, Saturday, and Sunday:**
   She slept 1 hour more than the 6 hours she slept on Tuesday and Wednesday.
   \[
   6 + 1 = 7 \text{ hours per day}
   \]
   Therefore, for the four days (Thursday, Friday, Saturday, and Sunday):
   \[
   4 \times 7 = 28 \text{ hours}
   \]

4. **Total hours for the week:**
   Adding up all the hours:
   \[
   8 \text{ (Monday)} + 12 \text{ (Tuesday and Wednesday)} + 28 \text{ (Thursday, Friday, Saturday, and Sunday)} = 48 \text{ hours}
   \]

Thus, the total number of hours Sadie slept throughout the week is ####48.
