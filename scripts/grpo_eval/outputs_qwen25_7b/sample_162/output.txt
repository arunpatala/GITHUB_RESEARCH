To determine the cost of a bag of marbles after 36 months, given that the price increases by 20% of the original price every two months, we can follow these steps:

1. **Identify the initial price and the rate of increase:**
   - Initial price \( P_0 = \$20 \)
   - Increase rate per period (every 2 months) is 20%, which can be expressed as a decimal: \( r = 0.20 \)

2. **Determine the number of periods in 36 months:**
   - Since the price increases every 2 months, the number of periods in 36 months is:
     \[
     \text{Number of periods} = \frac{36 \text{ months}}{2 \text{ months/period}} = 18 \text{ periods}
     \]

3. **Use the formula for compound interest to find the final price:**
   The formula for the future value \( P_n \) after \( n \) periods with a growth rate \( r \) is:
   \[
   P_n = P_0 \times (1 + r)^n
   \]
   Here, \( P_0 = 20 \), \( r = 0.20 \), and \( n = 18 \).

4. **Substitute the values into the formula:**
   \[
   P_{18} = 20 \times (1 + 0.20)^{18}
   \]
   Simplify inside the parentheses first:
   \[
   P_{18} = 20 \times (1.20)^{18}
   \]

5. **Calculate \( (1.20)^{18} \):**
   Using a calculator:
   \[
   (1.20)^{18} \approx 79.058
   \]

6. **Multiply by the initial price:**
   \[
   P_{18} = 20 \times 79.058 \approx 1581.16
   \]

Therefore, the cost of a bag of marbles after 36 months is approximately \(\boxed{1581.16}\).
