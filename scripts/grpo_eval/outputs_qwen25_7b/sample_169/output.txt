To determine how many miles Pancho walks in a week, we need to break down his walking schedule and calculate accordingly.

First, let's identify the number of weekdays and weekends in a week:
- There are 5 weekdays (Monday through Friday).
- There are 2 weekend days (Saturday and Sunday).

Next, we calculate the total distance Pancho walks during the weekdays:
- On each weekday, Pancho walks 20 miles.
- Therefore, for 5 weekdays, the total distance is \( 5 \times 20 = 100 \) miles.

Now, we calculate the total distance Pancho walks during the weekends:
- On each weekend day, Pancho walks 10 miles.
- Therefore, for 2 weekend days, the total distance is \( 2 \times 10 = 20 \) miles.

Finally, we add the distances from the weekdays and the weekends to find the total distance walked in a week:
\[ 100 \text{ miles} + 20 \text{ miles} = 120 \text{ miles} \]

Thus, the total number of miles Pancho walks in a week is ####120####.
