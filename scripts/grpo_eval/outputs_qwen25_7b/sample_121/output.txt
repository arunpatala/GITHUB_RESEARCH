To find out how many fruits Morisette and Kael have in total, we need to first determine the number of each type of fruit they brought.

1. **Morisette's Fruits:**
   - Apples: 5
   - Oranges: 8

2. **Kael's Fruits:**
   - Kael brought twice the number of apples that Morisette brought:
     \[
     2 \times 5 = 10 \text{ apples}
     \]
   - Kael brought half the number of oranges that Morisette brought:
     \[
     \frac{8}{2} = 4 \text{ oranges}
     \]

3. **Total Fruits:**
   - Total apples: 
     \[
     5 + 10 = 15 \text{ apples}
     \]
   - Total oranges:
     \[
     8 + 4 = 12 \text{ oranges}
     \]
   - Total fruits:
     \[
     15 + 12 = 27
     \]
