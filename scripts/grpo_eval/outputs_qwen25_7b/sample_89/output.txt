First, we need to determine how many guests will actually attend the party. Since 1/4 of the invited guests don't show up, this means that 3/4 of the invited guests will attend.

The number of guests who will attend is:
\[ \frac{3}{4} \times 16 = 12 \]

Next, we need to calculate the number of gift bags Christina will need. She needs 0.75 gift bags per attending guest. Therefore, the total number of gift bags required is:
\[ 12 \times 0.75 = 9 \]

Each gift bag costs $2, so the total cost for the gift bags is:
\[ 9 \times 2 = 18 \]

Thus, the amount Christina will spend on gift bags is:
#### 18
