To determine how much Cynthia will spend on ice cream after 60 days, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Calculate the total number of servings Cynthia consumes in 60 days:**
   - Cynthia eats 1 serving of ice cream every night.
   - There are 60 nights in 60 days.
   \[
   \text{Total servings} = 1 \text{ serving/night} \times 60 \text{ nights} = 60 \text{ servings}
   \]

2. **Determine how many cartons of ice cream Cynthia needs:**
   - Each carton contains 15 servings.
   \[
   \text{Number of cartons} = \frac{60 \text{ servings}}{15 \text{ servings/carton}} = 4 \text{ cartons}
   \]

3. **Calculate the total cost of the ice cream:**
   - Each carton costs $4.00.
   \[
   \text{Total cost} = 4 \text{ cartons} \times 4 \text{ dollars/carton} = 16 \text{ dollars}
   \]

Thus, the amount Cynthia will spend on ice cream after 60 days is ####16.
