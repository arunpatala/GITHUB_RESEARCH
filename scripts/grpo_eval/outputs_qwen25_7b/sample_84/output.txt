\[ W + L = 22 \]

Additionally, the team won 8 more games than they lost, which gives us another equation:

\[ W = L + 8 \]

We can substitute the second equation into the first one to solve for \( L \):

\[ (L + 8) + L = 22 \]
\[ 2L + 8 = 22 \]
\[ 2L = 14 \]
\[ L = 7 \]

Now that we know the team lost 7 games, we can find out how many games they won by substituting \( L \) back into the second equation:

\[ W = L + 8 \]
\[ W = 7 + 8 \]
\[ W = 15 \]
