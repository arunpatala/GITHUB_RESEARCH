To determine the total value of all the toys in the room, we need to find the individual values of each type of toy and then sum them up.

1. **Value of a Red Car:**
   - We know that one red car costs $4.
   - Therefore, the value of 5 red cars is:
     \[
     5 \times 4 = 20 \text{ dollars}
     \]

2. **Value of an Action Figure:**
   - We are given that an action figure costs $5.
   - Therefore, the value of 3 action figures is:
     \[
     3 \times 5 = 15 \text{ dollars}
     \]

3. **Value of the Doll:**
   - The doll costs as much as 3 action figures.
   - Since one action figure costs $5, three action figures cost:
     \[
     3 \times 5 = 15 \text{ dollars}
     \]
   - Therefore, the value of the doll is also $15.

4. **Total Value of All Toys:**
   - Summing up the values of all the toys:
     \[
     20 \text{ (red cars)} + 15 \text{ (action figures)} + 15 \text{ (doll)} = 50 \text{ dollars}
     \]

Thus, the total value of all the toys is ####50.
