To determine the total number of bananas the zookeeper needs to order for 2 months, we first calculate the monthly requirement for each type of ape and then extend that to two months.

1. **Monkeys:**
   - Monthly requirement: 200 bananas
   - For 2 months: \( 200 \times 2 = 400 \) bananas

2. **Gorillas:**
   - Monthly requirement: 400 bananas
   - For 2 months: \( 400 \times 2 = 800 \) bananas

3. **Baboons:**
   - Monthly requirement: 100 bananas
   - For 2 months: \( 100 \times 2 = 200 \) bananas

Next, we sum the total number of bananas required for all the apes over the 2-month period:

\[
400 \text{ (monkeys)} + 800 \text{ (gorillas)} + 200 \text{ (baboons)} = 1400 \text{ bananas}
\]

Thus, the zookeeper needs to order \(\boxed{1400}\) bananas to last for 2 months. #### 1400
