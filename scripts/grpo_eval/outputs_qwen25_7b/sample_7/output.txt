   \[
   \text{Time to download 80 GB} = \frac{80 \text{ GB}}{2 \text{ GB/minute}} = 40 \text{ minutes}
   \]

3. **Add the time for the Windows update installation:**
   \[
   \text{Total time so far} = 40 \text{ minutes} + 20 \text{ minutes} = 60 \text{ minutes}
   \]

4. **Calculate the remaining size of the file:**
   \[
   \text{Remaining size} = 200 \text{ GB} - 80 \text{ GB} = 120 \text{ GB}
   \]

5. **Determine the time taken to download the remaining 120 GB:**
   \[
   \text{Time to download 120 GB} = \frac{120 \text{ GB}}{2 \text{ GB/minute}} = 60 \text{ minutes}
   \]

6. **Calculate the total time to complete the download:**
   \[
   \text{Total time} = 60 \text{ minutes} + 60 \text{ minutes} = 120 \text{ minutes}
