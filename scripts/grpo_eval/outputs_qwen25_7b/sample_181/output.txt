To determine the number of red roses Fred needs to deliver, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Identify the relationship between the quantities:**
   - Let \( W \) represent the number of white carnations.
   - Sandra ordered 200 pink calla lilies, which are five times the number of white carnations.
     \[
     5W = 200
     \]

2. **Solve for \( W \):**
   \[
   W = \frac{200}{5} = 40
   \]
   So, Sandra ordered 40 white carnations.

3. **Determine the number of red roses:**
   - Sandra ordered four times as many red roses as white carnations.
     \[
     R = 4W
     \]
   - Substitute \( W = 40 \):
     \[
     R = 4 \times 40 = 160
     \]

Therefore, Fred must deliver \(\boxed{160}\) red roses by 5 pm to satisfy Sandra's order and keep her business.
