To determine the total number of pets that Jan, Marcia, and Cindy have, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Identify the number of pets Cindy has:**
   Cindy has 4 pets.

2. **Determine the number of pets Marcia has:**
   Marcia has two more pets than Cindy.
   \[
   \text{Number of pets Marcia has} = 4 + 2 = 6
   \]

3. **Determine the number of pets Jan has:**
   Jan has three times the number of pets as Marcia.
   \[
   \text{Number of pets Jan has} = 3 \times 6 = 18
   \]

4. **Calculate the total number of pets:**
   Add the number of pets each person has.
   \[
   \text{Total number of pets} = 4 + 6 + 18 = 28
   \]

Thus, the total number of pets the three have is ####28.
