To determine how many miles Sophia can drive on a single tank of gas, we need to first find out how many miles she can drive per gallon of gas.

1. **Calculate miles per gallon (mpg):**
   - Sophia has traveled 100 miles using 4 gallons of gas.
   - Therefore, the miles per gallon is:
     \[
     \text{mpg} = \frac{100 \text{ miles}}{4 \text{ gallons}} = 25 \text{ miles per gallon}
     \]

2. **Determine the total distance for a full tank:**
   - Sophia's car's tank holds 12 gallons of gas.
   - Using the mpg calculated above, the total distance she can drive on a full tank is:
     \[
     \text{Total distance} = 25 \text{ miles per gallon} \times 12 \text{ gallons} = 300 \text{ miles}
     \]

Thus, Sophia can drive \(\boxed{300}\) miles on a single tank of gas. #### 300
