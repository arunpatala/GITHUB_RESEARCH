To determine the total number of tickets used by Pam and Fred, we need to break down the problem step by step.

- Pam rode the roller coaster 2 times.
- Fred rode the roller coaster 4 times.
- Therefore, the total number of rides on the roller coaster is \(2 + 4 = 6\).
- Since each ride costs 6 tickets, the total number of tickets used for the roller coaster is \(6 \times 6 = 36\).

Next, let's calculate the number of tickets used for the luge:
- Both Pam and Fred rode the luge 2 times each.
- Therefore, the total number of rides on the luge is \(2 + 2 = 4\).
- Since each ride costs 6 tickets, the total number of tickets used for the luge is \(4 \times 6 = 24\).

Finally, we add the tickets used for the roller coaster and the luge to find the total number of tickets used:
\[36 + 24 = 60\]

Thus, the total number of tickets used by Pam and Fred is \(\boxed{60}\).
