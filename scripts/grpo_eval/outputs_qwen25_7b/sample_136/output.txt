To find the number of female Scottish unicorns, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Determine the number of unicorns in the Scottish Highlands:**
   - We know that one third of the 27 unicorns are in the Scottish Highlands.
   - Calculate one third of 27:
     \[
     \frac{1}{3} \times 27 = 9
     \]
   - So, there are 9 unicorns in the Scottish Highlands.

2. **Determine the number of female Scottish unicorns:**
   - We know that two thirds of the Scottish unicorns are female.
   - Calculate two thirds of 9:
     \[
     \frac{2}{3} \times 9 = 6
     \]
   - So, there are 6 female Scottish unicorns.

Thus, the number of female Scottish unicorns is \boxed{6}.
