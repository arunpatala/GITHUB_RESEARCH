To find the number of pink gumballs, we start by defining the relationship given in the problem. Let's denote the number of blue gumballs as \( B \) and the number of pink gumballs as \( P \).

According to the problem:
\[ P = 4B + 22 \]

We are given that there are 12 blue gumballs:
\[ B = 12 \]

Now, substitute \( B = 12 \) into the equation for \( P \):
\[ P = 4(12) + 22 \]

Calculate the multiplication first:
\[ P = 48 + 22 \]

Then add the numbers together:
\[ P = 70 \]

So, the number of pink gumballs is:
#### 70
