First, we calculate the number of pieces Poppy places on the board, which is a quarter of the total 1000 pieces:

\[ \frac{1}{4} \times 1000 = 250 \]

After Poppy places these 250 pieces, there are:

\[ 1000 - 250 = 750 \]

pieces left. Her mom then places a third of the remaining 750 pieces:

\[ \frac{1}{3} \times 750 = 250 \]

So, after her mom places these 250 pieces, the number of pieces left to be placed is:

\[ 750 - 250 = 500 \]

#### 500
