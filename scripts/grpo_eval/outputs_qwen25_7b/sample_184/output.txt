To solve this, we need to calculate the probabilities of two events and then find the difference between them.

1. **Probability of rolling a number greater than 3:**
   - The numbers greater than 3 on a six-sided die are 4, 5, and 6.
   - There are 3 favorable outcomes out of 6 possible outcomes.
   - Probability = \( \frac{3}{6} = \frac{1}{2} \).

2. **Probability of rolling two even numbers in a row:**
   - The even numbers on a six-sided die are 2, 4, and 6.
   - There are 3 even numbers out of 6 possible outcomes.
   - The probability of rolling an even number on one roll is \( \frac{3}{6} = \frac{1}{2} \).
   - Since the rolls are independent, the probability of rolling two even numbers in a row is \( \left( \frac{1}{2} \right) \times \left( \frac{1}{2} \right) = \frac{1}{4} \).

3. **Difference in probabilities:**
   - Probability of rolling a number greater than 3: \( \frac{1}{2} \)
   - Probability of rolling two even numbers in a row: \( \frac{1}{4} \)
   - Difference: \( \frac{1}{2} - \frac{1}{4} = \frac{2}{4} - \frac{1}{4} = \frac{1}{4} \)

4. **Expressing the difference as a percentage:**
   - \( \frac{1}{4} = 0.25 \)
   - Convert to percentage: \( 0.25 \times 100\% = 25\% \)
