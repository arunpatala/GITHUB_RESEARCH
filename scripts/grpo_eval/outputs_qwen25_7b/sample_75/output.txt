To find the average square footage of a level in Luke's 4-level sandcastle, we first need to determine the square footage of each level.

Given:
- The top (4th) level has a square footage of 16 square feet.
- Each level has half the square footage of the level directly below it.

Let's calculate the square footage for each level:

1. **Top Level (Level 4):**
   \[
   \text{Square footage} = 16 \text{ square feet}
   \]

2. **Third Level (Level 3):**
   \[
   \text{Square footage} = 16 \times 2 = 32 \text{ square feet}
   \]

3. **Second Level (Level 2):**
   \[
   \text{Square footage} = 32 \times 2 = 64 \text{ square feet}
   \]

4. **Bottom Level (Level 1):**
   \[
   \text{Square footage} = 64 \times 2 = 128 \text{ square feet}
   \]

Now, we sum the square footages of all levels:
\[
16 + 32 + 64 + 128 = 240 \text{ square feet}
\]

Next, we find the average square footage by dividing the total square footage by the number of levels:
\[
\text{Average square footage} = \frac{240}{4} = 60 \text{ square feet}
\]

Thus, the average square footage of a level is:
#### 60
