To determine how much Juan and Carlos spent on lemons, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Calculate the total revenue:**
   - They made a profit of $25.
   - Let \( C \) be their total cost.
   - Profit is calculated as Revenue minus Cost: 
     \[
     \text{Profit} = \text{Revenue} - \text{Cost}
     \]
   - Therefore:
     \[
     25 = \text{Revenue} - C
     \]
   - Rearrange to find Revenue:
     \[
     \text{Revenue} = C + 25
     \]

2. **Determine the cost per gallon:**
   - Each gallon costs $3 for lemons and $2 for sugar.
   - Total cost per gallon:
     \[
     \text{Cost per gallon} = 3 + 2 = 5 \text{ dollars}
     \]

3. **Express the total cost in terms of gallons:**
   - Let \( G \) be the number of gallons they made.
   - Total cost:
     \[
     C = 5G
     \]

4. **Relate revenue to the number of glasses sold:**
   - They sell each glass for $0.50.
   - They get 20 glasses per gallon.
   - Total number of glasses sold:
     \[
     \text{Total glasses} = 20G
     \]
   - Total revenue:
     \[
     \text{Revenue} = 0.50 \times 20G = 10G
     \]

5. **Set up the equation using the revenue and cost relationship:**
   - From step 1, we have:
     \[
     10G = 5G + 25
     \]

6. **Solve for \( G \):**
   - Subtract \( 5G \) from both sides:
     \[
     10G - 5G = 25
     \]
     \[
     5G = 25
     \]
   - Divide by 5:
     \[
     G = 5
     \]

7. **Calculate the total cost on lemons:**
   - Since each gallon costs $3 for lemons:
     \[
     \text{Total cost on lemons} = 3G = 3 \times 5 = 15 \text{ dollars}
     \]

Thus, the amount they spent on lemons is ####15.
