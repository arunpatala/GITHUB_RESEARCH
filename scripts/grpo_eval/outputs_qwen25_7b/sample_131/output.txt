To find out how much Joseph's total expenditure was for May and June, we need to follow these steps:

1. Determine Joseph's expenditure in June.
2. Add the expenditures of May and June together.

First, let's find out how much Joseph spent in June. We know that his expenditure in June was $60 less than in May. Since his expenditure in May was $500, we can calculate his June expenditure as follows:
\[ 500 - 60 = 440 \]
So, Joseph's expenditure in June was $440.

Next, we add the expenditures of May and June together:
\[ 500 + 440 = 940 \]

Therefore, Joseph's total expenditure for those two months is \####940.
