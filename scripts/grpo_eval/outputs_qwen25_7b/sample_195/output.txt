First, we need to find out how much a pen costs. According to the problem, a pen costs as much as a pencil and an eraser combined.

The cost of a pencil is $1.20 and the cost of an eraser is $0.30. Therefore, the cost of a pen is:
\[ 1.20 + 0.30 = 1.50 \]

Now, we need to find out how much 8 pens will cost. Since each pen costs $1.50, the cost of 8 pens is:
\[ 8 \times 1.50 = 12.00 \]

Therefore, the cost of 8 pens is \(\boxed{12.00}\). #### 12.00
