To determine how much feed Wendi needs to give her chickens in the final meal of the day, we first need to calculate the total amount of feed required for all the chickens over the entire day.

Each chicken requires 3 cups of feed per day. With 20 chickens, the total daily feed requirement is:
\[ 20 \text{ chickens} \times 3 \text{ cups/chicken} = 60 \text{ cups} \]

Next, we know that Wendi has already given her chickens 15 cups in the morning and 25 cups in the afternoon. The total amount of feed given so far is:
\[ 15 \text{ cups} + 25 \text{ cups} = 40 \text{ cups} \]

To find out how much more feed is needed for the final meal, we subtract the amount already given from the total daily requirement:
\[ 60 \text{ cups} - 40 \text{ cups} = 20 \text{ cups} \]

Therefore, the amount of feed Wendi needs to give her chickens in the final meal of the day is:
#### 20
