To solve this problem, we need to determine how many crabs each person has and then sum these amounts.

1. **Bo's Crabs:**
   - We are given that Bo has 40 crabs.
   \[
   \text{Bo's crabs} = 40
   \]

2. **Monic's Crabs:**
   - Monic has 4 fewer crabs than Bo.
   \[
   \text{Monic's crabs} = \text{Bo's crabs} - 4 = 40 - 4 = 36
   \]

3. **Rani's Crabs:**
   - Rani has ten more crabs than Monic.
   \[
   \text{Rani's crabs} = \text{Monic's crabs} + 10 = 36 + 10 = 46
   \]

4. **Total Number of Crabs:**
   - To find the total number of crabs, we add the crabs of Bo, Monic, and Rani.
   \[
   \text{Total crabs} = \text{Bo's crabs} + \text{Monic's crabs} + \text{Rani's crabs} = 40 + 36 + 46
   \]
   - Performing the addition:
   \[
   40 + 36 = 76
   \]
   \[
   76 + 46 = 122
   \]

Thus, the total number of crabs the three have together is ####122####.
