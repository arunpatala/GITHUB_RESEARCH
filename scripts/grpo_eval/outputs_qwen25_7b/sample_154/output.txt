To determine the total number of hours Kimo spends attending classes in one semester, we need to break down his weekly schedule and then multiply by the number of weeks in the semester.

First, let's calculate the number of hours Kimo spends on Mondays, Wednesdays, and Fridays:
- Each of these days, Kimo has 3 classes, each lasting 1 hour.
- Therefore, the total number of hours per day is \(3 \times 1 = 3\) hours.
- Since there are 3 such days in a week, the total for these days is \(3 \times 3 = 9\) hours.

Next, let's calculate the number of hours Kimo spends on Tuesdays and Thursdays:
- Each of these days, Kimo has 2 classes, each lasting 2 hours.
- Therefore, the total number of hours per day is \(2 \times 2 = 4\) hours.
- Since there are 2 such days in a week, the total for these days is \(4 \times 2 = 8\) hours.

Now, we add the hours from both parts of the week to find the total number of hours Kimo spends in class each week:
\[ 9 \text{ hours} + 8 \text{ hours} = 17 \text{ hours per week} \]

Finally, we multiply the weekly total by the number of weeks in the semester (16 weeks):
\[ 17 \text{ hours/week} \times 16 \text{ weeks} = 272 \text{ hours} \]

Thus, the total number of hours Kimo spends attending classes in one semester is ####272.
