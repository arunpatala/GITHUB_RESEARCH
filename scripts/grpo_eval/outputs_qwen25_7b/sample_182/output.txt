     \[
     J = M + 2
     \]

   - Jean's age two years ago was \( J - 2 \).

4. **Additional relationship:**
   - Two years ago, Mark was 5 years older than half of Jean's age at that time:
     \[
     M - 2 = \frac{1}{2}(J - 2) + 5
     \]

   - Substitute \( J \) in the second equation:
     \[
     M - 2 = \frac{1}{2}((M + 2) - 2) + 5
     \]
   - Simplify inside the parentheses:
     \[
     M - 2 = \frac{1}{2}(M) + 5
     \]
   - Multiply both sides by 2 to clear the fraction:
     \[
     2(M - 2) = M + 10
     \]
   - Distribute and simplify:
     \[
     2M - 4 = M + 10
     \]
   - Subtract \( M \) from both sides:
     \[
     M - 4 = 10
     \]
   - Add 4 to both sides:
     \[
     M = 14
     \]

   - Using \( J = M + 2 \):
     \[
     J = 14 + 2 = 16
