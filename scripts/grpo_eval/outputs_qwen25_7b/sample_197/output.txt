To find out how many children live in Soda, we need to subtract the total number of men and women from the total population.

The total population of Soda is 23786 inhabitants. Out of these, 8417 are men and 9092 are women. So, the number of children can be calculated as follows:

\[
\text{Number of children} = \text{Total population} - (\text{Number of men} + \text{Number of women})
\]

Substituting the given values:

\[
\text{Number of children} = 23786 - (8417 + 9092)
\]

First, add the number of men and women:

\[
8417 + 9092 = 17509
\]

Now, subtract this sum from the total population:

\[
23786 - 17509 = 6277
\]

So, the number of children in Soda is 6277. #### 6277
