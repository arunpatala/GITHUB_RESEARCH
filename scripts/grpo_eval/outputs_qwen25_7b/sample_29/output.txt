Let's denote the cost of the boots as \( B \).

According to the problem, the total cost of the two pairs of high heels is five dollars less than the cost of the boots. The first pair of heels costs $33, and the second pair costs twice as much, which means it costs \( 2 \times 33 = 66 \) dollars.

So, the combined cost of the two pairs of heels is:
\[ 33 + 66 = 99 \text{ dollars} \]

This combined cost is five dollars less than the cost of the boots, so we can write the equation:
\[ B - 5 = 99 \]

To find the cost of the boots, we solve for \( B \):
\[ B = 99 + 5 = 104 \]

Thus, the cost of the boots is \( \boxed{104} \). ####104
