To determine how many glasses of water John drinks in a week, we need to break down his daily routine and then sum it up for the entire week.

1. **Daily Routine:**
   - John has a glass of water with breakfast.
   - John has a glass of water with lunch.
   - John has a glass of water with dinner.
   - John has a glass of water before bed.

   Therefore, each weekday, John drinks:
   \[
   4 \text{ glasses of water}
   \]

2. **Weekday Calculation:**
   - There are 5 weekdays in a week.
   - Each weekday, John drinks 4 glasses of water.
   - Total glasses of water on weekdays:
   \[
   5 \text{ days} \times 4 \text{ glasses/day} = 20 \text{ glasses}
   \]

3. **Weekend Routine:**
   - On weekends (Saturday and Sunday), John does not have a glass of water with dinner; instead, he has a soda.
   - Therefore, on each weekend day, John drinks:
   \[
   3 \text{ glasses of water}
   \]

4. **Weekend Calculation:**
   - There are 2 weekend days in a week.
   - Each weekend day, John drinks 3 glasses of water.
   - Total glasses of water on weekends:
   \[
   2 \text{ days} \times 3 \text{ glasses/day} = 6 \text{ glasses}
   \]

5. **Total Weekly Consumption:**
   - Adding the glasses of water from weekdays and weekends:
   \[
   20 \text{ glasses (weekdays)} + 6 \text{ glasses (weekends)} = 26 \text{ glasses}
   \]

Thus, the total number of glasses of water John drinks in a week is ####26####.
