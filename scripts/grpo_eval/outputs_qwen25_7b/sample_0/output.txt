To determine how much Janet makes from selling the remaining duck eggs at the farmers' market, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Calculate the total number of eggs laid per day:**
   \[
   \text{Total eggs per day} = 16
   \]

2. **Determine the number of eggs used by Janet:**
   - Eggs eaten for breakfast each day:
     \[
     \text{Eggs for breakfast} = 3
     \]
   - Eggs used for baking muffins each day:
     \[
     \text{Eggs for muffins} = 4
     \]
   - Total eggs used:
     \[
     \text{Total eggs used} = 3 + 4 = 7
     \]

3. **Calculate the number of eggs remaining after Janet uses some:**
   \[
   \text{Remaining eggs} = \text{Total eggs per day} - \text{Total eggs used} = 16 - 7 = 9
   \]

4. **Determine the revenue from selling the remaining eggs:**
   - Price per egg:
     \[
     \text{Price per egg} = \$2
     \]
   - Revenue from selling the remaining eggs:
     \[
     \text{Revenue} = \text{Remaining eggs} \times \text{Price per egg} = 9 \times 2 = 18
     \]

Thus, Janet makes \(\boxed{18}\) dollars every day at the farmers' market.
