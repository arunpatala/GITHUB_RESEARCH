Let's denote the number of cars that drove through the traffic jam in the first 15 minutes as \( x \).

According to the problem:
- In the first 15 minutes, \( x \) cars drove through.
- In the next 15 minutes, 20 more cars drove through.
- 5 cars took an exit and did not drive through the traffic jam.

The total number of cars originally on the motorway is 30. After the traffic jam, the number of cars that actually drove through the traffic jam can be calculated by subtracting the 5 cars that took an exit from the original number of cars:

\[ \text{Total cars that drove through} = 30 - 5 = 25 \]

This total includes both the cars that drove through in the first 15 minutes and the cars that drove through in the second 15 minutes:

\[ x + 20 = 25 \]

To find \( x \), we solve the equation:

\[ x = 25 - 20 \]
\[ x = 5 \]

So, the number of cars that drove through the traffic jam in the first 15 minutes is 5. #### 5
