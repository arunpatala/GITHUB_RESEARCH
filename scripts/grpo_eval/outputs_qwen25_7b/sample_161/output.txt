To find out how many fish Anakin and Locsin caught in total, we need to first determine how many of each type of fish Locsin caught.

Anakin's catches:
- Starfish: 10
- Sea horses: 6
- Clownfish: 3

Locsin's catches:
- Starfish: \(10 - 5 = 5\)
- Sea horses: \(6 - 3 = 3\)
- Clownfish: \(3 + 2 = 5\)

Next, we calculate the total number of each type of fish caught by both Anakin and Locsin:

Total starfish:
\[ 10 \text{ (Anakin)} + 5 \text{ (Locsin)} = 15 \]

Total sea horses:
\[ 6 \text{ (Anakin)} + 3 \text{ (Locsin)} = 9 \]

Total clownfish:
\[ 3 \text{ (Anakin)} + 5 \text{ (Locsin)} = 8 \]

Finally, we sum up all the fish:
\[ 15 \text{ (starfish)} + 9 \text{ (sea horses)} + 8 \text{ (clownfish)} = 32 \]
