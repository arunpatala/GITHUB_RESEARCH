To determine how many hours John spends taking care of his dogs each week, we can break down the problem into smaller steps.

First, calculate the total time John spends on each dog per day:
\[ \text{Time per dog per day} = 0.5 \text{ hours} \]

Next, find out the total time spent on all 10 dogs per day:
\[ \text{Total time per day} = 10 \text{ dogs} \times 0.5 \text{ hours/dog} = 5 \text{ hours/day} \]

Now, calculate the total time spent on all 10 dogs per week (assuming there are 7 days in a week):
\[ \text{Total time per week} = 5 \text{ hours/day} \times 7 \text{ days/week} = 35 \text{ hours/week} \]

Thus, the total number of hours John spends taking care of the dogs each week is:
\[ \boxed{35} \]
