To determine how much Shawnda made from inflating tires, we need to calculate the total number of tires inflated and then convert that into dollars.

1. **Bicycles:**
   - Each bicycle has 2 tires.
   - 5 people had both tires inflated.
   - Total tires for bicycles: \( 5 \text{ people} \times 2 \text{ tires/person} = 10 \text{ tires} \)

2. **Tricycles:**
   - Each tricycle has 3 tires.
   - 3 people had all their tires inflated.
   - Total tires for tricycles: \( 3 \text{ people} \times 3 \text{ tires/person} = 9 \text{ tires} \)

3. **Unicycle:**
   - A unicycle has 1 tire.
   - 1 person had their tire inflated.
   - Total tires for unicycle: \( 1 \text{ person} \times 1 \text{ tire/person} = 1 \text{ tire} \)

4. **Total Tires Inflated:**
   - Sum of all tires: \( 10 \text{ (bicycles)} + 9 \text{ (tricycles)} + 1 \text{ (unicycle)} = 20 \text{ tires} \)

5. **Cost per Tire:**
   - Each tire costs 25 cents to inflate.
   - Total cost in cents: \( 20 \text{ tires} \times 25 \text{ cents/tire} = 500 \text{ cents} \)

6. **Convert Cents to Dollars:**
   - 100 cents = 1 dollar.
   - Total cost in dollars: \( \frac{500 \text{ cents}}{100 \text{ cents/dollar}} = 5 \text{ dollars} \)

Thus, Shawnda made ####5 dollars that day.
