First, we need to convert the lengths of each type of baked good into feet since the final answer needs to be in feet.

- Rolls: Each roll is 4 inches long. Since there are 12 inches in a foot, each roll is \( \frac{4}{12} = \frac{1}{3} \) feet long.
- Chocolate croissants: Each croissant is 6 inches long, which is \( \frac{6}{12} = \frac{1}{2} \) feet long.
- Baguettes: Each baguette is 2 feet long.

Next, we calculate the total length for each type of baked good:

- Total length of rolls: \( 300 \times \frac{1}{3} = 100 \) feet
- Total length of chocolate croissants: \( 120 \times \frac{1}{2} = 60 \) feet
- Total length of baguettes: \( 60 \times 2 = 120 \) feet

Finally, we add these lengths together to find the total length of all the baked goods:

\[ 100 + 60 + 120 = 280 \]
