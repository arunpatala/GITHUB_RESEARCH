To determine how many Facebook friends James has, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Identify the number of friends Dorothy has:**
   - We know that Charlie has three times as many friends as Dorothy.
   - Charlie has 12 friends.
   - Let \( D \) represent the number of friends Dorothy has.
   - Therefore, \( 3D = 12 \).

2. **Solve for \( D \):**
   \[
   D = \frac{12}{3} = 4
   \]
   So, Dorothy has 4 friends.

3. **Determine the number of friends James has:**
   - James has four times as many friends as Dorothy.
   - Let \( J \) represent the number of friends James has.
   - Therefore, \( J = 4D \).
   - Substitute \( D = 4 \) into the equation:
   \[
   J = 4 \times 4 = 16
   \]

Thus, James has \(\boxed{16}\) Facebook friends.
