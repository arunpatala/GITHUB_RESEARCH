To determine how much shorter the candle will be after burning from 1:00 PM to 5:00 PM, we need to calculate the total time the candle is burning and then use the rate at which it melts.

1. Calculate the total time the candle is burning:
   - From 1:00 PM to 5:00 PM is a span of 4 hours.

2. Determine the rate at which the candle melts:
   - The candle melts by 2 centimeters every hour.

3. Calculate the total amount the candle will melt over the 4-hour period:
   - Since the candle melts 2 centimeters per hour, over 4 hours it will melt \( 2 \text{ cm/hour} \times 4 \text{ hours} = 8 \text{ cm} \).

Thus, the candle will be 8 centimeters shorter after burning from 1:00 PM to 5:00 PM. #### 8
