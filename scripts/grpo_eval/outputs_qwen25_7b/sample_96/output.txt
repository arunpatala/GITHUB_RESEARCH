To find out how many more hours Harry slept than James, we first need to calculate how long James slept. 

James slept \( \frac{2}{3} \) of what Harry slept. Since Harry slept 9 hours, we can calculate James's sleep time as follows:

\[ \text{James's sleep time} = \frac{2}{3} \times 9 \]

Now, let's perform the multiplication:

\[ \frac{2}{3} \times 9 = \frac{2 \times 9}{3} = \frac{18}{3} = 6 \]

So, James slept 6 hours.

Next, we need to find the difference between the amount of sleep Harry and James got:

\[ \text{Difference} = \text{Harry's sleep} - \text{James's sleep} = 9 - 6 = 3 \]

Therefore, Harry slept 3 more hours than James. #### 3
