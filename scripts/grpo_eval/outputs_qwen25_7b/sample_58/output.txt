To find the final price of Stephen's groceries, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Calculate the initial total with the 25% fee:**
   - The original total is $40.00.
   - The 25% fee is calculated as \( 0.25 \times 40 = 10 \) dollars.
   - Adding the 25% fee to the original total gives us \( 40 + 10 = 50 \) dollars.

2. **Add the delivery fees:**
   - The delivery fee is $3.00.
   - Adding the delivery fee to the total from step 1 gives us \( 50 + 3 = 53 \) dollars.

3. **Add the tip:**
   - The tip is $4.00.
   - Adding the tip to the total from step 2 gives us \( 53 + 4 = 57 \) dollars.

Thus, the final price of Stephen's groceries is ####57.
