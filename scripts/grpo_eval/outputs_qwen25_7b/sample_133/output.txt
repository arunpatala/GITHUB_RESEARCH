On Tuesday, Walt walked 6 times the distance he walked on Monday, which is \(6 \times 4 = 24\) miles.

The total distance from Monday to Wednesday is 41 miles. We know that on Monday he walked 4 miles and on Tuesday 24 miles. So, the distance he walked on Wednesday can be calculated by subtracting the distances of Monday and Tuesday from the total:

\[ 41 - (4 + 24) = 41 - 28 = 13 \]

Therefore, Walt walked 13 miles on Wednesday. #### 13
