Let's denote the number of cookies Henry baked last year as \( x \).

This year, he wanted to bake twice as many as last year, which would be \( 2x \). However, he ended up baking 15 more than he intended, so he actually baked \( 2x + 15 \) cookies.

After baking, he dropped 5 cookies, leaving him with \( (2x + 15) - 5 = 2x + 10 \) cookies. According to the problem, this equals 110 cookies:

\[
2x + 10 = 110
\]

To find \( x \), we solve the equation:

\[
2x + 10 = 110
\]

Subtract 10 from both sides:

\[
2x = 100
\]

Divide by 2:

\[
x = 50
\]

So, Henry baked \(\boxed{50}\) cookies last year.
