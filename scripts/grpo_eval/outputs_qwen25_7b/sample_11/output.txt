To find the total cost of the pastries that Toula bought, we need to calculate the cost for each type of pastry separately and then sum them up.

1. **Cost of Donuts:**
   - Toula bought 3 dozen donuts.
   - The cost per dozen is $68.
   - Therefore, the total cost for the donuts is:
     \[
     3 \text{ dozen} \times \$68 \text{ per dozen} = 3 \times 68 = \$204
     \]

2. **Cost of Mini Cupcakes:**
   - Toula bought 2 dozen mini cupcakes.
   - The cost per dozen is $80.
   - Therefore, the total cost for the mini cupcakes is:
     \[
     2 \text{ dozen} \times \$80 \text{ per dozen} = 2 \times 80 = \$160
     \]

3. **Cost of Mini Cheesecakes:**
   - Toula bought 6 dozen mini cheesecakes.
   - The cost per dozen is $55.
   - Therefore, the total cost for the mini cheesecakes is:
     \[
     6 \text{ dozen} \times \$55 \text{ per dozen} = 6 \times 55 = \$330
     \]

Now, we sum up the costs of all the pastries:
\[
\$204 + \$160 + \$330 = \$694
\]
