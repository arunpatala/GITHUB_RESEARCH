To determine how much Mark makes in a week, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Determine the number of sentences edited for each publisher:**
   - Mark edits a total of 1000 sentences each week.
   - He edits an equal number of sentences for both publishers.
   - Therefore, he edits \( \frac{1000}{2} = 500 \) sentences for each publisher.

2. **Calculate the payment from Publisher A:**
   - Publisher A pays 5 cents per sentence.
   - For 500 sentences, the payment from Publisher A is:
     \[
     500 \text{ sentences} \times 5 \text{ cents/sentence} = 2500 \text{ cents}
     \]

3. **Determine the payment rate from Publisher B:**
   - Publisher B pays twice what Publisher A pays.
   - Since Publisher A pays 5 cents per sentence, Publisher B pays:
     \[
     2 \times 5 \text{ cents/sentence} = 10 \text{ cents/sentence}
     \]

4. **Calculate the payment from Publisher B:**
   - For 500 sentences, the payment from Publisher B is:
     \[
     500 \text{ sentences} \times 10 \text{ cents/sentence} = 5000 \text{ cents}
     \]

5. **Calculate the total payment for the week:**
   - The total payment is the sum of the payments from both publishers:
     \[
     2500 \text{ cents} + 5000 \text{ cents} = 7500 \text{ cents}
     \]

Thus, Mark makes \(\boxed{7500}\) cents in a week.
