To solve this problem, we need to follow the sequence of events and calculate the number of each type of flamingo at each step.

1. **Initial Placement (Friday Morning):**
   - Sue's front yard has 18 pink plastic flamingos.

2. **First Adjustment (Saturday Morning):**
   - The neighbors take back one third of the flamingos.
     \[
     \text{Number of flamingos taken back} = \frac{1}{3} \times 18 = 6
     \]
   - These 6 flamingos are then painted white and returned to the yard.
   - Number of pink flamingos remaining:
     \[
     18 - 6 = 12
     \]
   - Number of white flamingos added:
     \[
     6
     \]

3. **Second Adjustment (Sunday Morning):**
   - The neighbors add another 18 pink plastic flamingos.
   - Number of pink flamingos now:
     \[
     12 + 18 = 30
     \]
   - Number of white flamingos remains:
     \[
     6
     \]

4. **Final Count (Sunday Noon):**
   - Number of pink flamingos: 30
   - Number of white flamingos: 6

5. **Difference Calculation:**
   - Difference between the number of pink and white flamingos:
     \[
     30 - 6 = 24
     \]

Thus, the number of more pink plastic flamingos than white plastic flamingos at noon on Sunday is ####24####.
