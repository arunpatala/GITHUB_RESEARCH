Let the original price be \( P \). Given that Kyle bought the book at a 25% discount, he paid 75% of the original price. Therefore, we can write the equation:

\[ 0.75P = 19.50 \]

To find the original price \( P \), we solve for \( P \) by dividing both sides of the equation by 0.75:

\[ P = \frac{19.50}{0.75} \]

Performing the division:

\[ P = 26.00 \]
