First, we need to calculate the total cost of the five cell phones before interest is added. Each phone costs $150, and she bought five of them:

\[ 150 \times 5 = 750 \]

Next, we need to calculate the total interest on the purchase. The interest rate is 2%, so we multiply the total cost by 2% (or 0.02):

\[ 750 \times 0.02 = 15 \]

Now, we add the interest to the original total cost to find the total amount Shiela needs to pay:

\[ 750 + 15 = 765 \]

Since Shiela will pay this amount in 3 monthly installments, we divide the total amount by 3:

\[ \frac{765}{3} = 255 \]
