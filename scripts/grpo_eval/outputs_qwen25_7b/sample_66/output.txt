To determine the total number of people sent by all the schools, we need to consider the composition of the teams and the coaches.

1. **Girls' Basketball Teams:**
   - Each school has one girls' basketball team.
   - Each team consists of 5 players.
   - There are 4 schools.
   - Therefore, the total number of girls' basketball players is:
     \[
     4 \text{ schools} \times 5 \text{ players per school} = 20 \text{ players}
     \]

2. **Boys' Basketball Teams:**
   - Each school has one boys' basketball team.
   - Each team consists of 5 players.
   - There are 4 schools.
   - Therefore, the total number of boys' basketball players is:
     \[
     4 \text{ schools} \times 5 \text{ players per school} = 20 \text{ players}
     \]

3. **Coaches:**
   - Each school sends one coach for each team (both girls' and boys').
   - There are 4 schools.
   - Therefore, the total number of coaches is:
     \[
     4 \text{ schools} \times 2 \text{ coaches per school} = 8 \text{ coaches}
     \]

Now, we sum up all the participants:
\[
20 \text{ girls' players} + 20 \text{ boys' players} + 8 \text{ coaches} = 48 \text{ people}
\]

Thus, the total number of people sent by all the schools is ####48.
