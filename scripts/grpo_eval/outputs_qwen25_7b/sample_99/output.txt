To determine how many potted plants Mary will have remaining, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Calculate the initial number of potted plants:**
   Mary already has 2 potted plants on each of the 40 window ledges.
   \[
   2 \text{ plants/ledge} \times 40 \text{ ledges} = 80 \text{ plants}
   \]

2. **Determine the number of plants given away:**
   Mary plans to give 1 potted plant from each of the 40 window ledges to friends and family.
   \[
   1 \text{ plant/ledge} \times 40 \text{ ledges} = 40 \text{ plants}
   \]

3. **Add the new potted plants received:**
   Mary received 18 new potted plants from her plant nursery.
   \[
   80 \text{ plants} - 40 \text{ plants} + 18 \text{ plants} = 58 \text{ plants}
   \]

Thus, the number of potted plants Mary will remain with is:
\[
\boxed{58}
\]
