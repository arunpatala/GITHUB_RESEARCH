To determine the hospital's profit, we need to calculate both the revenue generated from patients and the cost incurred by the hospital for providing the services.

First, let's calculate the total time spent by all patients in a day:
\[ \text{Total time} = 500 \text{ patients} \times 24 \text{ minutes/patient} = 12000 \text{ minutes} \]

Convert the total time into hours:
\[ \text{Total time in hours} = \frac{12000 \text{ minutes}}{60 \text{ minutes/hour}} = 200 \text{ hours} \]

Next, we calculate the revenue from patients:
\[ \text{Revenue from patients} = 200 \text{ hours} \times \$200/\text{hour} = \$40000 \]

Now, we calculate the cost to the hospital for providing the services:
\[ \text{Cost to the hospital} = 200 \text{ hours} \times \$150/\text{hour} = \$30000 \]

Finally, we find the profit by subtracting the cost from the revenue:
\[ \text{Profit} = \$40000 - \$30000 = \$10000 \]
