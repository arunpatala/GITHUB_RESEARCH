To solve this problem, we need to follow these steps:

1. Calculate the net increase in the number of trees each year.
2. Determine the total number of trees after 10 years.
3. Apply the 30% loss to the total number of trees after 10 years.

**Step 1: Net Increase in Trees Each Year**

- Tom plants 10 trees each year.
- He chops down 2 trees each year.

Net increase per year = \( 10 - 2 = 8 \) trees

**Step 2: Total Number of Trees After 10 Years**

- Initial number of trees: 50
- Net increase over 10 years: \( 8 \times 10 = 80 \)

Total number of trees after 10 years:
\[ 50 + 80 = 130 \]

**Step 3: Applying the 30% Loss**

- 30% of the trees die, so 70% of the trees survive.

Number of trees that survive:
\[ 130 \times 0.70 = 91 \]

#### 91
