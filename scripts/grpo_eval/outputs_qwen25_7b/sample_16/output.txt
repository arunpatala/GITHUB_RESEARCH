To find the total distance covered by each train over the two days, we need to sum up the distances traveled in each direction.

**Day 1:**
- Both trains travel westward for 80 miles.

**Day 2:**
- Both trains travel northward for 150 miles.

Now, let's calculate the total distance for one train:

\[
\text{Total distance} = \text{Distance traveled westward} + \text{Distance traveled northward}
\]

Substituting the given values:

\[
\text{Total distance} = 80 \text{ miles} + 150 \text{ miles} = 230 \text{ miles}
\]

Since both trains follow the same route and cover the same distances, the distance covered by each train is:

#### 230
