To determine how much faster the faster team will win by, we need to calculate the total time taken by each team to complete the 4 by 400 meter relay.

**Faster Team:**
Each member runs 400 meters in exactly 55 seconds.
Since there are 4 members:
\[
4 \times 55 = 220 \text{ seconds}
\]

**Less Well-Rounded Team:**
The first runner runs 400 meters in 60 seconds.
The second runner is 3 seconds faster, so they run in:
\[
60 - 3 = 57 \text{ seconds}
\]
The third runner is another 3 seconds faster, so they run in:
\[
57 - 3 = 54 \text{ seconds}
\]
The fourth runner is yet another 3 seconds faster, so they run in:
\[
54 - 3 = 51 \text{ seconds}
\]
Adding these times together for the less well-rounded team:
\[
60 + 57 + 54 + 51 = 222 \text{ seconds}
\]

**Difference in Time:**
Now, we find the difference between the total times of the two teams:
\[
222 - 220 = 2 \text{ seconds}
\]

Thus, the faster team will win by \boxed{2} seconds.
