To determine how many elves are left, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Initial Number of Elves:**
   Nissa initially hires 60 elves.

2. **First Group of Quitters:**
   A third of the elves quit after children vomit on them.
   \[
   \text{Number of elves who quit} = \frac{1}{3} \times 60 = 20
   \]
   Therefore, the number of elves remaining is:
   \[
   60 - 20 = 40
   \]

3. **Second Group of Quitters:**
   After the first group quits, 10 more elves quit after kids kick their shins.
   \[
   \text{Number of elves who quit} = 10
   \]
   Therefore, the number of elves remaining is:
   \[
   40 - 10 = 30
   \]

Thus, the number of elves left is:
\[
\boxed{30}
\]
