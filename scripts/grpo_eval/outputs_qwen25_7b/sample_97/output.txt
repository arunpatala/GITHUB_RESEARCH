To determine how many tomatoes Freda used, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Understand the volume loss**: When Freda cooks the canned tomatoes into sauce, they lose half their volume. This means that the volume of the sauce is half the volume of the tomatoes before cooking.

2. **Determine the initial volume of tomatoes**: Since the sauce made is 32 ounces and it is half the volume of the tomatoes before cooking, the initial volume of tomatoes must be:
   \[
   32 \text{ ounces} \times 2 = 64 \text{ ounces}
   \]

3. **Calculate the number of cans used**: Each can of tomatoes is 16 ounces. Therefore, the number of cans used is:
   \[
   \frac{64 \text{ ounces}}{16 \text{ ounces per can}} = 4 \text{ cans}
   \]

4. **Determine the number of tomatoes in the cans**: Each can contains 3 tomatoes. Thus, the total number of tomatoes used is:
   \[
   4 \text{ cans} \times 3 \text{ tomatoes per can} = 12 \text{ tomatoes}
   \]

Therefore, the number of tomatoes Freda used is \(\boxed{12}\).
