To determine how much money Tommy raises from selling brownies and cheesecakes, we need to calculate the total revenue from each type of baked good separately and then sum these amounts.

First, let's calculate the revenue from the brownies:
- Tommy sells each brownie slice for $3.
- He sells 43 brownies.

The revenue from brownies is calculated as:
\[
43 \text{ brownies} \times 3 \text{ dollars per brownie} = 129 \text{ dollars}
\]

Next, let's calculate the revenue from the cheesecakes:
- Tommy sells each cheesecake slice for $4.
- He sells 23 slices of cheesecake.

The revenue from cheesecakes is calculated as:
\[
23 \text{ slices} \times 4 \text{ dollars per slice} = 92 \text{ dollars}
\]

Now, we sum the revenues from both types of baked goods to find the total amount of money raised:
\[
129 \text{ dollars} + 92 \text{ dollars} = 221 \text{ dollars}
\]

Thus, the total amount of money Tommy raises is:
\[
\boxed{221}
\]
