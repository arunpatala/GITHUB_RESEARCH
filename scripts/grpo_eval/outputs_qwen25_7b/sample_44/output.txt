To determine Charlie's net profit from selling 20 candles, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Calculate the total revenue from selling 20 candles:**
   - Each candle is sold for $2.00.
   - Total revenue = Number of candles × Price per candle
   \[
   \text{Total revenue} = 20 \times 2 = 40 \text{ dollars}
   \]

2. **Determine the amount of beeswax needed to make 20 candles:**
   - Charlie can make 10 candles from 1 pound of beeswax.
   - To find out how many pounds of beeswax are needed for 20 candles:
   \[
   \text{Pounds of beeswax needed} = \frac{20 \text{ candles}}{10 \text{ candles per pound}} = 2 \text{ pounds}
   \]

3. **Calculate the cost of the beeswax and wicks:**
   - The cost for 1 pound of beeswax and the wicks is $10.00.
   - Therefore, the cost for 2 pounds of beeswax and the wicks is:
   \[
   \text{Total cost} = 2 \times 10 = 20 \text{ dollars}
   \]

4. **Calculate the net profit:**
   - Net profit is calculated by subtracting the total cost from the total revenue.
   \[
   \text{Net profit} = \text{Total revenue} - \text{Total cost} = 40 - 20 = 20 \text{ dollars}
   \]

Thus, Charlie's net profit if he makes and sells 20 candles is ####20.
