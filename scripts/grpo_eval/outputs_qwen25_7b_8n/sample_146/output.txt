To find out how many LEGO pieces Johnny has to pick up, we need to consider each of the sets individually and add their quantities.

1. **First Set**:
   - The first set contains \( 500 \) pieces.

2. **Second Set**:
   - The second set has \( 3 \) times more pieces than the first set.
   - Calculation: 
     \[
     3 \times 500 = 1500 \text{ pieces}
     \]

3. **Third Set**:
   - The third set has \( \frac{1}{4} \) the number of pieces as the first set.
   - Calculation: 
     \[
     \frac{500}{4} = 125 \text{ pieces}
     \]

Now, we sum up the pieces from all three sets:

\[
500 + 1500 + 125 = 2125
\]

Therefore, **2125** is the total number of LEGO pieces Johnny has to pick up.
