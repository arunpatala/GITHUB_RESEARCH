First, we need to calculate the total minimum payment Jessica needs to make every month for all her debts:

- Student loan: $300/month
- Credit card: $200/month
- Mortgage: $500/month

So, the total minimum monthly payment is:
\[ 300 + 200 + 500 = 1000 \text{ dollars/month} \]

Next, if Jessica wants to pay 50% more than the minimum, we need to calculate 50% of $1000 and add it to the minimum payment.

We find 50% of $1000 by:
\[ 0.50 \times 1000 = 500 \text{ dollars} \]

Thus, the additional amount she wants to pay each month is:
\[ 1000 + 500 = 1500 \text{ dollars/month} \]

Now, to find out how much she pays in a year:
\[ 1500 \text{ dollars/month} \times 12 \text{ months/year} = 18000 \text{ dollars/year} \]
