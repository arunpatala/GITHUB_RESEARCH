The pages from each book are:
- Science: 4 pages
- Social Studies: 20 pages
- History: 7 pages
- Geography: 8 pages

Adding these together, we get:
\[
4 + 20 + 7 + 8 = 39 \text{ pages}
\]

On Monday, Solo read 15 pages. Therefore, the remaining pages he needs to read are:
\[
39 - 15 = 24 \text{ pages}
\]

Since Solo has 4 more days to finish reading, we divide the remaining pages by the number of days left:
\[
