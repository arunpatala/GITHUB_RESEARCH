To determine the total number of bananas needed to last for 2 months, we first calculate the monthly requirement for each type of ape:

- **Monkeys**: Need 200 bananas per month.
- **Gorillas**: Need 400 bananas per month.
- **Baboons**: Need 100 bananas per month.

Next, we sum these requirements to find the total number of bananas needed per month:
\[
200 \text{ (monkeys)} + 400 \text{ (gorillas)} + 100 \text{ (baboons)} = 700 \text{ bananas per month}
\]

Since the zookeeper needs to supply enough bananas for 2 months, we multiply the monthly requirement by 2:
\[
700 \text{ bananas/month} \times 2 \text{ months} = 1400 \text{ bananas}
\]

Thus, the zoonkeeper needs to order \boxed{1400} bananas to last for 2 months. #### 1400
