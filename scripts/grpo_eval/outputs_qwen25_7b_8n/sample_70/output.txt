\[ 5 \text{ classes/day} \times 15 \text{ students/class} = 75 \text{ students/day} \]

\[ 75 \text{ students} \times 15 \text{ dollars/student} = 1125 \text{ dollars/day} \]
There are 5 weekdays, so her earnings over these days are:
\[ 1125 \text{ dollars/day} \times 5 \text{ days} = 5625 \text{ dollars} \]

\[ 8 \text{ classes} \times 15 \text{ students/class} = 120 \text{ students} \]
Thus, her earnings for Saturday are:
\[ 120 \text{ students} \times 15 \text{ dollars/student} = 1800 \text{ dollars} \]

### Total Earnings for One Week:
Now we sum the earnings from the weekdays and the weekend:
\[ 5625 \text{ dollars (weekdays)} + 1800 \text{ dollars (Saturday)} = 7425 \text{ dollars} \]

So, the total amount of money Judy makes in one week is:
#### 7425
