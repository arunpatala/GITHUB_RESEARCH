To determine the total number of raspberries on the bush, we need to consider both the clusters and the individual raspberries.

   - There are 6 clusters.
   - Each cluster contains 20 raspberries.

   \[
   6 \text{ clusters} \times 20 \text{ raspberries per cluster} = 120 \text{ raspberries}
   \]

2. **Counting the individual raspberries:**
   - There are 67 individual raspberries.
   
3. **Adding the results from both parts:**
   - Cluster raspberries: 120
   - Individual raspberries: 67

   The total number of raspberries is:
   \[
   120 + 67 = 187
   \]
