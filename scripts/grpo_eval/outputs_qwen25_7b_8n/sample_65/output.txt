To find out how many hours Jim spends on TV and reading in 4 weeks, we need to first calculate his weekly time spent and then multiply by 4.

1. **Time Spent Watching TV per Session:**
   - Jim watches TV for 2 hours each session.

2. **Time Spent Reading per Session:**
   - Jim reads for half as long as he watches TV.
   - Time spent reading = \( \frac{1}{2} \times 2 \) hours = 1 hour.

3. **Total Time Spent per Session:**
   - Total time per session (TV + Reading) = 2 hours + 1 hour = 3 hours.

4. **Total Time Spent per Week (3 Sessions):**
   - Total time per week = 3 sessions \(\times\) 3 hours/session = 9 hours.

5. **Total Time Spent in 4 Weeks:**
   - Total time in 4 weeks = 4 weeks \(\times\) 9 hours/week = 36 hours.
