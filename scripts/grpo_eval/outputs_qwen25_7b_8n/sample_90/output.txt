To determine how many pounds of potato salad Ted needs to bring, we first need to calculate the total amount of potato salad that the adult and child dinosaurs will consume.

1. **Calculate the amount each child will eat:**
   - An adult dinosaur eats 10 lbs of potato salad.
   - A child eats half as much as an adult.
   \[
   \text{Amount a child eats} = \frac{10 \text{ lbs}}{2} = 5 \text{ lbs}
   \]

2. **Calculate the total amount of potato salad eaten by the adults:**
   - There are 20 adult dinosaurs.
   \[
   \text{Total for adults} = 20 \times 10 \text{ lbs} = 200 \text{ lbs}
   \]

3. **Calculate the total amount of potato salad eaten by the children:**
   - There are 5 children.
   \[
   \text{Total for children} = 5 \times 5 \text{ lbs} = 25 \text{ lbs}
   \]

4. **Calculate the total amount of potato salad needed:**
   \[
   \text{Total potato salad} = 200 \text{ lbs} + 25 \text{ lbs} = 225 \text{ lbs}
   \]

Thus, Ted needs to bring \boxed{225} pounds of potato salad to the picnic.
