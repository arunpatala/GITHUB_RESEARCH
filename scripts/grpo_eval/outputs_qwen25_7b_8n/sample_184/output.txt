- Total outcomes: 6
- Favorable outcomes for numbers greater than 3: 3

\[
P(\text{number > 3}) = \frac{\text{Favorable outcomes}}{\text{Total outcomes}} = \frac{3}{6} = \frac{1}{2}
\]

Converting this to a percentage:

\[
P(\text{number > 3}) = \left(\frac{1}{2}\right) \times 100 = 50\%
\]
