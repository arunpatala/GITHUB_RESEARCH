To determine how many times Peter can go to the movies, we need to calculate the total cost of one trip and then find out how many such trips he can afford with his budget.

First, let's add up the cost of one trip:
- Movie ticket: $7
- Popcorn: $7

\[
\text{Total cost per trip} = 7 + 7 = 14 \text{ dollars}
\]

Next, we need to determine how many times Peter can afford this total cost with his budget of 42 dollars. We do this by dividing his total budget by the cost of one trip:

\[
\frac{42}{14} = 3
\]

Therefore, Peter can go to the movies \(\boxed{3}\) times.####
