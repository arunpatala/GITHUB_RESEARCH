To determine the total number of thorns, we'll follow these steps:

1. **Calculate the number of roses:**
   - Dan plants 3 rose bushes.
   - Each rose bush has 25 roses.
   - Therefore, the total number of roses is calculated as:
     \[
     3 \text{ rose bushes} \times 25 \text{ roses per bush} = 75 \text{ roses}
     \]

2. **Calculate the total number of thorns:**
   - Each rose has 8 thorns.
   - We found that there are 75 roses in total.
   - Therefore, the total number of thorns is calculated as:
     \[
     75 \text{ roses} \times 8 \text{ thorns per rose} = 600 \text{ thorns}
     \]

Thus, the total number of thorns is ####600####.
