To determine the total amount of insects needed per day for a flock of ten ducks, we can follow these steps:

1. Calculate the total weekly consumption of insects for the group.
2. Convert this weekly intake into a daily average.

First, let's calculate the total weekly consumption:
Each duck needs 3.5 pounds of insects per week. Thus, for 10 ducks:
\[ 10 \text{ ducks} \times 3.5 \text{ pounds/duck/week} = 35 \text{ pounds/week}. \]

Next, convert this to daily consumption by dividing the weekly total by the number of days in a week (7):
\[ \frac{35 \text{ pounds}}{7 \text{ days}} = 5 \text{ pounds/day}. \]

Therefore, the flock of 10 ducks needs \boxed{5} pounds of insects per day.
