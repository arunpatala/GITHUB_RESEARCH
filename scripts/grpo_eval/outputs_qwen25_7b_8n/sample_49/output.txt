- Each floor has 8 units.

- 3/4 of the building is occupied.
- Total number of units = 120.

Occupied units = (3/4) × Total number of units
Occupied units = (3/4) × 120
Occupied units = 90

Step 3: Find the number of unoccupied units.
- Total number of units = 120
- Occupied units = 90

To find the total number of unoccupied units in the building, we need to follow these steps:

1. Calculate the total number of units in the building.
2. Determine how many of those units are occupied.
3. Subtract the occupied units from the total units to get the unoccupied units.

**Step 1: Calculate the total number of units in the building**

The building has:
- 15 floors
- Each floor has 8 units

Therefore, the total number of units is \( 15 \times 8 = 120 \).

**Step 2: Determine how many units are occupied**

We know that \( \frac{3}{4} \) of the building is occupied. So, we calculate:
\[ \text{Occupied units} = \frac{3}{4} \times 120 \]

Performing the multiplication:
\[ \frac{3}{4} \times 120 = 3 \times (120 / 4) = 3 \times 30 = 90 \]

So, there are 90 occupied units.

**Step 3: Calculate the number of unoccupied units**

To find the number of unoccupied units, we subtract the occupied units from the total units:
\[ \text{Unoccupied units} = 120 - 90 = 30 \]

Thus, the total number of unoccupied units in the building is 30.
