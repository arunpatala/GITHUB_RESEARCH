To calculate how many pineapples John can harvest in a year, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Calculate the total number of pineapples per hectare for one harvest:**
   \[
   \text{Pineapples per hectare} = 100
   \]

2. **Calculate the total number of pineapples over all hectares for one harvest:**
   Since John has 10 hectares:
   \[
   \text{Total pineapples per harvest} = 100 \text{ pineapples/hectare} \times 10 \text{ hectares} = 1000 \text{ pineapples}
   \]

3. **Determine the number of harvests in a year:**
   There are 12 months in a year and John can harvest every 3 months:
   \[
   \text{Number of harvests per year} = \frac{12 \text{ months}}{3 \text{ months/harvest}} = 4 \text{ harvests}
   \]

4. **Calculate the total number of pineapples harvested in a year:**
   \[
   \text{Total pineapples/year} = 1000 \text{ pineapples/harvest} \times 4 \text{ harvests} = 4000 \text{ pineapples}
   \]

Thus, the number of pineapples John can harvest within a year is **4000**. #### 4000
