To find the number of good oranges in the basket, we need to account for all other categories of oranges first: bad, unripe, and sour.

1. **Total oranges**: 25
2. **Bad oranges**: Given as 1.
3. **Unripe oranges**: 20% of 25.
   \[
   \text{Unripe oranges} = 20\% \times 25 = \frac{20}{100} \times 25 = 0.2 \times 25 = 5
   \]
4. **Sour oranges**: Given as 2.

Next, sum up the quantities of bad, unripe, and sour oranges:
\[
\text{Bad + Unripe + Sour} = 1 + 5 + 2 = 8
\]

Subtract the number of bad, unripe, and sour oranges from the total number of oranges to get the number of good oranges:
\[
\text{Good oranges} = 25 - (1 + 5 + 2) = 25 - 8 = 17
\]

Thus, the number of good oranges is \boxed{17}.
