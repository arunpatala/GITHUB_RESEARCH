Let's denote the number of silver coins as \( S \) and the number of gold coins as \( G \).

1. Total number of coins = 110
2. \( G = S + 30 \) (since there are 30 more gold coins than silver coins)

We can set up the equation for the total number of coins as follows:
\[ S + G = 110 \]

Substitute \( G \) from the second equation into the first equation:
\[ S + (S + 30) = 110 \]

Combine like terms:
\[ 2S + 30 = 110 \]

Subtract 30 from both sides to isolate the term with \( S \):
\[ 2S = 80 \]

Next, divide by 2 to solve for \( S \):
\[ S = 40 \]

\[ G = S + 30 = 40 + 30 = 70 \]
