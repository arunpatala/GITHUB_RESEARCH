To determine the total number of doorbell rings, we need to find out how many times each friend pressed the doorbell and then sum these values.

First, we know the first friend pressed the doorbell 20 times.
\[
\text{First friend} = 20 \text{ times}
\]

Next, we calculate how many times the second friend pressed the doorbell. The second friend pressed 1/4 times more than the first friend. This means:
\[
\text{Second friend} = 20 + \frac{1}{4} \times 20 = 20 + 5 = 25 \text{ times}
\]

For the third and fourth friends, we are given that the third friend pressed the doorbell 10 times more than the fourth friend. We know the fourth friend pressed the doorbell 60 times:
\[
\text{Fourth friend} = 60 \text{ times}
\]
\[
\text{Third friend} = 60 + 10 = 70 \text{ times}
\]

Now, we sum the number of times each friend pressed the doorbell:
\[
\text{Total doorbell rings} = 20 (\text{first friend}) + 25 (\text{second friend}) + 70 (\text{third friend}) + 60 (\text{fourth friend})
\]
\[
\text{Total doorbell rings} = 20 + 25 + 70 + 60 = 175
\]

Thus, the total number of doorbell rings is:
\[
\boxed{175}
