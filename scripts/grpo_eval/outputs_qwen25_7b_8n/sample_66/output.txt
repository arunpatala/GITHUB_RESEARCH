3. Each team has 5 players.
4. Each team also has 1 coach.

- The number of coaches in both teams combined is \(1 \text{ coach per team} \times 2 \text{ teams} = 2 \text{ coaches}\).

So for one school:
\[10 \text{ players} + 2 \text{ coaches} = 12 \text{ people}.\]

\[12 \text{ people per school} \times 4 \text{ schools} = 48 \text{ people}.\]
