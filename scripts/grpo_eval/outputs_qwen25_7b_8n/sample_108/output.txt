Let \( x \) be the number of cookies Henry baked last year. He wanted to bake twice as many this year, which is \( 2x \). However, he ended up with 15 more cookies than intended, so he baked \( 2x + 15 \) cookies. After dropping 5 cookies, he has \( 2x + 15 - 5 = 2x + 10 \) cookies left, and we know this number is 110.

We can set up the equation:
\[
2x + 10 = 110
\]

Subtract 10 from both sides:
\[
2x = 100
\]

Divide both sides by 2:
\[
x = 50
\]

Thus, Henry baked \(\boxed{50}\) cookies last year.
