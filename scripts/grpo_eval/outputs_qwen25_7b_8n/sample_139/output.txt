To determine the number of pink gumballs in the candy machine, we start by defining the relationship given in the problem. According to the problem, the number of pink gumballs is 22 more than four times the number of blue gumballs. Let's denote the number of blue gumballs as \( B \) and the number of pink gumballs as \( P \).

We are given:
\[ B = 12 \]

The relationship between the number of pink and blue gumballs can be expressed with the equation:
\[ P = 4B + 22 \]

Now, substituting the value of \( B \):
\[ P = 4(12) + 22 \]

First, calculate \( 4 \times 12 \):
\[ 4 \times 12 = 48 \]

Next, add 22 to the result:
\[ 48 + 22 = 70 \]

Hence, the number of pink gumballs is:
\[ P = 70 \]
