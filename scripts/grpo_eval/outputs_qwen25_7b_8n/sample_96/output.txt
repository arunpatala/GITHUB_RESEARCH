To determine how many more hours Harry slept than James, we start by calculating how long James slept.

1. We know that James slept \(\frac{2}{3}\) of what Harry slept.
2. Harry slept for 9 hours.
3. Therefore, James slept:
   \[
   \frac{2}{3} \times 9 = \frac{2 \times 9}{3} = \frac{18}{3} = 6
   \]
4. Hence, James slept 6 hours.
5. To find out how many more hours Harry slept than James, we subtract the number of hours James slept from the number of hours Harry slept:
   \[
   9 - 6 = 3
   \]
