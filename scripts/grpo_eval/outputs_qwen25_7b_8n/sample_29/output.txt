Let's denote the cost of the boots as \( B \). According to the problem:

- One pair of high heels costs $33.
- The second pair of high heels costs twice as much as the first, which means \( 2 \times 33 = $66 \).

Together, the two pairs of high heels cost:
\[ 33 + 66 = $99 \]

It is given that these two pairs of heels together cost five dollars less than the pair of boots:
\[ 99 = B - 5 \]

To find the cost of the boots (\( B \)), we solve for \( B \):
\[ 99 = B - 5 \]
Adding 5 to both sides:
\[ B = 99 + 5 \]
\[ B = 104 \]

Therefore, the boots cost \( \boxed{104} \) dollars. #### 104
