To determine the total expenditure of Joseph for May and June, we can follow these steps:

1. Identify Joseph's expenditure in May:
   \[
   \text{Expenditure in May} = \$500
   \]

2. Calculate Joseph's expenditure in June by subtracting $60 from his May expenditure:
   \[
   \text{Expenditure in June} = \$500 - \$60 = \$440
   \]

3. Add the expenditures of May and June to find the total expenditure for these two months:
   \[
   \text{Total expenditure} = \$500 + \$440 = \$940
   \]

Thus, the total expenditure for those two months is ####940.

To find Joseph's total expenditure for May and June, we need to calculate his expenditure for each month and then sum them up.

First, we know that Joseph's expenditure in May was $500.

Next, his expenditure in June was $60 less than May. Thus, we can calculate June's expenditure as follows:
\[ \text{June's Expenditure} = \text{May's Expenditure} - 60 \]
\[ \text{June's Expenditure} = 500 - 60 \]
\[ \text{June's Expenditure} = 440 \]

Now, we sum the expenditures of both months to find the total:
\[ \text{Total Expenditure} = \text{May's Expenditure} + \text{June's Expenditure} \]
\[ \text{Total Expenditure} = 500 + 440 \]
\[ \text{Total Expenditure} = 940 \]

Thus, Joseph's total expenditure for May and June is ####940.
