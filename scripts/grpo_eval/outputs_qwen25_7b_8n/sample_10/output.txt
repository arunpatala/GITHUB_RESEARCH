1. **First Month Downloads:**
   - Given: 60 downloads

2. **Second Month Downloads:**
   - The number of downloads is three times the first month.
   \[
   3 \times 60 = 180
   \]

3. **Third Month Downloads:**
   - The number of downloads reduces by 30% from the second month.
   - First, calculate 30% of 180:
   \[
   0.30 \times 180 = 54
   \]
   - Subtract this from 180 to find the reduced number:
   \[
   180 - 54 = 126
   \]

4. **Total Downloads Over Three Months:**
   - Sum the downloads from each month:
   \[
   60 + 180 + 126 = 366
   \]
