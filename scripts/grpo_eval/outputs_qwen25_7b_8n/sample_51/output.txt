To determine how long it takes Tom to get back, we need to follow these steps:

1. Calculate the distance traveled from 1 to 4 PM.
2. Use the distance and the return speed to find the time taken for the return trip.

**Step 1: Calculate the distance traveled from 1 to 4 PM.**
- The ship travels at 10 miles per hour.
- From 1 to 4 PM is a total of 3 hours.
- Distance = Speed × Time
\[ \text{Distance} = 10 \, \text{miles per hour} \times 3 \, \text{hours} = 30 \, \text{miles} \]

**Step 2: Determine the time taken to travel back at 6 miles per hour.**
- The return distance is also 30 miles.
- The speed on the return trip is 6 miles per hour.
- Time = Distance / Speed
\[ \text{Time} = \frac{30 \, \text{miles}}{6 \, \text{miles per hour}} = 5 \, \text{hours} \]

Thus, it takes Tom \boxed{5} hours to get back.
