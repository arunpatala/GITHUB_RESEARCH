To determine how many sheep Toulouse, Charleston, and Seattle have together, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Identify the number of sheep in Seattle:**
   - Seattle has 20 sheep.

2. **Determine the number of sheep in Charleston:**
   - Charleston has 4 times as many sheep as Seattle.
   - Therefore, the number of sheep in Charleston is \( 4 \times 20 = 80 \).

3. **Determine the number of sheep in Toulouse:**
   - Toulouse has twice as many sheep as Charleston.
   - Therefore, the number of sheep in Toulouse is \( 2 \times 80 = 160 \).

4. **Calculate the total number of sheep:**
   - The total number of sheep in Toulouse, Charleston, and Seattle is:
     \[
     160 + 80 + 20 = 260
     \]
