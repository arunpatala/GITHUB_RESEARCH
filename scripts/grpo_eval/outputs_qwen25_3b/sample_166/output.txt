To determine how many times Sandra needs to babysit to make 15 Spanish flans, we can follow these steps:

1. **Calculate the total number of eggs needed:**
   Each Spanish flan requires 3 eggs.
   \[
   \text{Total eggs needed} = 15 \text{ flans} \times 3 \text{ eggs/flan} = 45 \text{ eggs}
   \]

2. **Determine how many times Sandra needs to babysit:**
   Sandra receives 9 eggs each time she babysits.
   \[
   \text{Number of babysitting sessions} = \frac{\text{Total eggs needed}}{\text{Eggs per babysitting session}} = \frac{45 \text{ eggs}}{9 \text{ eggs/session}} = 5 \text{ sessions}
   \]

Thus, Sandra needs to babysit 5 times to gather enough eggs to make 15 Spanish flans. The final answer is:
####5
