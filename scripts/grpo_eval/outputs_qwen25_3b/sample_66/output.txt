To determine the total number of people sent by all four schools to the basketball tournament, we need to consider the following:

1. Each school sends two teams: one girls' team and one boys' team.
2. Each team consists of 5 players.
3. Each school also sends one coach.

Let's break down the calculation step-by-step:

### Players:
- Number of teams per school = 2 (girls' and boys')
- Number of players per team = 5
- Total number of players per school = \(2 \times 5 = 10\)
- Total number of players from all four schools = \(4 \times 10 = 40\)

### Coaches:
- Number of coaches per school = 2 (one for the girls' team and one for the boys' team)
- Total number of coaches from all four schools = \(4 \times 2 = 8\)

### Total People:
- Total number of players + Total number of coaches = \(40 + 8 = 48\)

Thus, the total number of people sent by all four schools is ####48####.
