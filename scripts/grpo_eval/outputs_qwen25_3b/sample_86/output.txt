To determine the total number of times the alarm rang, we need to calculate the ringing duration for each instance and then sum them up.

1. **First Ringing:**
   - The alarm rang 4 times.
   
2. **Second Ringing:**
   - It rang for three times as long as the first time.
   - Duration of the second ringing = \(3 \times 4 = 12\) times.
   
3. **Third Ringing:**
   - It rang for half as long as the second time.
   - Duration of the third ringing = \(\frac{1}{2} \times 12 = 6\) times.

Now, we sum the total number of rings:
\[
4 + 12 + 6 = 22
\]

Thus, the total number of times the alarm rang is \(\boxed{22}\).
