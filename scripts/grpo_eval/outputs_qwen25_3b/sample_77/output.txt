To solve the problem, we need to determine how much laundry Raymond and David do, given the relationships between their amounts of laundry.

1. **Determine how much laundry Sarah does:**
   Sarah does 400 pounds of laundry.

2. **Determine how much laundry Raymond does:**
   According to the problem, Raymond does half as much laundry as Sarah.
   \[
   \text{Laundry done by Raymond} = \frac{1}{2} \times 400 = 200 \text{ pounds}
   \]

3. **Determine how much laundry David does:**
   The problem states that Sarah does 4 times as much laundry as David.
   \[
   \text{Laundry done by Sarah} = 4 \times \text{Laundry done by David}
   \]
   Given that Sarah does 400 pounds:
   \[
   400 = 4 \times \text{Laundry done by David}
   \]
   Solving for the laundry done by David:
   \[
   \text{Laundry done by David} = \frac{400}{4} = 100 \text{ pounds}
   \]

4. **Calculate the difference in the amount of laundry Raymond and David do:**
   \[
   \text{Difference} = \text{Laundry done by Raymond} - \text{Laundry done by David} = 200 - 100 = 100 \text{ pounds}
   \]

Thus, the difference in the amount of laundry Raymond and David do is \(\boxed{100}\).
