To determine the speed Marissa needs to walk the remaining distance of the trail to achieve an average speed of 4 miles per hour, we can follow these steps:

1. **Calculate the total time Marissa has planned for the hike:**
   - She plans to take 1 hour for the first 4 miles.
   - She plans to take another 1 hour for the next 2 miles.
   - Let \( t \) be the time she needs to walk the remaining distance.

   Therefore, the total planned time is:
   \[
   1 + 1 + t = 2 + t \text{ hours}
   \]

2. **Determine the total distance of the trail:**
   - The total distance is 12 miles.

3. **Set up the equation for the average speed:**
   - Average speed is given by the total distance divided by the total time.
   - We want the average speed to be 4 miles per hour.

   Therefore, we set up the equation:
   \[
   \frac{12}{2 + t} = 4
   \]

4. **Solve for \( t \):**
   \[
   12 = 4(2 + t)
   \]
   \[
   12 = 8 + 4t
   \]
   \[
   12 - 8 = 4t
   \]
   \[
   4 = 4t
   \]
   \[
   t = 1 \text{ hour}
   \]

5. **Calculate the remaining distance:**
   - Total distance: 12 miles
   - Distance already covered: 4 miles + 2 miles = 6 miles

   Therefore, the remaining distance is:
   \[
   12 - 6 = 6 \text{ miles}
   \]

6. **Determine the required speed for the remaining distance:**
   - Remaining distance: 6 miles
   - Time available: 1 hour

   Required speed:
   \[
   \text{Speed} = \frac{\text{Distance}}{\text{Time}} = \frac{6 \text{ miles}}{1 \text{ hour}} = 6 \text{ miles per hour}
   \]

Thus, Marissa needs to walk the remaining distance at a speed of \(\boxed{6}\) miles per hour.
