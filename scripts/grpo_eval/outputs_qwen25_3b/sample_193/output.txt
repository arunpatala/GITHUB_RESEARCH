To determine how many more pink plastic flamingos are out than white plastic flamingos at noon on Sunday, we need to follow the sequence of events step by step.

1. **Initial Setup:**
   - On Friday morning, Sue has 18 pink plastic flamingos.
   
2. **Saturday Morning:**
   - The neighbors take back one-third of the flamingos.
   - One-third of 18 is calculated as:
     \[
     \frac{1}{3} \times 18 = 6
     \]
   - Therefore, 6 pink flamingos are taken back.
   - The remaining flamingos are:
     \[
     18 - 6 = 12
     \]
   - The neighbors then paint these 6 flamingos white and place them back out.
   - Now, Sue has:
     - 12 pink flamingos (remaining from Friday)
     - 6 white flamingos (painted from Saturday)

3. **Sunday Morning:**
   - The neighbors add another 18 pink flamingos to the collection.
   - The total number of flamingos now is:
     \[
     12 + 6 + 18 = 36
     \]

4. **Counting Flamingos at Noon on Sunday:**
   - Pink flamingos: 36
   - White flamingos: 6

5. **Difference Calculation:**
   - To find how many more pink flamingos there are compared to white flamingos:
     \[
     36 - 6 = 30
     \]

Thus, at noon on Sunday, there are 30 more pink plastic flamingos than white plastic flamingos. The final answer is:

\boxed{30}
