To determine how many tomatoes Freda used, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Determine the initial volume of tomatoes before cooking:**
   - The final volume of the sauce is 32 ounces.
   - Since the tomatoes lose half their volume when cooked, the initial volume of tomatoes must be twice the final volume.
   \[
   \text{Initial volume of tomatoes} = 32 \times 2 = 64 \text{ ounces}
   \]

2. **Calculate the number of tomatoes:**
   - Each 16-ounce can contains 3 tomatoes.
   - To find out how many 16-ounce cans are needed to get 64 ounces of tomatoes:
   \[
   \text{Number of 16-ounce cans} = \frac{64}{16} = 4 \text{ cans}
   \]
   - Since each can contains 3 tomatoes:
   \[
   \text{Total number of tomatoes} = 4 \times 3 = 12
   \]

Thus, Freda used \(\boxed{12}\) tomatoes.
