To determine the total number of hours Sadie slept throughout the week, we need to break down her sleep schedule day by day and then sum it up.

1. **Monday:**
   Sadie slept for 8 hours.
   \[
   \text{Monday} = 8 \text{ hours}
   \]

2. **Tuesday:**
   Sadie slept 2 hours less than Monday.
   \[
   \text{Tuesday} = 8 - 2 = 6 \text{ hours}
   \]

3. **Wednesday:**
   Sadie also slept 2 hours less than Tuesday.
   \[
   \text{Wednesday} = 6 - 2 = 4 \text{ hours}
   \]

4. **Thursday and Friday:**
   For these two days, Sadie slept 1 hour more than what she slept on Tuesday and Wednesday combined.
   
   First, calculate the total sleep from Tuesday and Wednesday:
   \[
   \text{Total from Tuesday and Wednesday} = 6 + 4 = 10 \text{ hours}
   \]
   
   Then, add 1 hour more:
   \[
   \text{Thursday and Friday} = 10 + 1 = 11 \text{ hours}
   \]

5. **Summing up the total sleep for the week:**
   \[
   \text{Total sleep} = \text{Monday} + \text{Tuesday} + \text{Wednesday} + \text{Thursday and Friday}
   \]
   \[
   \text{Total sleep} = 8 + 6 + 4 + 11 = 29 \text{ hours}
   \]

Thus, the total number of hours Sadie slept throughout the week is:
\boxed{29}
