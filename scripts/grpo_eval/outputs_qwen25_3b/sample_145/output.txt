To determine how many pineapples John can harvest within a year, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Calculate the total number of pineapples per hectare:**
   - John has 100 pineapples per hectare.

2. **Determine the number of hectares John has:**
   - John has 10 hectares.

3. **Calculate the total number of pineapples on all hectares:**
   \[
   100 \text{ pineapples/hectare} \times 10 \text{ hectares} = 1000 \text{ pineapples}
   \]

4. **Determine how many times John can harvest in a year:**
   - John harvests every 3 months.
   - There are 12 months in a year.
   - Number of harvests in a year:
     \[
     \frac{12 \text{ months}}{3 \text{ months/harvest}} = 4 \text{ harvests/year}
     \]

5. **Calculate the total number of pineapples harvested in a year:**
   - Each harvest yields 1000 pineapples.
   - Total pineapples harvested in a year:
     \[
     1000 \text{ pineapples/harvest} \times 4 \text{ harvests/year} = 4000 \text{ pineapples/year}
     \]

Thus, the total number of pineapples John can harvest within a year is \(\boxed{4000}\).
