To determine the total number of fruits brought by Morisette and Kael, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Calculate the number of fruits Morisette brought:**
   - Apples: 5
   - Oranges: 8

2. **Calculate the number of fruits Kael brought:**
   - Apples: Twice the amount Morisette brought.
     \[
     2 \times 5 = 10 \text{ apples}
     \]
   - Oranges: Half the number Morisette brought.
     \[
     \frac{8}{2} = 4 \text{ oranges}
     \]

3. **Sum up all the fruits:**
   - Total apples:
     \[
     5 \text{ (Morisette's apples)} + 10 \text{ (Kael's apples)} = 15 \text{ apples}
     \]
   - Total oranges:
     \[
     8 \text{ (Morisette's oranges)} + 4 \text{ (Kael's oranges)} = 12 \text{ oranges}
     \]

4. **Calculate the total number of fruits:**
   \[
   15 \text{ apples} + 12 \text{ oranges} = 27 \text{ fruits}
   \]

Thus, the total number of fruits they have is \(\boxed{27}\).
