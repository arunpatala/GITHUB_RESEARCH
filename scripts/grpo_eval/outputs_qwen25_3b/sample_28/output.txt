To determine how many miles Henry traveled between his first and second stops, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Identify the total distance of the trip:**
   \[
   \text{Total distance} = 60 \text{ miles}
   \]

2. **Determine the distance of the first stop:**
   \[
   \text{Distance of first stop} = 20 \text{ miles}
   \]

3. **Calculate the remaining distance after the first stop:**
   \[
   \text{Remaining distance after first stop} = 60 \text{ miles} - 20 \text{ miles} = 40 \text{ miles}
   \]

4. **Determine the distance of the second stop:**
   \[
   \text{Second stop is 15 miles before the end of the trip}
   \]
   Since the total trip is 60 miles:
   \[
   \text{Distance of second stop} = 60 \text{ miles} - 15 \text{ miles} = 45 \text{ miles}
   \]

5. **Calculate the distance traveled between the first and second stops:**
   \[
   \text{Distance between first and second stops} = 45 \text{ miles} - 20 \text{ miles} = 25 \text{ miles}
   \]

Thus, the number of miles Henry traveled between his first and second stops is:
\boxed{25000}
