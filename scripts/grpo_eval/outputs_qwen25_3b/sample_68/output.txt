To solve this problem, we need to follow these steps:

1. Determine the number of boys at the school.
2. Calculate the total number of students.
3. Use the ratio of students to teachers to find the number of teachers.

**Step 1: Determine the number of boys.**

Given that there are twice as many boys as girls, and there are 60 girls:
\[ \text{Number of boys} = 2 \times \text{Number of girls} = 2 \times 60 = 120 \]

**Step 2: Calculate the total number of students.**

The total number of students is the sum of the number of boys and the number of girls:
\[ \text{Total number of students} = \text{Number of boys} + \text{Number of girls} = 120 + 60 = 180 \]

**Step 3: Find the number of teachers.**

We know that there are 5 students for every teacher. Therefore, the number of teachers can be calculated by dividing the total number of students by 5:
\[ \text{Number of teachers} = \frac{\text{Total number of students}}{5} = \frac{180}{5} = 36 \]

Thus, the number of teachers is:
####36
