To determine the number of bags sold by the trader, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Calculate the total cost incurred by the trader:**
   - The cost of purchasing one bag of wheat is $20.
   - The cost of transporting one bag of wheat is $2.
   - Therefore, the total cost per bag is:
     \[
     20 + 2 = 22 \text{ dollars}
     \]

2. **Determine the total revenue from selling all the bags:**
   - The trader sells each bag for $30.
   - Let \( n \) be the number of bags sold.

3. **Calculate the total profit:**
   - The total profit is given as $400.
   - Profit can be calculated as total revenue minus total cost.
   - Total revenue from selling \( n \) bags is:
     \[
     30n \text{ dollars}
     \]
   - Total cost for \( n \) bags is:
     \[
     22n \text{ dollars}
     \]
   - Therefore, the profit equation is:
     \[
     30n - 22n = 400
     \]

4. **Simplify the profit equation:**
   \[
   8n = 400
   \]

5. **Solve for \( n \):**
   \[
   n = \frac{400}{8} = 50
   \]

Thus, the trader sold \(\boxed{50}\) bags.
