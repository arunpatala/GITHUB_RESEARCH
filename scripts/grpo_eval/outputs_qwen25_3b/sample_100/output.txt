To determine the total number of doorbell rings, we need to calculate the number of rings each friend pressed and then sum them up.

First, let's find out how many times the second friend pressed the doorbell:
- The first friend pressed the doorbell 20 times.
- The second friend pressed it \( \frac{1}{4} \) times more than the first friend.

Calculate \( \frac{1}{4} \) of 20:
\[
\frac{1}{4} \times 20 = 5
\]

Add this to the initial 20 presses:
\[
20 + 5 = 25
\]

Next, we know the fourth friend pressed the doorbell 60 times. We need to find out how many times the third friend pressed the doorbell:
- The third friend pressed the doorbell 10 times more than the fourth friend.

Calculate the number of presses for the third friend:
\[
60 + 10 = 70
\]

Now, we have the number of presses from all four friends:
- First friend: 20 times
- Second friend: 25 times
- Third friend: 70 times
- Fourth friend: 60 times

Sum these values to get the total number of doorbell rings:
\[
20 + 25 + 70 + 60 = 175
\]

Thus, the total number of doorbell rings is:
\boxed{175}
