To solve this problem, we need to set up equations based on the information given.

Let's denote the ages of the children as follows:
- \( A \) for Amy,
- \( J \) for Jackson,
- \( C \) for Corey,
- \( H \) for James.

From the problem statement, we have the following information:
1. James is 10 years old: \( H = 10 \).
2. James is 1 year younger than Corey: \( C = H + 1 \).
3. Amy is 5 years older than Jackson: \( A = J + 5 \).
4. Amy is 2 years younger than Corey: \( A = C - 2 \).

First, let's use the information about James and Corey:
\[ C = H + 1 = 10 + 1 = 11 \]

Next, using the relationship between Amy and Corey:
\[ A = C - 2 = 11 - 2 = 9 \]

Now, using the relationship between Amy and Jackson:
\[ A = J + 5 \]
Since \( A = 9 \):
\[ 9 = J + 5 \]
Solving for \( J \):
\[ J = 9 - 5 = 4 \]

Thus, Jackson is \(\boxed{4}\) years old.
