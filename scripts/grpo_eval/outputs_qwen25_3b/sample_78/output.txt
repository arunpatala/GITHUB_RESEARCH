To determine how much Vincent saves by buying 18 flowers at the better price, we need to compare the cost of buying 18 flowers in both package sizes and then calculate the savings.

First, let's calculate the cost if Vincent buys 18 flowers in packages of 3:
- Each package of 3 flowers costs $2.50.
- To find out how many packages of 3 flowers are needed for 18 flowers, we divide 18 by 3:
  \[
  \frac{18}{3} = 6
  \]
- Therefore, 6 packages of 3 flowers will be bought.
- The total cost for 6 packages is:
  \[
  6 \times 2.50 = 15.00
  \]

Next, let's calculate the cost if Vincent buys 18 flowers in packages of 2:
- Each package of 2 flowers costs $1.00.
- To find out how many packages of 2 flowers are needed for 18 flowers, we divide 18 by 2:
  \[
  \frac{18}{2} = 9
  \]
- Therefore, 9 packages of 2 flowers will be bought.
- The total cost for 9 packages is:
  \[
  9 \times 1.00 = 9.00
  \]

Now, we compare the two costs:
- Cost of buying 18 flowers in packages of 3: $15.00
- Cost of buying 18 flowers in packages of 2: $9.00

The better price is buying the flowers in packages of 2. The savings by choosing this option is:
\[
15.00 - 9.00 = 6.00
\]

Thus, Vincent saves \(\boxed{6000}\) dollars by buying 18 flowers at the better price.
