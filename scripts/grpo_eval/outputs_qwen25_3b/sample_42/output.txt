To determine how many pieces of apple pie were taken by the guests, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Calculate the total number of pieces of pie initially:**
   Grandma Jones baked 5 apple pies, and each pie was cut into 8 pieces.
   \[
   5 \text{ pies} \times 8 \text{ pieces per pie} = 40 \text{ pieces}
   \]

2. **Determine the number of pieces remaining:**
   At the end of the evening, there were 14 pieces of pie remaining.

3. **Calculate the number of pieces taken by the guests:**
   Subtract the number of remaining pieces from the total number of pieces initially:
   \[
   40 \text{ pieces} - 14 \text{ pieces remaining} = 26 \text{ pieces taken}
   \]

Thus, the number of pieces of pie taken by the guests is \(\boxed{26}\).
