To determine how much money Jimmy has, we start by defining the amount of money Ethel has and then use that information to find out how much Jimmy has.

1. **Identify Ethel's Money:**
   Ethel has \( \$8 \).

2. **Formulate the Relationship Between Jimmy's and Ethel's Money:**
   According to the problem, Jimmy has $2 more than twice the money Ethel has. We can express this relationship with the following equation:
   \[
   \text{Jimmy's money} = 2 \times (\text{Ethel's money}) + 2
   \]

3. **Substitute Ethel's Money into the Equation:**
   Since Ethel has \( \$8 \), we substitute \( 8 \) for Ethel's money in the equation:
   \[
   \text{Jimmy's money} = 2 \times 8 + 2
   \]

4. **Calculate Jimmy's Money:**
   Perform the multiplication and addition:
   \[
   2 \times 8 = 16
   \]
   \[
   16 + 2 = 18
   \]

Therefore, Jimmy has \( \$18 \).

The final answer is:
\[
\boxed{18}
