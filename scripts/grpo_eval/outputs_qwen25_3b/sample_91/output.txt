To determine the total number of pets that Jan, Marcia, and Cindy have, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Determine the number of pets Marcia has:**
   - We know that Cindy has 4 pets.
   - Marcia has 2 more pets than Cindy.
   \[
   \text{Number of pets Marcia has} = 4 + 2 = 6
   \]

2. **Determine the number of pets Jan has:**
   - Jan has three times the number of pets as Marcia.
   \[
   \text{Number of pets Jan has} = 3 \times 6 = 18
   \]

3. **Calculate the total number of pets:**
   - Add the number of pets each person has.
   \[
   \text{Total number of pets} = \text{Pets Jan has} + \text{Pets Marcia has} + \text{Pets Cindy has}
   \]
   \[
   \text{Total number of pets} = 18 + 6 + 4 = 28
   \]

Thus, the total number of pets is \(\boxed{28}\).
