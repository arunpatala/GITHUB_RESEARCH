To determine the number of containers imported on the second day, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Calculate the initial number of vehicles:**
   - Each container has 5 vehicles.
   - There are 2 containers initially.
   \[
   \text{Initial number of vehicles} = 2 \times 5 = 10
   \]

2. **Determine the total number of vehicles after the second day:**
   - The total number of vehicles at the port after the second day is 30.

3. **Calculate the number of additional vehicles added on the second day:**
   \[
   \text{Additional vehicles} = 30 - 10 = 20
   \]

4. **Determine the number of containers needed to add the additional vehicles:**
   - Each container holds 5 vehicles.
   \[
   \text{Number of containers} = \frac{20}{5} = 4
   \]

Thus, the number of containers imported on the second day is \(\boxed{4}\).
