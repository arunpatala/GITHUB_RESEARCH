To determine how many miles Sophia can drive on a single tank of gas, we first need to calculate the total amount of gas in the tank when it is full.

Given:
- Sophia used 4 gallons of gas to refill her tank.
- Her tank holds 12 gallons of gas.

Thus, the total capacity of the tank is 12 gallons.

Next, we know that Sophia has already driven 100 miles with the current amount of gas in the tank. We need to find out how much gas was in the tank before she drove these 100 miles.

Let \( x \) be the amount of gas in the tank before driving 100 miles. Since she used 4 gallons to refill the tank, the amount of gas left in the tank after driving 100 miles is:

\[ 12 - 4 = 8 \text{ gallons} \]

We know that 8 gallons of gas allowed her to drive 100 miles. Therefore, we can set up the following proportion to find out how many miles she can drive per gallon:

\[
\frac{100 \text{ miles}}{8 \text{ gallons}} = \frac{x \text{ miles}}{1 \text{ gallon}}
\]

Solving for \( x \):

\[
x = \frac{100 \text{ miles}}{8 \text{ gallons}} = 12.5 \text{ miles per gallon}
\]

Now, to find out how many miles she can drive on a full tank (12 gallons), we multiply the miles per gallon by the total gallons in the tank:

\[
12.5 \text{ miles per gallon} \times 12 \text{ gallons} = 150 \text{ miles}
\]

Therefore, the number of miles Sophia can drive on a single tank of gas is:

\(\boxed{150}\)
