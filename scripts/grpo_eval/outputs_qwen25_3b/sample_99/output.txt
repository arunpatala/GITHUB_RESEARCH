To determine how many potted plants Mary will have left after giving some away, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Calculate the total number of potted plants Mary currently has:**
   - Mary has 2 potted plants on each of the 40 window ledges.
   \[
   2 \text{ plants/ledge} \times 40 \text{ ledges} = 80 \text{ plants}
   \]

2. **Determine the number of potted plants Mary will give away:**
   - Mary plans to give 1 potted plant from each of the 40 window ledges.
   \[
   1 \text{ plant/ledge} \times 40 \text{ ledges} = 40 \text{ plants}
   \]

3. **Calculate the number of potted plants Mary will have left:**
   - Subtract the number of plants given away from the total number of plants she currently has.
   \[
   80 \text{ plants} - 40 \text{ plants} = 40 \text{ plants}
   \]

Thus, the number of potted plants Mary will remain with is:
####40
