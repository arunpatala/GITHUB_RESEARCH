To determine how much each friend paid, we need to calculate the total expenses and then divide by three.

First, let's find the total cost of the tickets:
\[ \text{Total ticket cost} = \$20.25 \]

Next, we calculate the cost of the food. The friends spent $4.50 less on food than on the tickets:
\[ \text{Food cost} = \$20.25 - \$4.50 = \$15.75 \]

Now, we add the cost of the food to the total ticket cost to get the total expenses:
\[ \text{Total expenses} = \$20.25 + \$15.75 = \$36.00 \]

They also spent money on two different rides, each costing $33:
\[ \text{Ride cost per ride} = \$33 \]
\[ \text{Total ride cost} = 2 \times \$33 = \$66 \]

Adding the ride cost to the total expenses gives us the overall total cost:
\[ \text{Overall total cost} = \$36.00 + \$66.00 = \$102.00 \]

Since the total cost is to be split evenly among the three friends, we divide the overall total cost by 3:
\[ \text{Cost per person} = \frac{\$102.00}{3} = \$34.00 \]

Thus, each friend paid:
\boxed{34}
