- Kelly does this 34 times.
- Therefore, the total distance Kelly ran is \(200 + 34 \times 120 = 200 + 4080 = 4280\) yards.

- Blake ran 3000 yards.
- Kelly ran 4280 yards.

\[ 4280 - 3000 = 1280 \]
