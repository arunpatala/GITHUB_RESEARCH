To determine how many times Peter can go to the movies with his $42, we first need to calculate the total cost per visit.

The cost of one movie ticket is $7.
The cost of one popcorn is $7.

So, the total cost per visit is:
\[ 7 + 7 = 14 \text{ dollars} \]

Next, we need to find out how many visits Peter can make with his $42. We do this by dividing his total money by the cost per visit:
\[ \frac{42}{14} = 3 \]
