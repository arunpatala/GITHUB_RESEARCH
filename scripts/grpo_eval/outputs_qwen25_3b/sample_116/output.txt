To solve this problem, we need to determine the number of teaspoons of sugar Katy used based on the given ratio and total amount.

The ratio of teaspoons of sugar to cups of water is 7:13. This means for every 7 parts of sugar, there are 13 parts of water. The total number of parts (teaspoons of sugar + cups of water) is therefore \(7 + 13 = 20\).

Given that the total amount of sugar and water combined is 120 teaspoons, we can set up the following proportion to find out how many parts correspond to the teaspoons of sugar:

\[
\frac{7}{20} \text{ of the total amount} = \text{amount of sugar}
\]

We know the total amount is 120 teaspoons, so we calculate the amount of sugar as follows:

\[
\text{Amount of sugar} = \left(\frac{7}{20}\right) \times 120
\]

First, perform the multiplication:

\[
\left(\frac{7}{20}\right) \times 120 = \frac{7 \times 120}{20} = \frac{840}{20} = 42
\]
