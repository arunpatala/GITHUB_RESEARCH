To solve this problem, we first need to convert the length of the wire from feet to inches because the length of each piece is given in inches.

1 foot = 12 inches

So, 4 feet = 4 * 12 inches = 48 inches.

Each piece of wire is 6 inches long.

Now, to find out how many pieces Tracy obtained, we divide the total length of the wire by the length of each piece:

48 inches / 6 inches per piece = 8 pieces

Therefore, the answer is ####8.
