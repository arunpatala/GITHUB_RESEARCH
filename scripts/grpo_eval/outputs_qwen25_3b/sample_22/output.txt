To determine how many DVDs Billy sold on Tuesday, we need to break down the sales by customer group and sum them up.

1. **First Group of Customers:**
   - Number of customers: 3
   - Each buys 1 DVD
   - Total DVDs sold in this group: \( 3 \times 1 = 3 \)

2. **Second Group of Customers:**
   - Number of customers: 2
   - Each buys 2 DVDs
   - Total DVDs sold in this group: \( 2 \times 2 = 4 \)

3. **Third Group of Customers:**
   - Number of customers: 3
   - Each does not buy any DVDs
   - Total DVDs sold in this group: \( 3 \times 0 = 0 \)

Now, we add the total DVDs sold from all groups:
\[ 3 + 4 + 0 = 7 \]

Thus, the total number of DVDs Billy sold on Tuesday is \(\boxed{7}\).
