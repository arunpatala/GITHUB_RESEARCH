To determine how many Lego sets John still has, we need to follow these steps:

1. Calculate the total amount of money John earns from selling his Lego sets.
2. Determine the total cost of the video games he buys.
3. Use the remaining money to find out how much money John had initially.
4. Subtract the number of Lego sets he sold from the initial number of Lego sets to find out how many he still has.

Let's start with step 1:
John has 13 Lego sets and sells each for $15.
\[
\text{Total money earned} = 13 \times 15 = 195 \text{ dollars}
\]

Next, for step 2:
John buys 8 video games at $20 each.
\[
\text{Total cost of video games} = 8 \times 20 = 160 \text{ dollars}
\]

For step 3:
John has $5 left after buying the video games.
\[
\text{Initial money} = \text{Money earned} + \text{Remaining money} = 195 + 5 = 200 \text{ dollars}
\]

Finally, for step 4:
John initially had 13 Lego sets. After selling all of them, he has no Lego sets left.
\[
\text{Number of Lego sets left} = 13 - 13 = 0
\]

Thus, the number of Lego sets John still has is:
\boxed{0}
