Let's denote the original price by \( P \). According to the problem, Kyle paid $19.50 which is 75% of the original price. We can write this relationship as:
\[ 0.75P = 19.50 \]

To find \( P \), we need to solve for \( P \) by dividing both sides of the equation by 0.75:
\[ P = \frac{19.50}{0.75} \]

Now, let's perform the division:
\[ P = \frac{19.50}{0.75} = 26 \]
