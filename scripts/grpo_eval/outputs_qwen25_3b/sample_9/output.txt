To determine Eliza's total earnings for the week, we need to break down her pay into two parts: her regular pay for the first 40 hours and her overtime pay for the additional hours.

First, calculate Eliza's regular pay:
- Her regular hourly rate is $10.
- For the first 40 hours, she earns at this rate.

\[
\text{Regular pay} = 40 \text{ hours} \times \$10/\text{hour} = \$400
\]

Next, calculate her overtime pay:
- Overtime pay is calculated at 1.2 times her regular hourly rate.
- Eliza worked 5 hours of overtime (since she worked 45 hours in total).

\[
\text{Overtime hourly rate} = 1.2 \times \$10 = \$12/\text{hour}
\]

\[
\text{Overtime pay} = 5 \text{ hours} \times \$12/\text{hour} = \$60
\]

Now, add the regular pay and the overtime pay to find her total earnings for the week:

\[
\text{Total earnings} = \text{Regular pay} + \text{Overtime pay} = \$400 + \$60 = \$460
\]

Thus, Eliza's total earnings for the week are ####460.
