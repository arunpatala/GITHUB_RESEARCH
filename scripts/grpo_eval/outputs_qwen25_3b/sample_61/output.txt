To determine the total amount Janet paid, we need to follow these steps:

1. Calculate the cost of the brooch materials.
2. Add the cost of the jeweler's construction.
3. Calculate the insurance cost (10% of the total cost from steps 1 and 2).
4. Sum up all the costs.

Let's start with the given values:
- Cost of materials: $500
- Cost of construction by the jeweler: $800

First, calculate the total cost before insurance:
\[
500 + 800 = 1300
\]

Next, calculate the insurance cost, which is 10% of the total cost:
\[
0.10 \times 1300 = 130
\]

Finally, add the insurance cost to the total cost:
\[
1300 + 130 = 1430
\]

Thus, the total amount Janet paid is:
\boxed{1430}
