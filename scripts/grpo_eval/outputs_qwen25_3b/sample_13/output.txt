1. **After selling at the green house:**

   \[
   \text{Vacuum cleaners left after green house} = \frac{2}{3}x
   \]

2. **After selling 2 more at the red house:**
   She then sold 2 more vacuum cleaners at the red house.
   \[
   \text{Vacuum cleaners left after red house} = \frac{2}{3}x - 2
   \]

3. **After selling half of what was left at the orange house:**
   She then sold half of the remaining vacuum cleaners at the orange house.
   \[
   \text{Vacuum cleaners left after orange house} = \frac{1}{2} \left( \frac{2}{3}x - 2 \right)
   \]

We know that after all these sales, Melanie has 5 vacuum cleaners left:
\[
\frac{1}{2} \left( \frac{2}{3}x - 2 \right) = 5
\]

4. **Solving for \( x \):**
   First, multiply both sides by 2 to clear the fraction:
   \[
   \frac{2}{3}x - 2 = 10
   \]
   
   Next, add 2 to both sides:
   \[
   \frac{2}{3}x = 12
   \]
