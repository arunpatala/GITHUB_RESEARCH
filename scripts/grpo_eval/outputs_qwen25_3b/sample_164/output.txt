To determine how much more money Jackie will have if she hires an accountant instead of doing her taxes herself, we need to calculate the lost income from doing fewer hours of freelance work and then compare it to the cost of hiring the accountant.

First, let's define the variables:
- Let \( h \) be the number of hours Jackie would normally work.
- The hourly rate for freelance work is $35.

If Jackie does her own taxes, she loses 3 hours of freelance work. Therefore, the lost income can be calculated as:
\[
\text{Lost Income} = 3 \times 35 = 105
\]

Next, we compare this lost income to the cost of hiring the accountant. The accountant charges $90.

Now, we find the difference between the lost income and the cost of hiring the accountant:
\[
\text{Additional Money} = \text{Lost Income} - \text{Cost of Hiring Accountant}
\]
\[
\text{Additional Money} = 105 - 90 = 15
\]

Thus, if Jackie hires the accountant, she will have \(\boxed{15}\) dollars more than if she did her taxes herself.
