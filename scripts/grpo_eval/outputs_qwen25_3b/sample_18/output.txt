To determine how many dozens of eggs Claire will consume in 4 weeks, we can break down the problem into smaller steps.

1. **Determine the number of eggs Claire uses per day:**
   Claire uses 3 eggs each day.

2. **Calculate the number of days in 4 weeks:**
   There are 7 days in a week, so:
   \[
   4 \text{ weeks} \times 7 \text{ days/week} = 28 \text{ days}
   \]

3. **Calculate the total number of eggs used in 4 weeks:**
   Since Claire uses 3 eggs each day for 28 days:
   \[
   3 \text{ eggs/day} \times 28 \text{ days} = 84 \text{ eggs}
   \]

4. **Convert the total number of eggs to dozens:**
   There are 12 eggs in one dozen. To find out how many dozens 84 eggs make:
   \[
   \frac{84 \text{ eggs}}{12 \text{ eggs/dozen}} = 7 \text{ dozens}
   \]

Thus, Claire will eat \(\boxed{7}\) dozens of eggs in 4 weeks.
