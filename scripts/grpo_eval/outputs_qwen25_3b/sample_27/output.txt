To determine how much Cynthia will spend on ice cream over 60 days, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Calculate the total number of servings Cynthia needs for 60 days:**
   \[
   \text{Total servings needed} = 60 \text{ days} \times 1 \text{ serving/day} = 60 \text{ servings}
   \]

2. **Determine how many cartons of ice cream Cynthia needs:**
   Each carton contains 15 servings.
   \[
   \text{Number of cartons needed} = \frac{60 \text{ servings}}{15 \text{ servings/carton}} = 4 \text{ cartons}
   \]

3. **Calculate the total cost for 4 cartons:**
   Each carton costs $4.00.
   \[
   \text{Total cost} = 4 \text{ cartons} \times 4 \text{ dollars/carton} = 16 \text{ dollars}
   \]

Thus, the total amount Cynthia will spend on ice cream over 60 days is:
\boxed{1600}
