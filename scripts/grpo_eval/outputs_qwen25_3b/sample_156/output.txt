To determine the total number of bananas the zookeeper needs to order to last for 2 months, we first calculate the monthly requirement for each type of ape:

- Monkeys: 200 bananas per month.
- Gorillas: 400 bananas per month.
- Baboons: 100 bananas per month.

Next, we find the total monthly requirement by summing these amounts:
\[
200 \text{ (monkeys)} + 400 \text{ (gorillas)} + 100 \text{ (baboons)} = 700 \text{ bananas per month}
\]

Since the zookeeper orders bananas every 2 months, we multiply the total monthly requirement by 2:
\[
700 \text{ bananas per month} \times 2 \text{ months} = 1400 \text{ bananas}
\]

Thus, the zookeeper needs to order \(\boxed{1400}\) bananas to last for 2 months.
