To solve this problem, we first calculate the total weight of the comic books Uriah removes.

Each comic book weighs \( \frac{1}{4} \) pound, and he removes 30 comic books. Therefore, the total weight of the comic books removed is:
\[
30 \times \frac{1}{4} = \frac{30}{4} = 7.5 \text{ pounds}
\]

Next, we determine the remaining weight that needs to be removed from his book bag. Since he needs to remove a total of 15 pounds, the remaining weight to be removed is:
\[
15 - 7.5 = 7.5 \text{ pounds}
\]

Now, we need to find out how many toys he needs to remove. Each toy weighs \( \frac{1}{2} \) pound. Let \( t \) represent the number of toys he needs to remove. The equation representing the total weight of the toys removed is:
\[
t \times \frac{1}{2} = 7.5
\]

To find \( t \), we solve the equation by multiplying both sides by 2:
\[
t = 7.5 \times 2 = 15
\]

Thus, Uriah needs to remove \(\boxed{15}\) toys.
