To find the total cost of the pastries Toula bought, we need to calculate the cost for each type of pastry separately and then sum them up.

1. **Donuts:**
   - Toula bought 3 dozen donuts.
   - Each dozen costs $68.
   \[
   \text{Total cost for donuts} = 3 \times 68 = 204
   \]

2. **Mini cupcakes:**
   - Toula bought 2 dozen mini cupcakes.
   - Each dozen costs $80.
   \[
   \text{Total cost for mini cupcakes} = 2 \times 80 = 160
   \]

3. **Mini cheesecakes:**
   - Toula bought 6 dozen mini cheesecakes.
   - Each dozen costs $55.
   \[
   \text{Total cost for mini cheesecakes} = 6 \times 55 = 330
   \]

Now, we add up the costs of all the pastries:
\[
\text{Total cost} = 204 + 160 + 330 = 694
\]

Thus, the total cost is \(\boxed{694}\).
