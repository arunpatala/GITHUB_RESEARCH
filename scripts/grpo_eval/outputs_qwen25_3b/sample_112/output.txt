To determine how many times the whiteboard is cleaned in a day, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Calculate the total number of lessons per day:**
   - There are 4 teachers.
   - Each teacher has 2 lessons per day.
   \[
   \text{Total lessons per day} = 4 \text{ teachers} \times 2 \text{ lessons/teacher} = 8 \text{ lessons}
   \]

2. **Determine the number of times the whiteboard is cleaned per lesson:**
   - The whiteboard is cleaned 3 times per lesson.

3. **Calculate the total number of cleanings per day:**
   - Multiply the total number of lessons by the number of cleanings per lesson.
   \[
   \text{Total cleanings per day} = 8 \text{ lessons} \times 3 \text{ cleanings/lesson} = 24 \text{ cleanings}
   \]

Thus, the whiteboard is cleaned \(\boxed{24}\) times in a day.
