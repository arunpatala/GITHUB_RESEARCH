To solve this problem, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Calculate the number of boys:**
   Given that \( \frac{2}{5} \) of the 200 students are boys:
   \[
   \text{Number of boys} = \frac{2}{5} \times 200 = \frac{400}{5} = 80
   \]

2. **Calculate the number of girls:**
   Since the total number of students is 200 and there are 80 boys, the number of girls is:
   \[
   \text{Number of girls} = 200 - 80 = 120
   \]

3. **Calculate the number of girls in the Girl Scout:**
   Given that \( \frac{2}{3} \) of the girls are in the Girl Scout:
   \[
   \text{Number of girls in the Girl Scout} = \frac{2}{3} \times 120 = \frac{240}{3} = 80
   \]

4. **Calculate the number of girls not in the Girl Scout:**
   The number of girls not in the Girl Scout is the total number of girls minus the number of girls in the Girl Scout:
   \[
   \text{Number of girls not in the Girl Scout} = 120 - 80 = 40
   \]

Thus, the number of girls not in the Girl Scout is \(\boxed{40}\).
