To find the combined weight of Grace and Alex, we need to follow these steps:

1. Determine Alex's weight.
2. Add Grace's weight and Alex's weight together.

First, let's calculate Alex's weight. According to the problem, Alex weighs 2 pounds less than 4 times what Grace weighs. Grace weighs 125 pounds. So, we can express Alex's weight as:
\[ \text{Alex's weight} = 4 \times \text{Grace's weight} - 2 \]
Substituting Grace's weight into the equation:
\[ \text{Alex's weight} = 4 \times 125 - 2 \]
\[ \text{Alex's weight} = 500 - 2 \]
\[ \text{Alex's weight} = 498 \]

Now that we know Alex's weight is 498 pounds, we can find their combined weight by adding Grace's weight and Alex's weight together:
\[ \text{Combined weight} = \text{Grace's weight} + \text{Alex's weight} \]
\[ \text{Combined weight} = 125 + 498 \]
\[ \text{Combined weight} = 623 \]

Therefore, the combined weight of Grace and Alex is \(\boxed{623}\).
