To determine the cost of 8 pens, we first need to find the cost of one pen. We know that:

- A pen costs as much as a pencil and an eraser combined.
- A pencil costs $1.20.
- An eraser costs $0.30.

First, calculate the combined cost of one pencil and one eraser:
\[
\text{Cost of one pencil} + \text{Cost of one eraser} = \$1.20 + \$0.30 = \$1.50
\]

Since a pen costs as much as this combined amount, the cost of one pen is:
\[
\$1.50
\]

Next, we need to find the total cost for 8 pens:
\[
\text{Cost of one pen} \times 8 = \$1.50 \times 8 = \$12.00
\]

Thus, the cost of 8 pens is:
\boxed{1200}
