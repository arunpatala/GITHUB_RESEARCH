To determine how much Terry spends on yogurt over 30 days, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Calculate the total number of yogurts Terry eats in 30 days:**
   \[
   \text{Yogurts per day} = 2
   \]
   \[
   \text{Days} = 30
   \]
   \[
   \text{Total yogurts} = 2 \times 30 = 60
   \]

2. **Determine the number of sets of 4 yogurts that Terry needs to buy:**
   \[
   \text{Yogurts per set} = 4
   \]
   \[
   \text{Total yogurts} = 60
   \]
   \[
   \text{Number of sets} = \frac{60}{4} = 15
   \]

3. **Calculate the cost for one set of 4 yogurts:**
   \[
   \text{Cost per set} = \$5.00
   \]

4. **Calculate the total cost for 15 sets:**
   \[
   \text{Total cost} = 15 \times 5.00 = 75.00
   \]

Thus, Terry spends \(\boxed{75}\) dollars on yogurt over 30 days.
