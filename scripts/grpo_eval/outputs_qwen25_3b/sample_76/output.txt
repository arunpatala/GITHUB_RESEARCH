To determine how many bags of dog food Cecilia will use in the first year, we need to calculate the total amount of dog food required for the first 180 days and then for the rest of the puppy's life within the first year.

First, let's calculate the amount of dog food needed for the first 180 days:
- The puppy needs 1 cup of dog food per day for the first 180 days.
- Therefore, the total amount of dog food for the first 180 days is:
  \[
  1 \text{ cup/day} \times 180 \text{ days} = 180 \text{ cups}
  \]

Next, we need to determine how much dog food is left for the rest of the puppy's life within the first year. Since there are 365 days in a year, the remaining days are:
\[
365 \text{ days} - 180 \text{ days} = 185 \text{ days}
\]

For the remaining 185 days, the puppy will need 2 cups of dog food per day:
\[
2 \text{ cups/day} \times 185 \text{ days} = 370 \text{ cups}
\]

Now, we add the total amount of dog food needed for both periods:
\[
180 \text{ cups} + 370 \text{ cups} = 550 \text{ cups}
\]

Each bag of dog food contains 110 cups. To find out how many bags Cecilia will need, we divide the total cups by the number of cups per bag:
\[
\frac{550 \text{ cups}}{110 \text{ cups/bag}} = 5 \text{ bags}
\]

Thus, the number of bags of dog food Cecilia will use in the first year is:
\boxed{5}
