To solve the problem, we need to determine the number of pink gumballs in the candy machine given that there are 12 blue gumballs and that there are 22 more than four times the number of pink gumballs as there are blue gumballs.

Let's denote:
- \( P \) as the number of pink gumballs.
- \( B \) as the number of blue gumballs.

From the problem statement, we know:
\[ B = 12 \]

The relationship between the number of pink gumballs and blue gumballs is given by:
\[ P = 4B + 22 \]

Substituting the value of \( B \):
\[ P = 4(12) + 22 \]

First, calculate \( 4 \times 12 \):
\[ 4 \times 12 = 48 \]

Next, add 22 to 48:
\[ 48 + 22 = 70 \]

Thus, the number of pink gumballs is:
\[ P = 70 \]

Therefore, the final answer is:
\[
boxed{70}
\]
