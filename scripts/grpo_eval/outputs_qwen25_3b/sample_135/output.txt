To determine how many video games Bobby has, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Determine the number of video games Brian has after losing some:**
   - Initially, Brian has 20 video games.
   - Brian lost 5 video games.
   - Therefore, Brian now has:
     \[
     20 - 5 = 15 \text{ video games}
     \]

2. **Calculate how many video games Bobby has based on Brian's current number of games:**
   - According to the problem, Bobby has 5 fewer than 3 times as many video games as Brian.
   - Let \( B \) represent the number of video games Brian has.
   - Then, Bobby has \( 3B - 5 \) video games.
   - Since Brian now has 15 video games (\( B = 15 \)):
     \[
     3B - 5 = 3(15) - 5
     \]
   - Calculate the expression:
     \[
     3(15) = 45
     \]
     \[
     45 - 5 = 40
     \]

Thus, Bobby has \(\boxed{40}\) video games.
