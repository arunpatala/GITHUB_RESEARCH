To solve this problem, we need to determine the age of Raymond when his son was born and then find out how many years ago that birth occurred.

1. **Determine Raymond's current age:**
   - We know Samantha is currently 31 years old.
   - Raymond was born 6 years before Samantha.
   - Therefore, Raymond is \( 31 + 6 = 37 \) years old.

2. **Calculate Raymond's age when his son was born:**
   - Raymond had a son at the age of 23.
   - Thus, Raymond's son was born when Raymond was 23 years old.

3. **Determine how many years ago Raymond's son was born:**
   - Since Raymond is currently 37 years old and his son was born when he was 23, the number of years since his son was born is:
     \[
     37 - 23 = 14
     \]

Therefore, Raymond's son was born 14 years ago. The final answer is ####14.
