To determine how many stickers Charlie has left, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Initial Stickers**: Charlie starts with 10 stickers.
2. **Stickers Bought**: He buys 21 more stickers.
   \[
   10 + 21 = 31
   \]
3. **Stickers Received for Birthday**: He receives 23 more stickers.
   \[
   31 + 23 = 54
   \]
4. **Stickers Given Away**: Charlie gives 9 stickers to his sister.
   \[
   54 - 9 = 45
   \]
5. **Stickers Used for Greeting Card**: He uses 28 stickers to decorate a greeting card.
   \[
   45 - 28 = 17
   \]
