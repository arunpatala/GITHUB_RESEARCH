To determine how much Kylar needs to pay for 16 glasses, we first need to understand the pricing structure and then calculate the total cost.

1. **Identify the prices:**
   - The first glass costs $5.
   - Every second glass (i.e., the 2nd, 4th, 6th, etc.) costs 60% of $5.

2. **Calculate the cost of every second glass:**
   \[
   60\% \text{ of } \$5 = 0.60 \times 5 = \$3
   \]

3. **Determine the pattern in the purchase:**
   - For every two glasses, the cost is:
     \[
     \$5 + \$3 = \$8
     \]

4. **Calculate the total cost for 16 glasses:**
   - Since 16 glasses can be grouped into 8 pairs of glasses:
     \[
     8 \text{ pairs} \times \$8 \text{ per pair} = 8 \times 8 = \$64
     \]

Thus, the total amount Kylar needs to pay for 16 glasses is:
\boxed{64####}
