To determine how many hours Jim spends on TV and reading in 4 weeks, we need to break down the problem step by step.

1. **Calculate the time spent on TV and reading per session:**
   - Jim spends 2 hours watching TV.
   - He reads for half as long as he watches TV.
     \[
     \text{Reading time} = \frac{2}{2} = 1 \text{ hour}
     \]
   - Total time spent on TV and reading per session:
     \[
     \text{Total time per session} = 2 \text{ hours (TV)} + 1 \text{ hour (reading)} = 3 \text{ hours}
     \]

2. **Calculate the total time spent per week:**
   - Jim does this 3 times a week.
     \[
     \text{Total time per week} = 3 \text{ sessions/week} \times 3 \text{ hours/session} = 9 \text{ hours/week}
     \]

3. **Calculate the total time spent over 4 weeks:**
   - Multiply the weekly total by 4:
     \[
     \text{Total time over 4 weeks} = 9 \text{ hours/week} \times 4 \text{ weeks} = 36 \text{ hours}
     \]

Thus, the total number of hours Jim spends on TV and reading in 4 weeks is \(\boxed{36}\).
