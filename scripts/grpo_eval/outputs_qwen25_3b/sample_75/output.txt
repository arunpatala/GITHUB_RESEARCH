To find the average square footage of a level in Luke's 4-level sandcastle, we first need to determine the square footage for each level.

Given:
- The top (4th) level has a square footage of 16.
- Each subsequent level has half the square footage of the level below it.

Let's denote the square footage of each level as follows:
- Level 1: \( A_1 \)
- Level 2: \( A_2 \)
- Level 3: \( A_3 \)
- Level 4: \( A_4 = 16 \)

From the problem statement, we know:
\[ A_4 = 16 \]

Since each level has half the square footage of the level below it:
\[ A_3 = 2 \times A_4 = 2 \times 16 = 32 \]
\[ A_2 = 2 \times A_3 = 2 \times 32 = 64 \]
\[ A_1 = 2 \times A_2 = 2 \times 64 = 128 \]

Now, we have the square footages for all four levels:
- Level 1: 128
- Level 2: 64
- Level 3: 32
- Level 4: 16

Next, we calculate the average square footage of these levels:
\[
\text{Average} = \frac{A_1 + A_2 + A_3 + A_4}{4} = \frac{128 + 64 + 32 + 16}{4}
\]

Summing up the values:
\[
128 + 64 + 32 + 16 = 240
\]

Dividing by 4 to find the average:
\[
\frac{240}{4} = 60
\]

Thus, the average square footage of a level is:
\boxed{60}
