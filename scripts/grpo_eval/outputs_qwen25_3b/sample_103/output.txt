To solve this problem, we first need to determine the total length of the city that needs to be covered by the fog bank. The city is described as being 42 miles across from the oceanfront to the opposite inland edge.

Given:
- The fog bank covers 3 miles in 10 minutes.

We can calculate the time required to cover 42 miles using the proportion:

\[
\text{Time to cover 42 miles} = \left(\frac{42 \text{ miles}}{3 \text{ miles}}\right) \times 10 \text{ minutes}
\]

Let's perform the calculation step-by-step:

1. Calculate the ratio of the total distance to the distance covered in 10 minutes:
   \[
   \frac{42 \text{ miles}}{3 \text{ miles}} = 14
   \]

2. Multiply this ratio by the given time (10 minutes):
   \[
   14 \times 10 \text{ minutes} = 140 \text{ minutes}
   \]

Thus, it will take \(\boxed{140}\) minutes for the fog bank to cover the whole city.
