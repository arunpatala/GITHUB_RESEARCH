To find the average of the guesses, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Determine each friend's guess:**
   - The first friend guesses 80 jelly beans.
   - The second friend guesses 20 more than half of the first friend's guess:
     \[
     \text{Second friend's guess} = \frac{80}{2} + 20 = 40 + 20 = 60
     \]
   - The third friend guesses 25% more than the first friend's guess:
     \[
     \text{Third friend's guess} = 80 + 0.25 \times 80 = 80 + 20 = 100
     \]

2. **Calculate the sum of all guesses:**
   \[
   \text{Sum of guesses} = 80 + 60 + 100 = 240
   \]

3. **Find the average of the guesses by dividing the sum by the number of guesses (which is 3):**
   \[
   \text{Average guess} = \frac{240}{3} = 80
   \]

Thus, the average guess is \(\boxed{80}\).
