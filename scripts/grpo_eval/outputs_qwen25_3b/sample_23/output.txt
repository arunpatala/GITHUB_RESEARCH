To determine how much shorter the candle will be after burning from 1:00 PM to 5:00 PM, we need to calculate the total time the candle burns and then find out how much it melts in that time.

First, let's find the duration of the burning period:
- The burning starts at 1:00 PM.
- The burning ends at 5:00 PM.

The difference between these times is:
\[ 5:00 \text{ PM} - 1:00 \text{ PM} = 4 \text{ hours} \]

Next, we know that the candle melts by 2 centimeters every hour it burns. Therefore, over 4 hours, the total melting would be:
\[ 2 \text{ cm/hour} \times 4 \text{ hours} = 8 \text{ cm} \]

Thus, the candle will be \(\boxed{8}\) centimeters shorter after burning from 1:00 PM to 5:00 PM.
