To solve this problem, we need to determine the salaries of both Adrien and Lylah four years later and then find their combined total.

1. **Determine Lylah's initial salary:**
   - Adrien's initial salary is given as $40000.
   - Adrien's total salary was 30 percent higher than Lylah's.
   - Let \( L \) be Lylah's initial salary.
   - Therefore, Adrien's initial salary can be expressed as:
     \[
     40000 = L + 0.3L = 1.3L
     \]
   - Solving for \( L \):
     \[
     L = \frac{40000}{1.3} = 30769.23 \text{ (approximately)}
     \]

2. **Calculate Adrien's salary four years later:**
   - Adrien's salary increased by 40% from four years ago.
   - His new salary is:
     \[
     40000 \times 1.40 = 56000
     \]

3. **Calculate Lylah's salary four years later:**
   - Lylah's salary also increased by 40% from four years ago.
   - Her new salary is:
     \[
     30769.23 \times 1.40 = 43076.92 \text{ (approximately)}
     \]

4. **Find the total salary the two were receiving four years later:**
   - Summing Adrien's and Lylah's new salaries:
     \[
     56000 + 43076.92 = 99076.92
     \]

Therefore, the total salary the two were receiving four years later is \(\boxed{99076.92}\).
