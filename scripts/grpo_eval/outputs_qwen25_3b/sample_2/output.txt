To determine Josh's profit from the house flipping project, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Initial Cost**: Josh initially buys the house for $80,000.

2. **Repairs Cost**: Josh spends an additional $50,000 on repairs.

3. **Total Initial Investment**:
   \[
   \text{Total Initial Investment} = \text{Cost of House} + \text{Repairs Cost} = 80,000 + 50,000 = 130,000
   \]

4. **Increase in Value**: The house's value increases by 150% due to the repairs. To find the increase in value, we calculate 150% of the original value of the house.
   \[
   \text{Increase in Value} = 150\% \times 80,000 = 1.5 \times 80,000 = 120,000
   \]

5. **New Value of the House**: Adding the increase in value to the original value gives us the new value of the house.
   \[
   \text{New Value} = \text{Original Value} + \text{Increase in Value} = 80,000 + 120,000 = 200,000
   \]

6. **Profit Calculation**: Profit is calculated by subtracting the total initial investment from the new value of the house.
   \[
   \text{Profit} = \text{New Value} - \text{Total Initial Investment} = 200,000 - 130,000 = 70,000
   \]

Thus, Josh made a profit of \(\boxed{70000}\).
