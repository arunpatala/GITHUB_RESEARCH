To determine the total number of hours Kimo spends attending classes in one semester, we need to calculate his weekly class hours and then multiply by the number of weeks in the semester.

First, let's calculate the number of hours Kimo spends on Monday, Wednesday, and Friday:
- Each of these days, he has 3 classes, each lasting 1 hour.
- Therefore, the total number of hours per day is \(3 \times 1 = 3\) hours.
- Since there are 3 such days (Monday, Wednesday, and Friday), the total for these days is \(3 \times 3 = 9\) hours.

Next, let's calculate the number of hours Kimo spends on Tuesday and Thursday:
- Each of these days, he has 2 classes, each lasting 2 hours.
- Therefore, the total number of hours per day is \(2 \times 2 = 4\) hours.
- Since there are 2 such days (Tuesday and Thursday), the total for these days is \(2 \times 4 = 8\) hours.

Now, we add the hours from both groups of days to find the total number of hours Kimo spends on classes in one week:
\[9 + 8 = 17\] hours per week.

Given that there are 16 weeks in the semester, we multiply the weekly total by 16:
\[17 \times 16 = 272\] hours.

Thus, the total number of hours Kimo spends attending classes in one semester is \(\boxed{272}\).
