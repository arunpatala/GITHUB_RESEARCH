To determine the total number of quilt blocks Gene has, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Calculate the total number of vacations Gene has taken:**
   - Gene is currently 34 years old.
   - He started vacationing at age 23.
   - Therefore, the number of years he has been vacationing is:
     \[
     34 - 23 = 11 \text{ years}
     \]

2. **Determine the number of quilt blocks per vacation:**
   - Gene has one shirt (quilt block) from each vacation.
   - Thus, for each vacation, there is one quilt block.

3. **Calculate the total number of quilt blocks:**
   - Since Gene has taken 11 vacations, and each vacation corresponds to one quilt block, the total number of quilt blocks is:
     \[
     11 \text{ quilt blocks}
     \]

Thus, the total number of quilt blocks Gene has is \(\boxed{11}\).
