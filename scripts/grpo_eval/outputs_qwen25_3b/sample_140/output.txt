To determine the percentage of semi-automatic cars among the total cars, we first need to find out how many semi-automatic cars there are.

Given:
- Total cars = 20
- Automatic cars = 12
- Manual cars = 4

The number of semi-automatic cars can be calculated by subtracting the number of automatic and manual cars from the total number of cars:

\[
\text{Semi-automatic cars} = \text{Total cars} - (\text{Automatic cars} + \text{Manual cars})
\]

Substituting the given values:

\[
\text{Semi-automatic cars} = 20 - (12 + 4) = 20 - 16 = 4
\]

Next, we calculate the percentage of semi-automatic cars. The formula for percentage is:

\[
\text{Percentage} = \left( \frac{\text{Number of semi-automatic cars}}{\text{Total number of cars}} \right) \times 100
\]

Substituting the known values:

\[
\text{Percentage} = \left( \frac{4}{20} \right) \times 100 = 0.2 \times 100 = 20\%
\]

Thus, the percentage of semi-automatic cars is:

\boxed{20####}
