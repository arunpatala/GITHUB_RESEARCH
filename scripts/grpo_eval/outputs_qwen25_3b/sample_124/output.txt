To solve this problem, we need to determine the initial number of letters that were already stamped before Jennie started stamping the new ones.

1. **Total Letters Needing Stamps**: Jennie starts with 60 letters needing stamps.
2. **Letters Already Stamped**: After Jennie finishes stamping one-third of the letters needing stamps, there are 30 letters that have been stamped.

Let's denote:
- \( x \) as the number of letters that were already stamped initially.

Since Jennie stamps one-third of the 60 letters needing stamps, she stamps:
\[ \frac{1}{3} \times 60 = 20 \text{ letters} \]

After stamping these 20 letters, the total number of stamped letters becomes:
\[ x + 20 \]

According to the problem, this total is given as 30:
\[ x + 20 = 30 \]

To find \( x \), we solve the equation:
\[ x = 30 - 20 \]
\[ x = 10 \]

Thus, the number of letters that were already stamped when Jennie began is:
\(\boxed{10}\)
