To solve this problem, we need to follow these steps:

1. Calculate the total number of cookies Carl buys.
2. Determine the total cost of all the cookies.
3. Subtract the total cost from the amount Carl pays to find the change.

Let's go through each step in detail:

1. **Calculate the total number of cookies:**
   - Carl buys 10 packs of cookies.
   - Each pack contains 6 cookies.
   \[
   \text{Total number of cookies} = 10 \text{ packs} \times 6 \text{ cookies/pack} = 60 \text{ cookies}
   \]

2. **Determine the total cost of all the cookies:**
   - Each cookie costs $0.10.
   \[
   \text{Total cost} = 60 \text{ cookies} \times \$0.10/\text{cookie} = \$6.00
   \]

3. **Calculate the change Carl receives:**
   - Carl pays with a $10 bill.
   \[
   \text{Change} = \$10.00 - \$6.00 = \$4.00
   \]

Thus, the amount of change Carl receives is:
####4.00
