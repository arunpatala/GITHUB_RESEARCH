To determine Sasha's profit, we need to calculate her total cost and her total revenue from selling the lumber.

First, let's calculate the total cost of the lumber:
- She has ten 2 x 4 x 10 boards, each costing $10.
  \[
  \text{Cost of 2 x 4 x 10 boards} = 10 \times 10 = \$100
  \]
- She also has five 4 x 4 x 10 boards, each costing $16.
  \[
  \text{Cost of 4 x 4 x 10 boards} = 5 \times 16 = \$80
  \]
- Total cost:
  \[
  \text{Total cost} = 100 + 80 = \$180
  \]

Next, let's calculate the total revenue from selling the lumber:
- She sells all ten 2 x 4 x 10 boards at the original price of $10 each.
  \[
  \text{Revenue from 2 x 4 x 10 boards} = 10 \times 10 = \$100
  \]
- She sells all five 4 x 4 x 10 boards at the original price of $16 each.
  \[
  \text{Revenue from 4 x 4 x 10 boards} = 5 \times 16 = \$80
  \]
- Total revenue:
  \[
  \text{Total revenue} = 100 + 80 = \$180
  \]

Finally, to find the profit, subtract the total cost from the total revenue:
\[
\text{Profit} = \text{Total revenue} - \text{Total cost} = 180 - 180 = \$0
\]

Thus, Sasha makes a profit of \(\boxed{0}\).
