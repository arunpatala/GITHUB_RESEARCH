To determine how much Raphael spent in total, we need to calculate the cost of each item he purchased and then sum these costs.

First, let's calculate the cost of the pens:
- Raphael bought 4 pens.
- Each pen costs $1.5.

\[
\text{Total cost for pens} = 4 \times 1.5 = 6
\]

Next, let's calculate the cost of the notebooks:
- Raphael bought 2 notebooks.
- Each notebook costs $4.

\[
\text{Total cost for notebooks} = 2 \times 4 = 8
\]

Then, let's consider the cost of the rim of bond paper:
- The rim of bond paper costs $20.

Now, we add up all the individual costs to find the total amount spent:

\[
\text{Total amount spent} = \text{Cost of pens} + \text{Cost of notebooks} + \text{Cost of bond paper}
\]
\[
\text{Total amount spent} = 6 + 8 + 20 = 34
\]

\boxed{34}
