To determine how much Shiela will pay each month for the five cell phones, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Calculate the total cost of the cell phones before interest:**
   \[
   \text{Total cost} = 5 \times \$150 = \$750
   \]

2. **Calculate the total interest on the cell phones:**
   The interest rate is 2% per unit.
   \[
   \text{Interest per phone} = 0.02 \times \$150 = \$3
   \]
   Since Shiela bought five phones:
   \[
   \text{Total interest} = 5 \times \$3 = \$15
   \]

3. **Calculate the total amount to be paid including interest:**
   \[
   \text{Total amount} = \text{Total cost} + \text{Total interest} = \$750 + \$15 = \$765
   \]

4. **Determine the monthly payment:**
   Shiela will pay this total amount over 3 months.
   \[
   \text{Monthly payment} = \frac{\$765}{3} = \$255
   \]

Thus, Shiela will pay \(\boxed{255}\) each month for 3 months.
