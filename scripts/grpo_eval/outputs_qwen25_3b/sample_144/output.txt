To calculate Kim's weekly profit, we first need to determine his total expenses for the bleach and cloths needed for all his clients.

1. **Number of Clients:**
   - Current clients: 3
   - Potential clients: 5
   - Total clients: \(3 + 5 = 8\)

2. **Cost per Client:**
   - Each client needs 2 bottles of bleach.
   - Each bottle of bleach costs $2.
   - Cost for bleach per client: \(2 \text{ bottles} \times \$2/\text{bottle} = \$4\).

   - Each client also needs 1 pack of cloths.
   - Each pack of cloths costs $5.
   - Cost for cloths per client: \$5.

   - Total cost per client: \( \$4 (\text{bleach}) + \$5 (\text{cloths}) = \$9 \).

3. **Total Expenses:**
   - Total clients: 8
   - Cost per client: \$9
   - Total expenses: \( 8 \text{ clients} \times \$9/\text{client} = \$72 \).

4. **Total Income:**
   - Given: \$92

5. **Profit Calculation:**
   - Profit = Total Income - Total Expenses
   - Profit = \$92 - \$72 = \$20

Therefore, the profit Kim will make each week is ####20.
