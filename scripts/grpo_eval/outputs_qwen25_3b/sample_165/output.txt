To determine how much money Elvira will have left for her clothing, we need to calculate the total cost of the computer accessories she bought and then subtract this amount from her budget.

First, let's list the costs of each accessory:
- Screen, keyboard, and mouse: €1090
- Scanner: €157
- CD burner: €74
- Printer: €102

Next, we sum these costs to find the total expenditure on the accessories:
\[
1090 + 157 + 74 + 102 = 1423
\]

\[
