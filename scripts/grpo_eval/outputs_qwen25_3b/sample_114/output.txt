- \( P_B \) as the capacity of Brittany's phone for storing photographs.
- \( B \) as the number of birds in Jamal's photograph of the ducks.

   \[
   P_J = 1800
   \]

   \[
   P_J = 6 \times P_B

   1800 = 6 \times P_B
   \]
   Solving for \( P_B \):
   \[
   P_B = \frac{1800}{6} = 300
