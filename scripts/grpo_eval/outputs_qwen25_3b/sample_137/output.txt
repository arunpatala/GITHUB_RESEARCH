To determine the total cost of Rory's delivery order, we need to break down the costs step by step and then sum them up.

1. **Calculate the cost of the subs:**
   Rory orders 2 subs at $7.50 each.
   \[
   2 \times 7.50 = 15.00
   \]

2. **Calculate the cost of the chips:**
   Rory orders 2 bags of chips at $1.50 each.
   \[
   2 \times 1.50 = 3.00
   \]

3. **Calculate the cost of the cookies:**
   Rory orders 2 cookies at $1.00 each.
   \[
   2 \times 1.00 = 2.00
   \]

4. **Sum the individual costs:**
   Add the costs of the subs, chips, and cookies.
   \[
   15.00 + 3.00 + 2.00 = 20.00
   \]

5. **Calculate the delivery fee:**
   The delivery fee is 20% of the subtotal.
   \[
   0.20 \times 20.00 = 4.00
   \]

6. **Add the delivery fee to the subtotal:**
   \[
   20.00 + 4.00 = 24.00
   \]

7. **Add the tip:**
   Rory wants to add a $5.00 tip.
   \[
   24.00 + 5.00 = 29.00
   \]

Thus, the total cost of Rory's delivery order is \(\boxed{29.00}\).
