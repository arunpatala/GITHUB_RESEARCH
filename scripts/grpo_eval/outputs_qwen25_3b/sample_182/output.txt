To solve the problem, we need to determine Jean's current age given the information about Mark and Jan's ages.

1. **Determine Mark's Age:**
   - We know that Jan is currently 30 years old.
   - Two years ago, Jan was \(30 - 2 = 28\) years old.
   - According to the problem, two years ago Mark was 5 years older than half of Jan's age at that time.
   - Half of Jan's age two years ago was \(\frac{28}{2} = 14\).
   - Therefore, Mark was \(14 + 5 = 19\) years old two years ago.
   - So, Mark's current age is \(19 + 2 = 21\).

2. **Determine Jean's Age:**
   - The problem states that Jean is two years older than Mark.
   - Since Mark is currently 21 years old, Jean is \(21 + 2 = 23\) years old.

Thus, Jean is \(\boxed{23}\) years old.
