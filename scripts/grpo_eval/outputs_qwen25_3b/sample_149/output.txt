To determine how many Facebook friends James has, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Determine Dorothy's Facebook friends:**
   - We know that Charlie has three times as many Facebook friends as Dorothy.
   - Charlie has 12 Facebook friends.
   - Let \( D \) represent the number of Dorothy's Facebook friends.
   - According to the problem, \( 3D = 12 \).

   Solving for \( D \):
   \[
   D = \frac{12}{3} = 4
   \]
   So, Dorothy has 4 Facebook friends.

2. **Calculate James's Facebook friends:**
   - We are given that James has four times as many Facebook friends as Dorothy.
   - Dorothy has 4 Facebook friends.
   - Let \( J \) represent the number of James's Facebook friends.
   - According to the problem, \( J = 4D \).

   Substituting the value of \( D \):
   \[
   J = 4 \times 4 = 16
   \]

Therefore, James has 16 Facebook friends. The final answer is:
####16
