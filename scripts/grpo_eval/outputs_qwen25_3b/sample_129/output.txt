To determine the hospital's profit from the daily visits, we need to calculate both the revenue generated by the patients and the cost incurred by the hospital, then find the difference.

First, let's calculate the total revenue from the patients:
- The hospital sees 500 people a day.
- Each patient is seen for an average of 24 minutes.
- There are 60 minutes in an hour.

The number of patients seen per hour is:
\[
\frac{500 \text{ patients/day}}{24 \text{ minutes/patient}} = \frac{500}{24} \approx 20.83 \text{ patients/hour}
\]

Next, we calculate the revenue from these patients:
- Doctors charge $150 per hour.
- Patients pay $200 per hour.

Revenue from patients per hour:
\[
20.83 \text{ patients/hour} \times \$200/\text{patient} = \$4166.67/\text{hour}
\]

Now, we calculate the total revenue per day:
\[
\$4166.67/\text{hour} \times 24 \text{ hours/day} = \$100000/\text{day}
\]

Next, let's calculate the cost incurred by the hospital:
- Doctors charge $150 per hour.
- The hospital pays $150 per hour to the doctors.

Cost per hour:
\[
\$150/\text{hour}
\]

Total cost per day:
\[
\$150/\text{hour} \times 24 \text{ hours/day} = \$3600/\text{day}
\]

Finally, we calculate the profit:
\[
\text{Profit} = \text{Total Revenue} - \text{Total Cost} = \$100000 - \$3600 = \$96400
\]

Thus, the hospital's profit from these visits is:
\boxed{96400}
