To determine Sylvie's annual salary after three more years of service, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Initial Salary Calculation:**
   Sylvie's initial monthly salary is $600.

2. **Salary Increase After 5 Years:**
   According to the company's policy, after 5 years of service, each employee receives a 10% increase in their salary.
   
   To calculate the new monthly salary:
   \[
   \text{New Monthly Salary} = \text{Initial Monthly Salary} + (\text{Initial Monthly Salary} \times 0.10)
   \]
   Substituting the initial monthly salary:
   \[
   \text{New Monthly Salary} = 600 + (600 \times 0.10) = 600 + 60 = 660
   \]

3. **Annual Salary After 5 Years:**
   Since there are 12 months in a year, the annual salary after 5 years is:
   \[
   \text{Annual Salary} = \text{New Monthly Salary} \times 12 = 660 \times 12 = 7920
   \]

4. **Salary Increase After Additional 3 Years:**
   Sylvie will continue to receive a 10% increase annually for each subsequent year. We need to calculate her salary after an additional 3 years.

   First, find the new monthly salary after the first additional year:
   \[
   \text{New Monthly Salary} = 660 + (660 \times 0.10) = 660 + 66 = 726
   \]

   Next, find the new monthly salary after the second additional year:
   \[
   \text{New Monthly Salary} = 726 + (726 \times 0.10) = 726 + 72.6 = 798.6
   \]

   Finally, find the new monthly salary after the third additional year:
   \[
   \text{New Monthly Salary} = 798.6 + (798.6 \times 0.10) = 798.6 + 79.86 = 878.46
   \]

5. **Annual Salary After 8 Years:**
   The annual salary after 8 years is:
   \[
   \text{Annual Salary} = 878.46 \times 12 = 10541.52
   \]

Therefore, Sylvie's annual salary after three more years of service is \(\boxed{10541.52}\).
