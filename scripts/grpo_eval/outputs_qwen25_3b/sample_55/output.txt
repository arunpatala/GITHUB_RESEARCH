To determine how many bags Jean can fill, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Calculate the remaining lollipops after Jean eats some:**
   Jean initially has 30 lollipops and eats 2.
   \[
   30 - 2 = 28
   \]
   So, Jean has 28 lollipops left.

2. **Determine how many bags Jean can fill with the remaining lollipops:**
   Jean wants to package 2 lollipops in each bag. To find out how many bags she can fill, we divide the remaining lollipops by 2:
   \[
   \frac{28}{2} = 14
   \]

Thus, Jean can fill \(\boxed{14}\) bags.
