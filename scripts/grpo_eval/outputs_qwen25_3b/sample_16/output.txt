To solve this problem, we need to calculate the total distance traveled by each train over the two days.

First, let's break down the distances traveled by each train:

1. **Traveling Westward:**
   - Each train travels 80 miles westward.
   - Since there are two trains, the total distance traveled westward is:
     \[
     80 \text{ miles} \times 2 = 160 \text{ miles}
     \]

2. **Traveling Northward:**
   - Each train travels 150 miles northward.
   - Since there are two trains, the total distance traveled northward is:
     \[
     150 \text{ miles} \times 2 = 300 \text{ miles}
     \]

Next, we sum up the distances traveled in both directions to find the total distance covered by each train over the two days:

\[
160 \text{ miles (westward)} + 300 \text{ miles (northward)} = 460 \text{ miles}
\]

Thus, the total distance covered by each train in the two days is:

\boxed{460}
