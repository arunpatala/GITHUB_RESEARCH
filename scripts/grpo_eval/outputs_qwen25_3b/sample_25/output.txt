To determine how many boxes of pizza Marie ordered, we need to break down the total cost and isolate the cost of the pizza.

First, let's calculate the cost of the items Marie already knows about:
- The cost of one chicken meal is $12.
- The cost of 5 packs of milk, with each pack costing $3, is \(5 \times 3 = 15\) dollars.
- The cost of 4 apples, with each apple costing $1.50, is \(4 \times 1.50 = 6\) dollars.

Now, let's sum these known costs:
\[
12 + 15 + 6 = 33 \text{ dollars}
\]

Marie paid a total of $50. Therefore, the amount spent on the boxes of pizza is:
\[
50 - 33 = 17 \text{ dollars}
\]

Each box of pizza costs $8.50. To find out how many boxes Marie bought, we divide the total cost of the pizza by the cost per box:
\[
\frac{17}{8.5} = 2
\]

Thus, Marie ordered \(\boxed{2}\) boxes of pizza.
