To determine the final price of Stephen's groceries, we need to account for all the additional charges and fees applied to the original grocery cost.

1. **Original Total Cost**: 
   \[
   \$40.00
   \]

2. **Delivery Fee**:
   \[
   \$3.00
   \]

3. **Tip**:
   \[
   \$4.00
   \]

4. **Total Additional Charges**:
   \[
   \$3.00 + \$4.00 = \$7.00
   \]

5. **Delivery Vendor Fee**:
   The delivery vendor adds a 25% fee to the original total cost. To find this fee, we calculate 25% of $40.00:
   \[
   0.25 \times 40.00 = 10.00
   \]

6. **Combined Additional Fees**:
   Adding the delivery fee and the vendor fee:
   \[
   \$7.00 + \$10.00 = \$17.00
   \]

7. **Final Price Calculation**:
   Adding the combined additional fees to the original total cost:
   \[
   40.00 + 17.00 = 57.00
   \]

Thus, the final price of Stephen's groceries is:
\[
\boxed{57.00}
\]
