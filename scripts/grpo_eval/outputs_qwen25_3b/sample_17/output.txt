To determine Jill's annual salary, we need to calculate her earnings from both teaching and coaching separately, then sum them up.

First, let's calculate her annual earnings from teaching:
- Jill earns $20 per hour.
- She works 35 hours per week as a teacher.
- She works 50 weeks a year as a teacher.

\[
\text{Weekly earnings from teaching} = 35 \text{ hours/week} \times \$20/\text{hour} = \$700/\text{week}
\]

\[
\text{Annual earnings from teaching} = 50 \text{ weeks/year} \times \$700/\text{week} = \$35,000/\text{year}
\]

Next, let's calculate her annual earnings from coaching:
- Jill earns $30 per hour.
- She works 15 hours per week as a coach.
- She works 50 weeks a year as a coach.

\[
\text{Weekly earnings from coaching} = 15 \text{ hours/week} \times \$30/\text{hour} = \$450/\text{week}
\]

\[
\text{Annual earnings from coaching} = 50 \text{ weeks/year} \times \$450/\text{week} = \$22,500/\text{year}
\]

Now, we sum her annual earnings from both jobs:

\[
\text{Total annual salary} = \$35,000 + \$22,500 = \$57,500
\]

Thus, Jill's annual salary is ####57500.
