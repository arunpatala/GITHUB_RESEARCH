To solve this problem, we need to calculate the total time Billy spends on peeling and cutting the potatoes.

First, let's determine the time spent peeling the potatoes:
- Billy takes 1.5 minutes (or 90 seconds) to peel one potato.
- He has 60 potatoes to peel.

\[
\text{Total time to peel} = 60 \text{ potatoes} \times 90 \text{ seconds/potato} = 5400 \text{ seconds}
\]

Next, let's determine the time spent cutting the potatoes:
- Billy takes 5 seconds to cut one potato.
- He has 60 potatoes to cut.

\[
\text{Total time to cut} = 60 \text{ potatoes} \times 5 \text{ seconds/potato} = 300 \text{ seconds}
\]

Now, we add the total time spent peeling and cutting the potatoes:

\[
\text{Total time} = 5400 \text{ seconds} + 300 \text{ seconds} = 5700 \text{ seconds}
\]

Finally, convert the total time from seconds to minutes:

\[
5700 \text{ seconds} \div 60 \text{ seconds/minute} = 95 \text{ minutes}
\]

Thus, the total time it will take Billy to finish prepping the potatoes is:

\boxed{95}
