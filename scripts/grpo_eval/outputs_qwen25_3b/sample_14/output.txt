To determine the percentage of students who enrolled in hip-hop dance, we can follow these steps:

1. Calculate the number of students who enrolled in contemporary dance.
2. Determine the number of students remaining after those who enrolled in contemporary dance are accounted for.
3. Calculate the number of students who enrolled in jazz dance from the remaining students.
4. Find out how many students enrolled in hip-hop dance by subtracting the numbers from steps 1 and 3 from the total number of students.
5. Calculate the percentage of students who enrolled in hip-hop dance.

Let's start with step 1:
- The total number of students is 20.
- 20% of the students enrolled in contemporary dance.
\[ \text{Number of students in contemporary dance} = 20\% \times 20 = 0.20 \times 20 = 4 \]

Next, step 2:
- Subtract the number of students in contemporary dance from the total number of students to find the remaining students.
\[ \text{Remaining students} = 20 - 4 = 16 \]

Then, step 3:
- 25% of the remaining students enrolled in jazz dance.
\[ \text{Number of students in jazz dance} = 25\% \times 16 = 0.25 \times 16 = 4 \]

Finally, step 4:
- Subtract the number of students in contemporary and jazz dance from the total number of students to find the number of students who enrolled in hip-hop dance.
\[ \text{Number of students in hip-hop dance} = 20 - (4 + 4) = 20 - 8 = 12 \]

Step 5:
- Calculate the percentage of students who enrolled in hip-hop dance.
\[ \text{Percentage of students in hip-hop dance} = \left( \frac{12}{20} \right) \times 100 = 0.60 \times 100 = 60\% \]

Thus, the percentage of students who enrolled in hip-hop dance is ####60####.
