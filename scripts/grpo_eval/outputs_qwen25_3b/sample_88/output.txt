To solve this problem, we can set up an equation based on the information given.

Let \( H \) represent the number of copies Harald's record sold.
According to the problem, Marilyn's record sold 10 times as many copies as Harald's, so Marilyn's sales can be represented as \( 10H \).

The total number of copies sold by both records is given as 88,000. Therefore, we can write the equation:
\[
H + 10H = 88,000
\]

Combine like terms:
\[
11H = 88,000
\]

To find \( H \), divide both sides of the equation by 11:
\[
H = \frac{88,000}{11}
\]

Perform the division:
\[
H = 8,000
\]

Thus, Harald sold \(\boxed{8000}\) copies.
