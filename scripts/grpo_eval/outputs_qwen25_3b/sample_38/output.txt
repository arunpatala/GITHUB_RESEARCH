To determine John's running speed, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Calculate the total running time per week:**
   - John runs for 3 hours on the first day.
   - On the second and third days, he runs half as long as the first day.

   Therefore, the running time on the second and third days is:
   \[
   \frac{3}{2} = 1.5 \text{ hours}
   \]

   Adding these times together gives the total weekly running time:
   \[
   3 + 1.5 + 1.5 = 6 \text{ hours}
   \]

2. **Determine the average speed:**
   - John runs a total of 60 miles in 6 hours.

   The formula for speed is:
   \[
   \text{Speed} = \frac{\text{Distance}}{\text{Time}}
   \]

   Substituting the given values:
   \[
   \text{Speed} = \frac{60 \text{ miles}}{6 \text{ hours}} = 10 \text{ miles per hour}
   \]

Thus, John's running speed is:
\boxed{10}
