To solve the problem, we need to determine the number of crabs each person has and then sum them up.

1. **Determine the number of crabs Bo has:**
   \[
   \text{Bo has} = 40 \text{ crabs}
   \]

2. **Determine the number of crabs Monic has:**
   Monic has 4 fewer crabs than Bo.
   \[
   \text{Monic's crabs} = 40 - 4 = 36 \text{ crabs}
   \]

3. **Determine the number of crabs Rani has:**
   Rani has 10 more crabs than Monic.
   \[
   \text{Rani's crabs} = 36 + 10 = 46 \text{ crabs}
   \]

4. **Calculate the total number of crabs:**
   Add the number of crabs each person has:
   \[
   \text{Total crabs} = \text{Bo's crabs} + \text{Monic's crabs} + \text{Rani's crabs}
   \]
   \[
   \text{Total crabs} = 40 + 36 + 46
   \]

5. **Perform the addition:**
   \[
   40 + 36 = 76
   \]
   \[
   76 + 46 = 122
   \]

Thus, the total number of crabs the three have together is:
\[
\boxed{122}
\]
