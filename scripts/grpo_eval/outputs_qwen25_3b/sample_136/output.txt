To determine the number of female Scottish unicorns, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Calculate the total number of unicorns in the Scottish Highlands:**
   - There are 27 unicorns in total.
   - One third of them are in the Scottish Highlands.
   \[
   \text{Number of unicorns in the Scottish Highlands} = \frac{1}{3} \times 27 = 9
   \]

2. **Determine the number of female Scottish unicorns:**
   - Two thirds of the Scottish unicorns are female.
   \[
   \text{Number of female Scottish unicorns} = \frac{2}{3} \times 9 = 6
   \]

Thus, the number of female Scottish unicorns is \(\boxed{6}\).
