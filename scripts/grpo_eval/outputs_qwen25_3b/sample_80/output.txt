To solve this problem, we need to determine how much water two girls and one boy have together and then subtract that from the total amount of water.

First, calculate the amount of water each girl received:
\[
\text{Each girl's share} = \frac{1}{6} \times 24 \text{ liters} = 4 \text{ liters}
\]

Since there are two girls, the total amount of water they received is:
\[
\text{Total for two girls} = 2 \times 4 \text{ liters} = 8 \text{ liters}
\]

Next, add the amount of water the boy received:
\[
\text{Boy's share} = 6 \text{ liters}
\]

Now, sum up the total amount of water received by the two girls and the boy:
\[
\text{Total water received} = 8 \text{ liters} + 6 \text{ liters} = 14 \text{ liters}
\]

Finally, subtract the total amount of water received from the initial total amount of water:
\[
\text{Water left} = 24 \text{ liters} - 14 \text{ liters} = 10 \text{ liters}
\]

Thus, the number of liters of water left is:
####10
