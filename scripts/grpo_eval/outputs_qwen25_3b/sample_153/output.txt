   \[
   x - \frac{2}{3}x = \frac{1}{3}x = 5
   \]
   Solving for \( x \):
   \[
   x = 5 \times 3 = 15
   \]
   So, before the raccoon ate, there were 15 fries.

   \[
   3 \times 3 = 9 \text{ fries}

   \[

   \[
   24 - \frac{14}{2} = 24 - 7 = 17 \text{ fries}
