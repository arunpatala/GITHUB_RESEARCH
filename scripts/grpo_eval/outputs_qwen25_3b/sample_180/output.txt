To determine the number of votes the loser received, we first need to find out how many votes the winner received.

Given:
- The total number of votes cast is 80.
- The winner received \(\frac{3}{4}\) of the votes.

First, calculate the number of votes the winner received:
\[
\text{Votes for the winner} = \frac{3}{4} \times 80
\]

Perform the multiplication:
\[
\frac{3}{4} \times 80 = 3 \times 20 = 60
\]

So, the winner received 60 votes.

Next, we need to find out how many votes the loser received. Since the total number of votes is 80 and the winner received 60 votes, the remaining votes must have gone to the loser:
\[
\text{Votes for the loser} = 80 - 60 = 20
\]

Thus, the number of votes the loser received is:
\boxed{20}
