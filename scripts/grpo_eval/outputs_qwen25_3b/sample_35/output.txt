To determine the total number of points Mike scored in his ping pong game, we need to break down the problem into smaller steps.

1. **Calculate the points scored in the first 20 minutes:**
   - Mike scores 4 points in the first 20 minutes.

2. **Calculate the points scored in the second 20 minutes:**
   - In the second 20 minutes, Mike scores 25% more points than he did in the first 20 minutes.
   - First, find 25% of the points scored in the first 20 minutes:
     \[
     25\% \text{ of } 4 = 0.25 \times 4 = 1
     \]
   - Add this additional point to the original 4 points:
     \[
     4 + 1 = 5
     \]
   - Therefore, Mike scores 5 points in the second 20 minutes.

3. **Calculate the total points scored:**
   - Sum the points from both segments of the game:
     \[
     4 \text{ (first 20 minutes)} + 5 \text{ (second 20 minutes)} = 9
     \]

Thus, the total number of points Mike scored is \(\boxed{9}\).
