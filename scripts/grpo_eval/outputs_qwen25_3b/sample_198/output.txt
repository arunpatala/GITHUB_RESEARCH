To determine the total number of Pokemon cards Elaine has, we need to calculate the number of cards she collected each month and then sum them up.

1. **Initial Number of Cards:**
   Elaine initially had 20 Pokemon cards.

2. **First Month Collection:**
   She collected three times the initial number of cards.
   \[
   3 \times 20 = 60 \text{ cards}
   \]

3. **Second Month Collection:**
   She collected 20 fewer cards than those collected in the first month.
   \[
   60 - 20 = 40 \text{ cards}
   \]

4. **Third Month Collection:**
   She collected twice the combined number of cards from the first and second months.
   First, find the combined number of cards from the first and second months:
   \[
   60 + 40 = 100 \text{ cards}
   \]
   Then, calculate the number of cards collected in the third month:
   \[
   2 \times 100 = 200 \text{ cards}
   \]

5. **Total Number of Cards:**
   Sum the initial number of cards and the cards collected over the three months:
   \[
   20 + 60 + 40 + 200 = 320 \text{ cards}
   \]

Thus, the total number of Pokemon cards Elaine has is \(\boxed{320}\).
