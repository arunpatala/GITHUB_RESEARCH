To determine how many sheets of paper Miguel uses every month, we need to follow these steps:

1. Calculate the total number of sheets of paper used per week.
2. Multiply the weekly usage by the number of weeks in a month.

First, we know that Miguel uses 2 pads of paper a week and each pad contains 30 sheets of paper. Therefore, the total number of sheets of paper used per week is:
\[ 2 \text{ pads/week} \times 30 \text{ sheets/pad} = 60 \text{ sheets/week} \]

Next, we need to find out how many sheets of paper Miguel uses in a month. Assuming a month has approximately 4 weeks, the total number of sheets of paper used per month is:
\[ 60 \text{ sheets/week} \times 4 \text{ weeks/month} = 240 \text{ sheets/month} \]

Thus, the number of sheets of paper Miguel uses every month is:
####240
