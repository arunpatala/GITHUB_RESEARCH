To determine how much money Bailey started with, we need to account for her initial amount and the total amount she received from her weekly allowance over 8 weeks.

First, calculate the total amount of money Bailey received from her weekly allowance:
\[
\text{Weekly allowance} = \$5
\]
\[
\text{Number of weeks} = 8
\]
\[
\text{Total allowance} = 5 \times 8 = \$40
\]

Next, we know that at the end of 8 weeks, Bailey has a total of $100. This total includes her initial amount plus the $40 from her allowance. Let \( x \) represent the initial amount of money Bailey had.

We can set up the following equation to represent this situation:
\[
x + 40 = 100
\]

To find \( x \), solve the equation by subtracting 40 from both sides:
\[
x = 100 - 40
\]
\[
x = 60
\]

Thus, Bailey started with \(\boxed{60}\).
