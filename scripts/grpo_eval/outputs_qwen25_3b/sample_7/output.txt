   \]

2. **Calculate the time taken to download the first 80 GB:**
   \[
   \frac{80 \text{ GB}}{2 \text{ GB/minute}} = 40 \text{ minutes}
   \]

3. **Account for the 20-minute Windows update delay:**
   The download stops for 20 minutes during this period.

4. **Resume the download from the 40% mark:**
   After the update, Carla needs to start downloading the remaining 120 GB (since \(200 \text{ GB} - 80 \text{ GB} = 120 \text{ GB}\)).

5. **Calculate the time to download the remaining 120 GB:**
   \[
   \frac{120 \text{ GB}}{2 \text{ GB/minute}} = 60 \text{ minutes}
   \]

6. **Sum up all the time taken:**
   - Time to download the first 80 GB: 40 minutes
   - Time for the Windows update delay: 20 minutes
   - Time to download the remaining 120 GB: 60 minutes

   Total time:
   \[
   40 \text{ minutes} + 20 \text{ minutes} + 60 \text{ minutes} = 120 \text{ minutes}
   \]

Thus, the total time required to download the file is \(\boxed{120}\).
