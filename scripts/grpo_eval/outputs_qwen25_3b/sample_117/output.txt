To determine how much John paid for the shoes, we can break down the problem into smaller steps:

1. **Number of pairs of shoes per child**: John buys 2 pairs of shoes for each child.
2. **Total number of children**: John has 3 children.
3. **Total number of pairs of shoes**: 
   \[
   2 \text{ pairs/child} \times 3 \text{ children} = 6 \text{ pairs}
   \]
4. **Cost per pair of shoes**: Each pair costs $60.
5. **Total cost**:
   \[
   6 \text{ pairs} \times 60 \text{ dollars/pair} = 360 \text{ dollars}
   \]

Thus, John paid \(\boxed{360}\) dollars.
