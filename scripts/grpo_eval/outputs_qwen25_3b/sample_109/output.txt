To determine the total cost of 10 gallons of gas after applying the cashback reward, we can follow these steps:

1. **Calculate the total cost without any discounts:**
   \[
   \text{Cost per gallon} = \$3.00
   \]
   \[
   \text{Number of gallons} = 10
   \]
   \[
   \text{Total cost without cashback} = 3.00 \times 10 = \$30.00
   \]

2. **Calculate the total cashback amount:**
   \[
   \text{Cashback per gallon} = \$0.20
   \]
   \[
   \text{Number of gallons} = 10
   \]
   \[
   \text{Total cashback} = 0.20 \times 10 = \$2.00
   \]

3. **Subtract the total cashback from the total cost without cashback:**
   \[
   \text{Total cost after cashback} = \$30.00 - \$2.00 = \$28.00
   \]

Thus, the total cost of 10 gallons of gas after the cashback rewards is \(\boxed{2800}\).
