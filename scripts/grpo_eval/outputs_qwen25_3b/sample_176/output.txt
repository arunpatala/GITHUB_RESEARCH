To determine the number of girls on the Chinese team, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Calculate the total number of non-Japanese Asians:**
   - Total number of Asians: 240
   - Number of Japanese Asians: 80
   - Therefore, the number of non-Japanese Asians (which are Chinese) is:
     \[
     240 - 80 = 160
     \]

2. **Determine the number of girls on the Chinese team:**
   - We know that the total number of Chinese Asians is 160.
   - The number of boys on the Chinese team is given as 60.
   - To find the number of girls, subtract the number of boys from the total number of Chinese Asians:
     \[
     160 - 60 = 100
     \]

Thus, the number of girls on the Chinese team is \(\boxed{100}\).
