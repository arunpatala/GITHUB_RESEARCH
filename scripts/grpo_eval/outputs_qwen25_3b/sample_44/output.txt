To determine Charlie's net profit, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Calculate the total cost of making the candles:**
   - Charlie needs 1 pound of beeswax and wicks for each 10 candles.
   - He makes 20 candles, so he needs \( \frac{20}{10} = 2 \) pounds of beeswax.
   - The cost for one pound of beeswax and wicks is $10.00.
   - Therefore, the total cost for 2 pounds is \( 2 \times 10 = 20 \) dollars.

2. **Calculate the revenue from selling the candles:**
   - Charlie sells each candle for $2.00.
   - He makes and sells 20 candles.
   - Therefore, the total revenue is \( 20 \times 2 = 40 \) dollars.

3. **Calculate the net profit:**
   - Net profit is calculated by subtracting the total cost from the total revenue.
   - Total cost = $20.00
   - Total revenue = $40.00
   - Net profit = \( 40 - 20 = 20 \) dollars.

Thus, Charlie's net profit is \(\boxed{20}\).
