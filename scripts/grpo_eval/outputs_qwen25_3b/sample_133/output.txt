To determine how many miles Walt walked on Wednesday, we need to follow these steps:

1. Calculate the number of miles Walt walked on Tuesday.
2. Use the total mileage from Monday through Wednesday to find out how many miles he walked on Wednesday.

First, let's calculate the number of miles Walt walked on Tuesday. According to the problem, Walt walked 6 times as many miles on Tuesday as he did on Monday. Since he walked 4 miles on Monday, we can calculate the miles for Tuesday as follows:
\[
\text{Miles on Tuesday} = 6 \times \text{Miles on Monday} = 6 \times 4 = 24 \text{ miles}
\]

Next, we know the total mileage from Monday through Wednesday is 41 miles. We already know the miles for Monday and Tuesday, so we can find the miles for Wednesday by subtracting the miles for Monday and Tuesday from the total mileage:
\[
\text{Miles on Wednesday} = \text{Total mileage} - (\text{Miles on Monday} + \text{Miles on Tuesday}) = 41 - (4 + 24) = 41 - 28 = 13 \text{ miles}
\]

Thus, the number of miles Walt walked on Wednesday is:
\boxed{13}
