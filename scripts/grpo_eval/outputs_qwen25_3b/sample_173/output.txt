To determine how much Jessica pays in a year if she wants to pay 50% more than the minimum amount for all her debts, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Calculate the total minimum monthly payments:**
   - Student loans: $300/month
   - Credit card: $200/month
   - Mortgage: $500/month

   \[
   \text{Total minimum monthly payments} = 300 + 200 + 500 = 1000 \text{ dollars}
   \]

2. **Determine the amount Jessica wants to pay per month (50% more than the minimum):**
   \[
   \text{Additional amount} = 1000 \times 0.50 = 500 \text{ dollars}
   \]
   \[
   \text{Total monthly payment} = 1000 + 500 = 1500 \text{ dollars}
   \]

3. **Calculate the annual payment by multiplying the monthly payment by 12 months:**
   \[
   \text{Annual payment} = 1500 \times 12 = 18000 \text{ dollars}
   \]

Thus, Jessica pays \(\boxed{18000}\) dollars in a year.
