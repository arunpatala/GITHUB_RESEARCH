To determine how many pages Solo needs to read on average each day over the next 4 days, we first calculate the total number of pages he needs to read.

Pages from each subject:
- Science: 4 pages
- Social Studies: 20 pages
- History: 7 pages
- Geography: 8 pages

Total pages to be read:
\[ 4 + 20 + 7 + 8 = 39 \text{ pages} \]

Solo has already read 15 pages on Monday. Therefore, the remaining pages he needs to read are:
\[ 39 - 15 = 24 \text{ pages} \]

Solo has 4 more days to complete his reading. To find out how many pages he needs to read on average each day, we divide the remaining pages by the number of days left:
\[ \frac{24}{4} = 6 \text{ pages per day} \]

Thus, the number of pages Solo needs to read, on average, in one day is:
####6
