To determine how many pounds of insects a flock of ten ducks needs per day, we start by calculating the total weekly insect consumption for the entire flock and then divide that by the number of days in a week.

First, we know that each duck needs 3.5 pounds of insects per week. For a flock of ten ducks, the total weekly insect consumption is:
\[ 10 \text{ ducks} \times 3.5 \text{ pounds/duck/week} = 35 \text{ pounds/week} \]

\[ \frac{35 \text{ pounds/week}}{7 \text{ days/week}} = 5 \text{ pounds/day} \]
