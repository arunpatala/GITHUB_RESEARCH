To solve this problem, we need to determine how many cars drove through the traffic jam in the first 15 minutes.

Let's denote:
- \( x \) as the number of cars that drove through in the first 15 minutes.
- The total number of cars initially on the motorway is 30.
- 20 more cars drove through in the remaining 15 minutes.
- 5 cars exited the motorway before the traffic jam ended.

The equation representing the situation can be written as follows:

\[ x + 20 - 5 = 30 \]

This equation accounts for the initial number of cars plus the additional cars that drove through minus the cars that exited.

Now, let's solve for \( x \):

\[
x + 20 - 5 = 30
\]

Simplify the left side:

\[
x + 15 = 30
\]

Subtract 15 from both sides to isolate \( x \):

\[
x = 30 - 15
\]

\[
x = 15
\]

Thus, the number of cars that drove through the traffic jam in the first 15 minutes is \(\boxed{15}\).
