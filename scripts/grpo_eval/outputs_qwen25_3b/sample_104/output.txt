To solve this problem, we need to determine how many pieces Poppy and her mom have placed and how many are left to be placed.

First, let's calculate how many pieces Poppy placed:
- The total number of pieces in the puzzle is 1000.
- Poppy places a quarter of the pieces on the board.

\[
\text{Pieces placed by Poppy} = \frac{1}{4} \times 1000 = 250
\]

Next, we find out how many pieces are left after Poppy places hers:
\[
\text{Remaining pieces after Poppy} = 1000 - 250 = 750
\]

Then, her mom places a third of the remaining pieces:
\[
\text{Pieces placed by her mom} = \frac{1}{3} \times 750 = 250
\]

Now, we calculate how many pieces are left after her mom places hers:
\[
\text{Remaining pieces after her mom} = 750 - 250 = 500
\]

Thus, the number of jigsaw pieces left to be placed is:

\boxed{500}
