   - 5 people came with bicycles.

   - Each tricycle has 3 tires.
   - 3 people came with tricycles.
   - Total tires for tricycles = \( 3 \text{ tricycles} \times 3 \text{ tires/tricycle} = 9 \text{ tires} \).

3. **Unicycle:**
   - A unicycle has 1 tire.
   - 1 person came on a unicycle.
   - Total tires for the unicycle = \( 1 \text{ unicycle} \times 1 \text{ tire/unicycle} = 1 \text{ tire} \).

\[
10 \text{ (bicycle tires)} + 9 \text{ (tricycle tires)} + 1 \text{ (unicycle tire)} = 20 \text{ tires}
\]

\[
20 \text{ tires} \times 25 \text{ cents/tire} = 500 \text{ cents}
\]

To convert the total cost from cents to dollars, we divide by 100 (since 1 dollar = 100 cents):
\[
500 \text{ cents} \div 100 = 5 \text{ dollars}
\]
