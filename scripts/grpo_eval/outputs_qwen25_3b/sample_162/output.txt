To solve this problem, we need to calculate the price increase step-by-step over the given period.

1. **Initial Price**: The initial price of a bag of marbles is $20.

2. **Price Increase Calculation**:
   - The price increases by 20% every two months.
   - We need to find the price after 36 months.

3. **Number of Increases**:
   - Since the price increases every two months, in 36 months there will be \( \frac{36}{2} = 18 \) increases.

4. **Price After Each Increase**:
   - Let's denote the price after \( n \) increases as \( P_n \).
   - Initially, \( P_0 = 20 \).

5. **Formula for Price After Each Increase**:
   - After each increase, the new price is calculated as:
     \[
     P_{n+1} = P_n \times (1 + 0.20) = P_n \times 1.20
     \]

6. **General Formula**:
   - To find the price after 18 increases, we use the formula:
     \[
     P_{18} = P_0 \times (1.20)^{18}
     \]
   - Substituting \( P_0 = 20 \):
     \[
     P_{18} = 20 \times (1.20)^{18}
     \]

7. **Calculating \( (1.20)^{18} \)**:
   - Using a calculator or logarithms, we find:
     \[
     (1.20)^{18} \approx 10.488
     \]

8. **Final Price Calculation**:
   - Multiply the initial price by the result:
     \[
     P_{18} = 20 \times 10.488 \approx 209.76
     \]

Therefore, the cost of a bag of marbles after 36 months is approximately $209.76. Putting the final answer in the required format:

\(\boxed{209.76}\)
