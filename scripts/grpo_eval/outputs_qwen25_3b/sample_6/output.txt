To solve the problem, we need to determine the number of sheep in each city based on the given relationships and then sum them up.

1. **Determine the number of sheep in Charleston:**
   - We know that Seattle has 20 sheep.
   - Charleston has 4 times as many sheep as Seattle.
   \[
   \text{Number of sheep in Charleston} = 4 \times \text{Number of sheep in Seattle} = 4 \times 20 = 80
   \]

2. **Determine the number of sheep in Toulouse:**
   - Toulouse has twice as many sheep as Charleston.
   \[
   \text{Number of sheep in Toulouse} = 2 \times \text{Number of sheep in Charleston} = 2 \times 80 = 160
   \]

3. **Sum the number of sheep in all three cities:**
   - Number of sheep in Seattle: 20
   - Number of sheep in Charleston: 80
   - Number of sheep in Toulouse: 160
   \[
   \text{Total number of sheep} = 20 + 80 + 160 = 260
   \]

Thus, the total number of sheep in Toulouse, Charleston, and Seattle is \(\boxed{260}\).
