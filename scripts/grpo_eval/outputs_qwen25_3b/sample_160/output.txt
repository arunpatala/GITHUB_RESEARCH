To determine the water depth in the tank on Wednesday, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Calculate the water depth on Tuesday:**
   - The depth of the water on Monday is given as 17 feet.
   - On Tuesday, the depth increased by 7 feet.
   \[
   \text{Depth on Tuesday} = 17 \text{ feet} + 7 \text{ feet} = 24 \text{ feet}
   \]

2. **Calculate the water depth on Wednesday:**
   - On Wednesday, the depth of the water is two-thirds of what it was on Tuesday.
   \[
   \text{Depth on Wednesday} = \frac{2}{3} \times 24 \text{ feet}
   \]
   - Perform the multiplication:
   \[
   \frac{2}{3} \times 24 = \frac{2 \times 24}{3} = \frac{48}{3} = 16 \text{ feet}
   \]

Thus, the water depth in the tank on Wednesday is \(\boxed{16}\).
