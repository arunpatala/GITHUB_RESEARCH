To determine how many trees Tom has left after 10 years, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Calculate the net increase in trees each year:**
   - Tom plants 10 trees.
   - Tom chops down 2 trees.
   - Net increase per year = \(10 - 2 = 8\) trees.

2. **Calculate the total number of trees after 10 years:**
   - Tom starts with 50 trees.
   - Over 10 years, the net increase is \(8 \text{ trees/year} \times 10 \text{ years} = 80 \text{ trees}\).
   - Total number of trees after 10 years = \(50 + 80 = 130\) trees.

3. **Account for the trees that die:**
   - 30% of the trees die after 10 years.
   - Number of trees that die = \(0.30 \times 130 = 39\) trees.
   - Number of trees left = \(130 - 39 = 91\) trees.

Thus, the number of trees Tom has left after 10 years is \(\boxed{91}\).
