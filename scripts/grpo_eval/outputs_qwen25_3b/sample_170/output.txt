To determine the total cost of each uniform, we need to calculate the cost of each component (the hat, the jacket, and the pants) and then sum these costs.

1. **Cost of the Hat:**
   The cost of the hat is given as \( \$25 \).

2. **Cost of the Jacket:**
   The jacket costs three times as much as the hat.
   \[
   \text{Cost of the jacket} = 3 \times 25 = 75 \text{ dollars}
   \]

3. **Cost of the Pants:**
   The pants cost the average of the costs of the hat and the jacket.
   \[
   \text{Average cost} = \frac{\text{Cost of the hat} + \text{Cost of the jacket}}{2} = \frac{25 + 75}{2} = \frac{100}{2} = 50 \text{ dollars}
   \]

4. **Total Cost of One Uniform:**
   Sum the costs of the hat, jacket, and pants.
   \[
   \text{Total cost} = \text{Cost of the hat} + \text{Cost of the jacket} + \text{Cost of the pants} = 25 + 75 + 50 = 150 \text{ dollars}
   \]

Thus, the total cost for each uniform is \(\boxed{150}\).
