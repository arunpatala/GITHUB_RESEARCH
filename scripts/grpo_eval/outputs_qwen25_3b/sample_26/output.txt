To determine how much Mishka spent in total, we need to calculate the cost for each type of clothing item separately and then sum these amounts.

First, let's calculate the cost of the shorts:
- Mishka bought 3 pairs of shorts.
- Each pair of shorts costs $16.50.

\[
\text{Total cost of shorts} = 3 \times 16.50 = 49.50
\]

Next, let's calculate the cost of the pants:
- Mishka bought 3 pairs of pants.
- Each pair of pants costs $22.50.

\[
\text{Total cost of pants} = 3 \times 22.50 = 67.50
\]

Then, let's calculate the cost of the shoes:
- Mishka bought 3 pairs of shoes.
- Each pair of shoes costs $42.

\[
\text{Total cost of shoes} = 3 \times 42 = 126
\]

Now, we sum the total costs of all the clothing items:

\[
\text{Total cost} = \text{Total cost of shorts} + \text{Total cost of pants} + \text{Total cost of shoes}
\]
\[
\text{Total cost} = 49.50 + 67.50 + 126 = 243
\]

Thus, Mishka spent \(\boxed{243}\) dollars on all the clothing items.
