To determine how many spoons were in the package that Julia bought, we can follow these steps:

1. **Identify the final number of spoons:**
   Julia ended up with a total of 12 spoons.

2. **Account for the spoons given by her husband:**
   Her husband gave her 5 new spoons.

3. **Determine the number of spoons used:**
   Julia used 3 spoons while making the stew.

4. **Calculate the initial number of spoons Julia had before her husband's gift:**
   Let \( x \) be the number of spoons Julia originally bought.
   After receiving 5 spoons from her husband, the total number of spoons Julia had is:
   \[
   x + 5
   \]

5. **Set up the equation based on the final count:**
   After using 3 spoons, the remaining number of spoons is:
   \[
   (x + 5) - 3 = 12
   \]

6. **Solve the equation:**
   Simplify the equation:
   \[
   x + 5 - 3 = 12
   \]
   \[
   x + 2 = 12
   \]
   Subtract 2 from both sides:
   \[
   x = 10
   \]

Thus, the number of spoons in the package that Julia bought is \(\boxed{10}\).
