To determine how much Mandy should give to Benedict after 3 months with a monthly interest rate of 2%, we need to calculate the total amount including interest.

First, let's find the interest for one month:
\[
\text{Monthly Interest} = 100 \times 0.02 = 2 \text{ dollars}
\]

Next, we calculate the total interest for 3 months:
\[
\text{Total Interest for 3 Months} = 2 \times 3 = 6 \text{ dollars}
\]

Now, we add the total interest to the original amount owed to find the total payment:
\[
\text{Total Payment} = 100 + 6 = 106 \text{ dollars}
\]

Thus, Mandy should give Benedict \(\boxed{106}\).
