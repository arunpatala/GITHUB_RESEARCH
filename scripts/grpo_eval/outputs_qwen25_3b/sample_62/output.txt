2. **Pension Calculation After 20 Years:**

   - Additional pension amount starting from year 21 = 5% of $50,000 = 0.05 * $50,000 = $2,500/year.

3. **Total Pension After 20 Years:**
   - Total pension in year 21 = Initial pension + Additional pension = $50,000 + $2,500 = $52,500/year.

4. **Pension Calculation After 30 Years:**
   - From year 21 onwards, the additional pension is 5% of the total pension amount.
   - Let \( P \) be the total pension amount in year 30.
   - The additional pension in year 30 = 5% of \( P \).

Since Marcy quits after 30 years, we need to find the total pension amount \( P \) in year 30.

5. **Setting Up the Equation:**
   - In year 30, the total pension \( P \) includes both the initial pension and the additional pension:
     \[
     P = 50,000 + 0.05P
     \]

6. **Solving for \( P \):**
   \[
   P - 0.05P = 50,000
   \]
   \[
   0.95P = 50,000
   \]
   \[
   P = \frac{50,000}{0.95} \approx 52,631.58
   \]

7. **Annual Pension in Year 30:**

     \[
     \text{Annual pension in year 30} = 52,631.58
