The dance class has 20 students. 

1. **Contemporary Dance**: 20% of 20 students = $0.20 \times 20 = 4$ students.  
2. **Remaining Students**: $20 - 4 = 16$.  
3. **Jazz Dance**: 25% of 16 students = $0.25 \times 16 = 4$ students.  
4. **Hip-Hop Dance**: Remaining students = $20 - 4 - 4 = 12$.  

To find the percentage of students in hip-hop:  
$$
\frac{12}{20} \times 100 = 60\%.
$$

#### Answer: 60
