Poppy starts with 1000 puzzle pieces. She places a quarter of them on the board, which is $ \frac{1}{4} \times 1000 = 250 $ pieces. This leaves $ 1000 - 250 = 750 $ pieces remaining. 

Her mom then places a third of the remaining pieces, which is $ \frac{1}{3} \times 750 = 250 $ pieces. Subtracting this from the remaining pieces gives $ 750 - 250 = 500 $.

Thus, the number of pieces left to be placed is $\boxed{500}$.
