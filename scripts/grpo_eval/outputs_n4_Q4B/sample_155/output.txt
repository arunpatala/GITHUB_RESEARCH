To determine the total length of all the baked goods when placed end to end, we first convert all measurements to the same unit—feet.

---

### Step 1: Convert each item's length to feet

- **Rolls**: Each roll is 4 inches long.  
  $$
  4 \text{ inches} = \frac{4}{12} = \frac{1}{3} \text{ feet}
  $$

- **Croissants**: Each croissant is 6 inches long.  
  $$
  6 \text{ inches} = \frac{6}{12} = \frac{1}{2} \text{ feet}
  $$

- **Baguettes**: Each baguette is 2 feet long (already in feet).

---

### Step 2: Calculate total length for each type

- **Rolls**:  
  $$
  300 \text{ rolls} \times \frac{1}{3} \text{ feet} = 100 \text{ feet}
  $$

- **Croissants**:  
  $$
  120 \text{ croissants} \times \frac{1}{2} \text{ feet} = 60 \text{ feet}
  $$

- **Baguettes**:  
  $$
  60 \text{ baguettes} \times 2 \text{ feet} = 120 \text{ feet}
  $$

---

### Step 3: Add all the lengths together

$$
100 \text{ feet} + 60 \text{ feet} + 120 \text{ feet} = 280 \text{ feet}
$$
