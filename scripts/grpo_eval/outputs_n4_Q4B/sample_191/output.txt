To determine how many pounds of insects a flock of ten ducks needs per day, we start by analyzing the weekly requirement for each duck. 

Each duck requires **3.5 pounds of insects per week**. For a flock of **10 ducks**, the total weekly insect requirement is:

$$
10 \text{ ducks} \times 3.5 \text{ pounds/duck/week} = 35 \text{ pounds/week}
$$

Next, we convert this weekly total into a daily amount by dividing by the number of days in a week (7):

$$
\frac{35 \text{ pounds/week}}{7 \text{ days/week}} = 5 \text{ pounds/day}
$$

Thus, the total amount of insects needed per day for the flock is:

$$
\boxed{5}
$$
