   - Total articles on Tuesday: $ 5 + 2 = 7 $.  
   - Total hours for Tuesday: $ 7 \times 4 = 28 \, \text{hours} $.

3. **Wednesday**:  
   - She wrote twice the number of articles as Tuesday.  
   - Total articles on Wednesday: $ 2 \times 7 = 14 $.  
   - Total hours for Wednesday: $ 14 \times 4 = 56 \, \text{hours} $.

4. **Total hours**:  
   $$
   20 + 28 + 56 = 104 \, \text{hours}
   $$

Thus, the total number of hours Meredith spent writing articles over the three days is:

#### $ \boxed{104} $
