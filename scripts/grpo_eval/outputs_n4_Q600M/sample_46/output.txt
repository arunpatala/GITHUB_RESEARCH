We can express the total post-it notes as follows:

$$
\text{Total post-it notes} = \text{Initial} + P - \text{Given out}
$$

Where:
- Initial = 80
- Given out = 220

Therefore:

$$
80 + P - 220 = 23
$$

Solve for $ P $:

$$
P - 140 = 23 \\
P = 163
$$
