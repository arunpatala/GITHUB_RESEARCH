To determine how much Lloyd earned over the first two weeks, we can calculate his earnings by breaking it down into individual weeks and then summing these amounts.

First, let's calculate his earnings for the first week:

- Lloyd earns $10 per hour.
- He tutored for 5 hours in the first week.

\[
\text{Earnings for the first week} = \text{Hourly wage} \times \text{Hours tutored} = 10 \, \text{dollars/hour} \times 5 \, \text{hours} = 50 \, \text{dollars}
\]

Next, let's calculate his earnings for the second week:

- Similarly, he earns $10 per hour.
- He tutored for 8 hours in the second week.

\[
\text{Earnings for the second week} = \text{Hourly wage} \times \text{Hours tutored} = 10 \, \text{dollars/hour} \times 8 \, \text{hours} = 80 \, \text{dollars}
\]

Now, sum the earnings of both weeks to find the total:

\[
\text{Total earnings} = \text{Earnings for the first week} + \text{Earnings for the second week} = 50 \, \text{dollars} + 80 \, \text{dollars} = 130 \, \text{dollars}
\]

Thus, Lloyd earned a total of **130** dollars during the first two weeks.
