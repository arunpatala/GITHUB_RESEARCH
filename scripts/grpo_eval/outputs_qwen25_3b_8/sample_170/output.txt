To solve this problem, we first calculate the cost of each item: the hat, the jacket, and the pants.

1. The cost of the hat is $25.
2. The jacket costs three times as much as the hat. Therefore,
   \[
   \text{Cost of the jacket} = 3 \times 25 = 75.
   \]

3. The pants cost the average of the costs of the hat and the jacket. So, we have:
   \[
   \text{Cost of the pants} = \frac{\text{Cost of the hat} + \text{Cost of the jacket}}{2} = \frac{25 + 75}{2} = \frac{100}{2} = 50.
   \]

Now, we sum the costs of the items to find the total cost of one uniform:

\[
\text{Total cost of one uniform} = \text{cost of the hat} + \text{cost of the jacket} + \text{cost of the pants} = 25 + 75 + 50.
\]

Adding these together:
\[
25 + 75 + 50 = 150.
\]

Thus, the total cost for each uniform is $150.

The final answer is:
\[
\boxed{150}
