   \[

   \]
   Given that the value increased by 150%:
   \[
   \text{Value increase percentage} = 150\% = 1.50
   \]
   Therefore,
   \[
   \text{Increase in value} = 1.50 \times 80,000 = 120,000
   \]

2. **Calculate the new total value of the house:**
   The increase in value must be added to the original value to find the new total value:
   \[
   \text{New total value} = \text{Original value} + \text{Increase in value}
   \]
   Substituting the values:
   \[
   \text{New total value} = 80,000 + 120,000 = 200,000
   \]

3. **Calculate the profit:**

   \[
   \text{Total renovation cost} = 80,000 + 50,000 = 130,000
   \]
   To calculate his profit:
   \[
   \text{Profit} = \text{New total value} - \text{Total renovation cost}
   \]
   Substituting the values:
   \[

   \]
