To determine the total number of sheep in Toulouse, Charleston, and Seattle, we need to calculate the number of sheep each city has based on the given information.

1. We know that Seattle has 20 sheep.
   \[
   \text{Sheep in Seattle} = 20
   \]

2. Charleston has 4 times as many sheep as Seattle. Let \( S_c \) represent the number of sheep Charleston has.
   \[
   S_c = 4 \times \text{Sheep in Seattle} = 4 \times 20 = 80
   \]
   \[
   \text{Sheep in Charleston} = 80
   \]

3. Toulouse has twice as many sheep as Charleston. Let \( S_t \) represent the number of sheep Toulouse has.
   \[
   S_t = 2 \times \text{Sheep in Charleston} = 2 \times 80 = 160
   \]
   \[
   \text{Sheep in Toulouse} = 160
   \]

Next, we sum up the total number of sheep:
\[
\text{Total Sheep} = \text{Sheep in Seattle} + \text{Sheep in Charleston} + \text{Sheep in Toulouse}
\]
\[
\text{Total Sheep} = 20 + 80 + 160 = 260
\]

Therefore, the total number of sheep is 260. The final answer is:
####260
