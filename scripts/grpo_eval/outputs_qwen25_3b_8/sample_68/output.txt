To solve this problem, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Determine the number of boys at the school:**
   - According to the problem, there are twice as many boys as girls.
   - Given that there are 60 girls, the number of boys can be calculated as:
     \[
     \text{Number of boys} = 2 \times \text{Number of girls} = 2 \times 60 = 120
     \]

2. **Calculate the total number of students:**
   - The total number of students at the school is the sum of the number of boys and girls:
     \[
     \text{Total students} = \text{Number of boys} + \text{Number of girls} = 120 + 60 = 180
     \]

3. **Determine the number of teachers:**
   - There are 5 students for every teacher. To find out how many teachers are there, divide the total number of students by the number of students per teacher:
     \[
     \text{Number of teachers} = \frac{\text{Total students}}{\text{Students per teacher}} = \frac{180}{5} = 36
     \]

Putting our final answer in the required format:
####36
