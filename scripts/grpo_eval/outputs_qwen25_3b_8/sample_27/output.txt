To determine how much Cynthia will spend on ice cream over 60 days, we can follow these steps:

1. **Calculate the total number of servings Cynthia needs for 60 days**:
   - Since Cynthia eats 1 serving per night:
     \[
     \text{Total servings needed} = 1 \text{ serving/day} \times 60 \text{ days} = 60 \text{ servings}
     \]

2. **Determine the number of cartons Cynthia needs**:
   - Each carton contains 15 servings.
   \[
   \text{Number of cartons needed} = \frac{60 \text{ servings}}{15 \text{ servings/carton}} = 4 \text{ cartons}
   \]

3. **Calculate the total cost for 4 cartons**:
   - The price per carton is $4.00.
   \[
   \text{Total cost} = 4 \text{ cartons} \times 4 \text{ dollars/carton} = 16 \text{ dollars}
   \]

Therefore, Cynthia will spend \(\boxed{16}\) dollars on ice cream over 60 days.
