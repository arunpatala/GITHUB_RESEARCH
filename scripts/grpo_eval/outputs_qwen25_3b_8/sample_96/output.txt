Solution: To determine how many more hours Harry slept than James, we will first find out how much time James slept.

Given:
- Harry slept for 9 hours.
- James slept \( \frac{2}{3} \) of what Harry slept.

First, calculate \( \frac{2}{3} \) of Harry's sleep hours:

\[
\text{James' Sleep} = 9 \times \frac{2}{3} = 6 \text{ hours}
\]

Next, find the difference between the number of hours Harry slept and the number of hours James slept:

\[
\text{Difference} = 9 \text{ hours} - 6 \text{ hours} = 3 \text{ hours}
\]

Therefore, Harry slept ####3#### hours more than James.
