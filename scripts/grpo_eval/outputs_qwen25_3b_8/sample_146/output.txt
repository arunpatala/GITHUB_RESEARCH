To determine the total number of Lego pieces Johnny picks up, we need to calculate the number of pieces from each of the three lego boxes and then sum them up.

1. The first lego box has:
   \[
   500 \text{ pieces}
   \]

2. The second lego box has 3 times the number of pieces of the first box:
   \[
   3 \times 500 = 1500 \text{ pieces}
   \]

3. The third lego box has \(\frac{1}{4}\) the number of pieces as the first box:
   \[
   \frac{1}{4} \times 500 = 125 \text{ pieces}
   \]

Next, we add the pieces from all three boxes to find the total number of Lego pieces Johnny picks up:
\[
500 + 1500 + 125 = 2125 \text{ pieces}
\]

Therefore, the total number of Lego pieces Johnny picks up is:
\boxed{2125}
