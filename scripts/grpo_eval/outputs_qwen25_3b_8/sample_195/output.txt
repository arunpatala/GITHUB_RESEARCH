To determine the total cost of 8 pens, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Identify individual costs:**
   - Cost of a pencil: \( \$1.20 \)
   - Cost of an eraser: \( \$0.30 \)

2. **Calculate combined cost of a pen:**
   Given that a pen costs as much as a pencil and an eraser combined:
   \[
   \text{Cost of a pen} = \text{Cost of a pencil} + \text{Cost of an eraser}
   \]
   Substituting the given values:
   \[
   \text{Cost of a pen} = \$1.20 + \$0.30 = \$1.50
   \]

3. **Calculate the total cost for 8 pens:**
   \[
   \text{Total cost of 8 pens} = 8 \times \text{Cost of one pen}
   \]
   Substituting the cost per pen:
   \[
   \text{Total cost of 8 pens} = 8 \times \$1.50 = \$12.00
   \]

Thus, the total cost of 8 pens is \(\boxed{1200}\).
