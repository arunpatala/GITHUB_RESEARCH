To determine how many pieces of apple pie were taken by the guests, follow these steps:

1. Calculate the total number of pieces of pie initially:
   - Grandma Jones cut each of the 5 pies into 8 pieces.
   \[
   \text{Total pieces} = 5 \times 8 = 40
   \]

2. Determine the number of remaining pieces at the end of the event:
   - By the end of the evening, there were 14 pieces remaining.

3. Subtract the remaining pieces from the total pieces to find the pieces taken by the guests:
   \[
   \text{Pieces taken} = \text{Total pieces} - \text{Remaining pieces}
   \]
   \[
   \text{Pieces taken} = 40 - 14 = 26
   \]

Thus, the number of pieces of pie taken by the guests is:
\(\boxed{26}\)
