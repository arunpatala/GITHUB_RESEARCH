To find the combined weight of Grace and Alex, we will follow these steps:

1. Determine Alex's weight based on Grace's weight.
2. Add Grace's weight and Alex's weight together to find their combined weight.

Let's begin with step 1: calculating Alex's weight.
Given:
- Grace weighs 125 pounds.
- Alex weighs 2 pounds less than 4 times what Grace weighs.

First, calculate 4 times Grace's weight:
\[
4 \times 125 = 500
\]
Then subtract 2 pounds from this result:
\[
500 - 2 = 498
\]
Thus, Alex weighs 498 pounds.

Now let's proceed with step 2: adding Grace's and Alex's weights together.
Adding Grace's weight (125 pounds) and Alex's weight (498 pounds):
\[
125 + 498 = 623
\]

Therefore, the combined weight of Grace and Alex is \(####623\).
