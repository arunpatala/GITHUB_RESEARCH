To determine Jill's annual salary, we need to calculate her earnings from both teaching and coaching, and then sum them up.

### Step 1: Calculate hourly earnings for teaching
- Hourly rate for teaching: $20
- Weekly working hours as a teacher: 35 hours
- Weeks worked in a year: 50

Hourly earnings for teaching each week:
\[ \text{Earnings/week (teaching)} = 35 \text{ hours/week} \times \$20/\text{hour} = \$700 \text{/week} \]

Total earnings for teaching in a year:
\[ \text{Earnings/year (teaching)} = 700 \text{ dollars/week} \times 50 \text{ weeks/year} = 35000 \text{ dollars/year} \]

### Step 2: Calculate hourly earnings for coaching
- Hourly rate for coaching: $30
- Weekly working hours as a coach: 15 hours
- Weeks worked in a year: 50

Hourly earnings for coaching each week:
\[ \text{Earnings/week (coaching)} = 15 \text{ hours/week} \times \$30/\text{hour} = \$450 \text{/week} \]

Total earnings for coaching in a year:
\[ \text{Earnings/year (coaching)} = 450 \text{ dollars/week} \times 50 \text{ weeks/year} = 22500 \text{ dollars/year} \]

### Step 3: Add teaching and coaching earnings to find total annual salary
\[ \text{Total annual salary} = 35000 \text{ dollars} + 22500 \text{ dollars} = 57500 \text{ dollars} \]

- Weekly hours spent teaching: 35

\[ \text{Weekly Teaching Income} = \text{Hourly Wage} \times \text{Hours per Week} \]
\[ \text{Weekly Teaching Income} = \$20 \times 35 = \$700 \]

Yearly income from teaching:
\[ \text{Yearly Teaching Income} = \text{Weekly Teaching Income} \times \text{Number of Weeks} \]
