To determine the total number of fruits brought by Morisette and Kael, we need to follow these steps:

1. Calculate the number of apples and oranges Kael brought.
2. Sum up all the fruits brought by both individuals.

### Calculation:

- **Apple count**:
  - Morisette brought 5 apples.
  - Kael brought twice the number of apples Morisette brought: \(2 \times 5 = 10\) apples.
  
Thus, the total number of apples they brought is:
\[ 5 \text{ (from Morisette)} + 10 \text{ (from Kael)} = 15 \text{ apples} \]

- **Orange count**:
  - Morisette brought 8 oranges.
  - Kael brought half the number of oranges Morisette brought: \( \frac{8}{2} = 4 \) oranges.
  
Thus, the total number of oranges they brought is:
\[ 8 \text{ (from Morisette)} + 4 \text{ (from Kael)} = 12 \text{ oranges} \]

- **Total number of fruits**:
Combining the apples and oranges, we get:
\[ 15 \text{ apples} + 12 \text{ oranges} = 27 \text{ total fruits} \]

Putting the final answer in the required format:
####27
