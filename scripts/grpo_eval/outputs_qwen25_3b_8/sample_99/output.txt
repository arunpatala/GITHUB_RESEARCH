To determine how many potted plants Mary will have left after giving some away, we can follow these steps:

1. Calculate the initial total number of potted plants Mary currently has.
2. Determine the number of potted plants Mary will give away.
3. Subtract the number given away from the initial total.

### Step 1: Initial Total Potted Plants
Mary's house has 40 window ledges on which she already has:
- **Potted plants per ledge**: 2  
- **Number of ledges**: 40

So, the total number of potted plants she already has initially is calculated as:
\[
40 \text{ ledges} \times 2 \text{ plants per ledge} = 80 \text{ plants}
\]

### Step 2: Potted Plants to Give Away
Mary plans to give 1 potted plant from each of the 40 window ledges:
\[
40 \text{ ledges} \times 1 \text{ plant per ledge} = 40 \text{ plants}
\]

### Step 3: Remaining Potted Plants
Subtracting the number of plants given away from the initial total gives us the remaining plants:
\[
80 \text{ initial plants} - 40 \text{ plants given away} = 40 \text{ plants}
\]

The final number of potted plants that Mary will remain with is:
\boxed{4000}
