To determine how many puzzle pieces are left to be placed, we start with the total number of pieces in the puzzle, which is 1000.

First, Poppy places a quarter of the pieces on the board:
\[
\text{Fraction of pieces Poppy places} = \frac{1}{4}
\]
\[
\text{Number of pieces Poppy places} = \frac{1}{4} \times 1000 = 250
\]

Next, we need to calculate the number of pieces remaining after Poppy places her part:
\[
\text{Remaining pieces after Poppy} = 1000 - 250 = 750
\]

Poppy's mom then places a third of the remaining pieces:
\[
\text{Fraction of pieces her mom places} = \frac{1}{3}
\]
\[
\text{Number of pieces her mom places} = \frac{1}{3} \times 750 = 250
\]

We now calculate the number of pieces left after her mom places her part:
\[
\text{Remaining pieces after her mom} = 750 - 250 = 500
\]

Thus, the number of jigsaw pieces that are left to be placed is:
\boxed{500}
