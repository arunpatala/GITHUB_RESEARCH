To determine the original price of last year's best-selling book, given that Kyle paid $19.50 after a 25% discount, we can break down the problem into straightforward mathematical steps.

Let's denote the original price of the book by \( P \).

Since a 25% discount means Kyle paid only 75% of the original price (because 100% - 25% = 75%), we can write:
\[ 0.75P = $19.50 \]

To find the original price \( P \), we divide both sides of the equation by 0.75:
\[ P = \frac{19.50}{0.75} \]
\[ P = 26 \]

Therefore, the original price of the book was \(\textbf{26}\).
