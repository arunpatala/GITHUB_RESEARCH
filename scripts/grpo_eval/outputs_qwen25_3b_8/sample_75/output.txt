To find the total square footage of the sandcastles and then the average square footage per level:

1. Given:
   - Top (4th) level: \(16 \, \text{sq ft}\)
   - Each level below has exactly half the square footage as the level below it.

2. Calculate the square footage for each level working upwards from the bottom:
   - Bottom (1st) level:
     Since the 4th level is half of the 3rd level, we solve:
     \[
     \text{Level 4 square footage} / 2 = \text{Level 3 square footage}
     \]
     \[
     16 / 2 = 8 \, \text{sq ft}
     \]
     Hence, the Level 3 square footage is \(8 \, \text{sq ft}\).

   - Middle (2nd) level:
     Solving:
     \[
     \text{Level 3 square footage} / 2 = \text{Level 2 square footage}
     \]
     \[
     8 / 2 = 4 \, \text{sq ft}
     \]
     Therefore, the Level 2 square footage is \(4 \, \text{sq ft}\).

3. Finally, the Square footage for the top level (Level 1):
     \[
     \text{Level 2 square footage} / 2 = \text{Level 1 square footage}
     \]
     \[
     4 / 2 = 2 \, \text{sq ft}
     \]

4. Adding all levels' square footage together:
   \[
   \text{Total square footage} = 16 + 8 + 4 + 2
   \]
   \[
   = 30 \, \text{sq ft}
   \]

5. Counting the total levels:
   There are 4 levels.

6. Calculating the average square footage for each level:
   \[
   \text{Average square footage} = \frac{\text{Total square footage}}{\text{Number of levels}}
   \]
   \[
   \frac{30}{4} = 7.5 \, \text{sq ft each level}
   \]

So, the final answer is 7.5.
