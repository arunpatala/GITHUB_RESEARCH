To determine the total amount of potato salad Ted the T-Rex needs to bring to the dinosaur picnic, we need to calculate the potato salad requirements for both the adults and the children separately and then sum these amounts.

First, let's calculate the potato salad needed for the adults:
- Each adult dinosaur eats 10 pounds of potato salad.
- There are 20 adults at the picnic.

\[
\text{Total potato salad for adults} = 20 \text{ adults} \times 10 \text{ pounds/adult} = 200 \text{ pounds}
\]

Next, we calculate the potato salad required for the children:
- Each child dinosaur eats half as much as an adult, which means each child eats:
\[
10 \text{ pounds}/\text{adult} \div 2 = 5 \text{ pounds}/\text{child}
\]
- There are 5 children at the picnic.

\[
\text{Total potato salad for children} = 5 \text{ children} \times 5 \text{ pounds/child} = 25 \text{ pounds}
\]

Finally, we add the amounts of potato salad needed for both the adults and the children to find the total amount:

\[
\text{Total potato salad} = 200 \text{ pounds} + 25 \text{ pounds} = 225 \text{ pounds}
\]

Thus, Ted needs to bring \(225\) pounds of potato salad to the picnic.
