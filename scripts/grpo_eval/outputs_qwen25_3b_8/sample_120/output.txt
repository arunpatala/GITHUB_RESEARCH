To solve this problem, we need to determine the total number of sheets of paper Miguel uses in a month based on his weekly usage.

First, let's calculate the total number of sheets used each week:
- Miguel uses 2 pads of paper a week.
- Each pad contains 30 sheets of paper.

So, the total number of sheets used per week is:
\[
2 \text{ pads/week} \times 30 \text{ sheets/pad} = 60 \text{ sheets/week}
\]

\[
60 \text{ sheets/week} \times 4 \text{ weeks/month} = 240 \text{ sheets/month}
\]
