To determine the number of pink gumballs in the candy machine, we are given the following information:

- There are 22 more than four times the number of blue gumballs.
- There are 12 blue gumballs.

Let's denote:
- \( P \) as the number of pink gumballs,
- \( B \) as the number of blue gumballs.

Given that:
\[ P = 4B + 22 \]
and
\[ B = 12 \]

First, substitute \( B = 12 \) into the equation for \( P \):
\[
P = 4 (12) + 22
\]

Now perform the multiplication and addition:
\[
P = 48 + 22 
\]
\[
P = 70
\]

Therefore, the number of pink gumballs is: ####70

To determine the number of pink gumballs in the candy machine, we need to follow these steps:

1. Start with the given information:
   - There are 12 blue gumballs.
   - The number of pink gumballs (\(P\)) is 22 more than four times the number of blue gumballs.

2. Express the relationship between the number of pink and blue gumballs mathematically:
   \[
   P = 4 \times B + 22
   \]
   where \( B \) represents the number of blue gumballs.

3. Substitute the given number of blue gumballs into the equation:
   \[
   B = 12
   \]
   \[
   P = 4 \times 12 + 22
   \]

4. Calculate four times 12:
   \[
   4 \times 12 = 48
   \]

5. Add 22 to 48 to find the number of pink gumballs:
   \[
   P = 48 + 22 = 70
   \]

Thus, the number of pink gumballs is \(70\). Putting the final answer in the required format, we get:
