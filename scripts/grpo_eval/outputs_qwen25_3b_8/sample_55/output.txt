To solve this math problem, first we need to figure out how many lollipops Jean has left after eating 2.

The total number of lollipops Jean has initially is 30. Then she eats 2, so the calculation to find the remainder is:
$$ 30 - 2 = 28 $$

Jean now has 28 lollipops left. To find out how many full bags of 2 lollipops each she can make, we divide the remaining number of lollipops by 2:
$$ \frac{28}{2} = 14 $$

Therefore, Jean can fill 14 bags. The final answer is ####14.
