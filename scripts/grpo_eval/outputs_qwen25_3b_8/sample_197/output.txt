To determine the number of children in the city, we need to subtract the number of men and women from the total population.

The total population of Soda is given as 23786 individuals.
The number of men is 8417.
The number of women is 9092.

First, let's add the number of men and women:
\[ 8417 + 9092 = 17509 \]

Next, subtract the sum of the number of men and women from the total population to find the number of children:
\[ 23786 - 17509 = 6277 \]

Thus, the number of kids in the city is:
####6277
