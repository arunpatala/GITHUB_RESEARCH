To determine how many times Peter can go to the movies given his budget of $42 and the cost structure of a movie ticket and popcorn at $7 each, we can follow these steps:

1. **Calculate the total amount spent on one visit:**
   \[
   \text{Cost per visit} = \text{Cost of a ticket} + \text{Cost of popcorn}
   \]
   Given:
   \[
   \text{Cost of a ticket} = \$7 \quad \text{and} \quad \text{Cost of popcorn} = \$7
   \]
   Therefore:
   \[
   \text{Cost per visit} = \$7 + \$7 = \$14
   \]

2. **Determine how many visits Peter can afford:**
   \[
   \text{Number of trips} = \frac{\text{Total money Peter has}}{\text{Cost per visit}}
   \]
   Substituting the values:
   \[
   \text{Number of trips} = \frac{42}{14} = 3
   \]

So Peter can go to the movies \boxed{3} times.
