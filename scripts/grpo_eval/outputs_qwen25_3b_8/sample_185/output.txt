To find the total number of apples picked by Jenna and her mother, we can break the problem down step by step.

1. Determine the number of apples picked by Jenna:
   - We know that if Jenna's mother picked 20 apples and Jenna picked half as many apples as her mother, then Jenna picked half of 20 apples.
   \[
   \text{Number of apples Jenna picked} = \frac{20}{2} = 10
   \]

2. Calculate the total number of apples picked by both Jenna and her mother:
   - Add the number of apples picked by Jenna to the number of apples picked by her mother.
   \[
   \text{Total number of apples} = 20 + 10 = 30
   \]

Thus, the total number of apples picked by both Jenna and her mother is \(\boxed{30}\).
