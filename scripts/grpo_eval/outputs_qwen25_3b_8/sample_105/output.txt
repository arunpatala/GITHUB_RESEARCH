To determine how many cookies both Cody and Amir eat together, we start by establishing the given information:

1. Amir eats 5 cookies.
2. Cody eats three times as many cookies as Amir.

First, calculate the number of cookies Cody eats:
\[
\text{Cookies eaten by Cody} = 3 \times \text{(Cookies eaten by Amir)}
\]
\[
\text{Cookies eaten by Cody} = 3 \times 5
\]
\[
\text{Cookies eaten by Cody} = 15
\]

Next, add the number of cookies eaten by both Cody and Amir to find the total number of cookies they eat together:
\[
\text{Total cookies} = \text{Cookies eaten by Cody} + \text{Cookies eaten by Amir}
\]
\[
\text{Total cookies} = 15 + 5
\]
\[
\text{Total cookies} = 20
\]

Thus, the total number of cookies eaten by both Cody and Amir together is:
