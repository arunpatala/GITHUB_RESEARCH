To determine how many dozens of eggs Claire will consume in 4 weeks, let's break down the problem step by step.

Firstly, we need to find out how many eggs Claire eats each day. According to the given information:
\[ \text{Eggs per omelet} = 3 \]

Next, figure out how many omelets Claire makes each day.
\[ \text{Omelets per day} = 1 \]

Then, add the total number of eggs she consumes per day:
\[ \text{Eggs per day} = \text{Eggs per omelet} \times \text{Omelets per day} = 3 \times 1 = 3 \text{ eggs/day} \]

Now, determine how many days are in 4 weeks. Each week has 7 days.
\[ 4 \text{ weeks} \times 7 \text{ days/week} = 28 \text{ days} \]

Next, calculate how many eggs are consumed over these 28 days:
\[ \text{Total eggs in 28 days} = 28 \text{ days} \times 3 \text{ eggs/day} = 84 \text{ eggs} \]

Lastly, convert that into dozens. There are 12 eggs in 1 dozen.
\[ \text{Dozens of eggs} = \frac{84 \text{ eggs}}{12 \text{ eggs/dozen}} = \frac{84}{12} = 7 \]

Thus, Claire eats \(\boxed{7}\) dozens of eggs in 4 weeks.
