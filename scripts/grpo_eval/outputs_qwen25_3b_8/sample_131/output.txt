To find out the total expenditure of Joseph for May and June, we first need to determine how much he spent in June.

Given:
- Expenditure in May = $500
- Expenditure in June = Expenditure in May - $60

June expenditure: 
$500 - $60 = $440

Now, we can calculate the total expenditure:
Total expenditure = Expenditure in May + Expenditure in June
Total expenditure = $500 + $440 = $940

Therefore, the total expenditure for those two months is ####940.
