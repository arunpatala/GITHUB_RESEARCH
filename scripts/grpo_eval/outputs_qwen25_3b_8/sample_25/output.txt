\[ \text{Cost of the chicken meal} = \$12 \]

Next, we calculate the cost of the 5 packs of milk:
\[ \text{Cost of 5 packs of milk} = 5 \times \$3 = \$15 \]

Then we calculate the cost of the 4 apples:
\[ \text{Cost of 4 apples} = 4 \times \$1.50 = \$6 \]

Let's denote the number of boxes of pizza Marie bought by \( x \). Each box costs \( \$8.50 \), so the total cost for the boxes of pizza is:
\[ \text{Cost of } x \text{ boxes of pizza} = 8.50x \]

The total amount Marie spent is given as \( \$50 \).

To find out \( x \), we set up the equation:
\[ 12 + 15 + 6 + 8.50x = 50 \]

Simplify this:
\[ 33 + 8.50x = 50 \]

Now, isolate \( x \):
\[ 8.50x = 50 - 33 \]
\[ 8.50x = 17 \]

Divide both sides by 8.50 to solve for \( x \):
