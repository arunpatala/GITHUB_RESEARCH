To determine how many pounds of carrots will not be used, we start by calculating the total amount of carrots that all 40 restaurants will receive.

Each restaurant receives:
\[ \text{Carrots per restaurant} = 2 \text{ pounds} \]

There are:
\[ \text{Number of restaurants} = 40 \]

So, the total amount of carrots distributed to the restaurants is:
\[ \text{Total distributed} = \text{Carrots per restaurant} \times \text{Number of restaurants} \]
\[ \text{Total distributed} = 2 \times 40 \]
\[ \text{Total distributed} = 80 \text{ pounds} \]

Next, we need to know the original amount of carrots, which is:
\[ \text{Total pounds of carrots} = 200 \text{ pounds} \]

We now subtract the total amount distributed from the original amount:
\[ \text{Carrots not used} = \text{Total pounds of carrots} - \text{Total distributed}
\]
\[ \text{Carrots not used} = 200 \text{ pounds} - 80 \text{ pounds}
\]
\[ \text{Carrots not used} = 120 \text{ pounds} \]

Therefore, the final answer is:
