To determine the total number of gems in the treasure chest, we'll calculate the number of each type of gem step by step.

First, we know that:
- The number of diamonds is \( 175 \).

Next, we are informed that there are \( 35 \) fewer rubies than diamonds. Therefore, we can calculate the number of rubies as follows:
\[ \text{Number of rubies} = 175 - 35 \]
\[ \text{Number of rubies} = 140 \]

Further information states that there are \( 2 \) times the number of emeralds compared to the rubies. Let's find the number of emeralds:
\[ \text{Number of emeralds} = 2 \times \text{Number of rubies} \]
\[ \text{Number of emeralds} = 2 \times 140 \]
\[ \text{Number of emeralds} = 280 \]

Now we have all the counts for individual types of gems:
- Diamonds: \( 175 \)
- Rubies: \( 140 \)
- Emeralds: \( 280 \)

Finally, we add these quantities together to get the total number of gems:
\[ \text{Total gems} = 175 + 140 + 280 \]
\[ \text{Total gems} = 695 \]

Thus, the total number of gems in the treasure chest is \(\boxed{695}\).
