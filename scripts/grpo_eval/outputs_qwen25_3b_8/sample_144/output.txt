- Each session requires:
  - 2 bottles of bleach at $2 each: \( 2 \times 2 = \$4 \)
  - 1 package of cloths at $5 each: \( 5 \)

Thus, per cleaning session total cost:
\[ 4 + 5 = \$9 \]

Next, we consider all of Kim’s planned clients:
- Currently serving 3 clients.
- Additionally talking to 5 potential clients, which he plans to serve as well.

Total number of clients:
\[ 3 + 5 = 8 \]

Therefore, the total weekly expense (at $9 per cleaning) for 8 sessions is:
\[ 8 \times 9 = \$72 \]

Kim’s weekly income is provided as:
\[ \$92 \]

Finally, the profit is obtained by subtracting the total expense from the total income:
