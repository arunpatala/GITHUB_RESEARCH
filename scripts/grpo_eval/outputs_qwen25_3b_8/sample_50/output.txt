To determine how much Lloyd makes from selling the eggs per week, let's outline the steps:

1. **Calculate the daily earnings:**
   - Each day, the farm produces 252 eggs.
   - Each dozen of eggs (which equals 12 eggs) is sold for $2.
   - To find how many dozens are sold each day:
     \[
     \frac{252 \text{ eggs}}{12 \text{ eggs per dozen}} = 21 \text{ dozens}
     \]
   - The daily earning from the sale of these eggs:
     \[
     21 \text{ dozens} \times 2 \text{ dollars per dozen} = 42 \text{ dollars}
     \]

2. **Calculate the weekly earning:**
   - There are 7 days in a week.
   - Weekly earnings are then:
     \[
     42 \text{ dollars per day} \times 7 \text{ days} = 294 \text{ dollars}
     \]

Therefore, Lloyd makes $\boxed{294}$ dollars on eggs per week.
