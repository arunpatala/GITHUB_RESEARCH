To determine how many stickers Charlie has left, we will follow these steps:

1. **Initial Stickers:**
   Charlie starts with 10 stickers.

2. **Stickers Bought:**
   Charlie buys 21 more stickers.
   \[
   10 + 21 = 31
   \]

3. **Stickers Received for Birthday:**
   For his birthday, he receives 23 more stickers.
   \[
   31 + 23 = 54
   \]

4. **Stickers Given Away:**
   Charlie gives 9 stickers to his sister.
   \[
   54 - 9 = 45
   \]

5. **Stickers Used:**
   Charlie uses 28 stickers to decorate a greeting card.
   \[
   45 - 28 = 17
   \]

Thus, Charlie has \(\boxed{17}\) stickers left.
