To determine how many guests will attend Christina's party, we first need to calculate the number of guests based on the given ratio:

\[
\text{Number of guests who will show up} = \text{Number of invited guests} - \frac{1}{4} \times \text{Number of invited guests}
\]

Christina invited 16 friends:

\[
\frac{1}{4} \times 16 = 4 \text{ guests will not show up}
\]
\[
\text{Number of guests who will show up} = 16 - 4 = 12 \text{ guests}
\]

Next, we calculate how many gift bags are needed if each of the attending guests receives .75 gift bag:

\[
\text{Total gift bags needed} = 12 \times 0.75 = 9
\]

The cost of one gift bag is \$2. Therefore,

\[
\text{Total cost} = 9 \times 2 = 18
\]

Christina will spend \(\boxed{18}\) dollars.
