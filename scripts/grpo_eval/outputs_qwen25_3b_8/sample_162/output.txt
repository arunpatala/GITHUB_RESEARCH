To find out how much a bag of marbles will cost after 36 months, given that its original price is $20 and it increases by 20% every two months, we need to perform the following steps:

Firstly, calculate the increase in price per period (every two months):
\[ \text{Price Increase} = \frac{20\%}{2} = 10\% \]

Next, determine how many such price increase periods fit into 36 months:
\[ \text{Number of Periods} = \frac{36\ \text{months}}{2\ \text{months/period}} = 18\ \text{periods} \]

Now, calculate the total factor by which the price will be multiplied after these 18 periods:
\[ \text{Total Increase Factor} = (1 + 10\%)^{18} = (1.10)^{18} \]

Use this factor to find the final price of the bag of marbles after 36 months. Note:
\[ 1.10^{18} \approx 9.528 \]

Thus, the new price will be:
\[ \text{New Price} = 20 \times 9.528 = 190.56 \]

Finally, round the result to four decimal points as required:
\[ \boxed{190.5600} \]
