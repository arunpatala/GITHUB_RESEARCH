Zaid spends **$ \frac{1}{4} $** of his salary on rent:
$$
\frac{1}{4} \times 6000 = 1500
$$

---

### 2. Car Fuel
He spends **$ \frac{1}{3} $** of his salary on car fuel:
$$
\frac{1}{3} \times 6000 = 2000
$$

---

### 3. Remaining After Rent and Car Fuel
$$
6000 - 1500 - 2000 = 2500
$$

---

### 4. Charity Donation
Zaid donates **half** of the remaining amount to his favorite charity:
$$
\frac{1}{2} \times 2500 = 1250
$$

---

### 5. Remaining After Donation
$$
2500 - 1250 = 1250
$$

---

### 6. Personal Allocations
- Gives **$200** to his daughter
- Gives **$700** to his wife

Total given to family:
$$
200 + 700 = 900
$$

---

### 7. Final Remaining Amount
$$
1250 - 900 = 350
$$

---

### Final Answer:
$$
\boxed{350}
$$
