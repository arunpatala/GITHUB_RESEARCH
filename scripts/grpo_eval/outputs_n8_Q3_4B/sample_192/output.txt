Tom starts with 50 trees. Each year, he plants 10 trees and chops down 2 trees, resulting in a net gain of 8 trees per year. After 10 years, the total number of trees before considering the 30% that die is calculated as:

$$
50 + (8 \times 10) = 130
$$

After 10 years, 30% of the trees die. Calculating 30% of 130:

$$
0.3 \times 130 = 39
$$

Subtracting the dead trees from the total:

$$
130 - 39 = 91
$$

Thus, the number of trees left after 10 years is:

$$
\boxed{91}
$$
